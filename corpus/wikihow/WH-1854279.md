1. [step: WH-1854279-01] Open GIMP. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1854279-02] Click File in the menu bar at the top. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1854279-03] Click Open in the drop-down menu. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1854279-04] Select an image and click Open. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1854279-05] Click the crop tool in the toolbar to the left. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1854279-06] Drag a sqaure around the area you want to crop. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1854279-07] Double-click inside the selected area of the image. requires: 06
