1. [step: WH-5996528-01] Measure the screen diagonally from corner to corner to determine the manufacturer's advertised screen size. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5996528-02] Measure the width and height of the screen to determine the aspect ratio. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5996528-03] Measure the width and height of the TV with the frame to determine if it will fit on your shelf or TV stand. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5996528-04] Multiply the diagonal screen size by 1.2 to determine the proper viewing distance from the TV. requires: 03
