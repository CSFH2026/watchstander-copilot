2. [step: WH-10069001-02] Click Windows or Mac OSX under ".ARF File.". requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10069001-03] Launch the setup file, and finish the install. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10069001-04] Open the NR Player app on your computer. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10069001-05] Click the File tab, and select Open. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10069001-06] Select your ARF file, and click Open. requires: 05
