1. [step: WH-12626411-01] Download Catan Universe from The Google Play Store, App Store, or Steam. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12626411-02] Open Catan Universe. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12626411-03] Click or tap Register and fill out the form. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12626411-04] Retrieve the verification code from your email and verify your account in Catan Universe. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12626411-05] Tap Multiplayer. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12626411-06] Tap a game expansion (all expansion except "The Game" must be purchased). requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12626411-07] Tap Free Match or Auto Match. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12626411-08] Tap a position on the board to play build a settlement, road, or city. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-12626411-09] Tap dice icon to roll the dice. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-12626411-10] Tap the icon that resembles a stack of cards with a plus sign (+) to purchase a Development card. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-12626411-11] Tap the icon that resembles a bag to trade with other players. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-12626411-12] Tap the icon that resembles a circle with an arrow to end your turn. requires: 11
