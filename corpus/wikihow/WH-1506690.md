1. [step: WH-1506690-01] Open your project in iMovie. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1506690-02] Tap T (iOS) or click the Titles tab (Mac). requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1506690-03] Select a style of title display you want to add to your iMovie project. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1506690-04] Double-click (Mac) or tap on (iOS) the "Title Text Here" to edit text. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1506690-05] Preview your work. requires: 04
