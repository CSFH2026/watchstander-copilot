1. [step: WH-4879249-01] Verify that all your local data has been saved to Google Drive or to another form of storage. requires: none
2. [step: WH-4879249-02] Press and hold down the Esc + Refresh keys, then press the Power button. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-4879249-03] Press Enter to confirm that you want to go into Developer Mode. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-4879249-04] Download crouton from GitHub and verify that the file saves to your Downloads folder. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-4879249-05] Press Ctrl + Alt + T. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-4879249-06] Type "shell" into the terminal and press Enter. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-4879249-07] Type “sudo sh ~/Downloads/crouton -t xfce” into the terminal, then press Enter. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-4879249-08] Wait for crouton to fully install on your Chromebook and enter a new username and password. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-4879249-09] Type “sudo startxfce4” into the terminal to launch Linux on your Chromebook. requires: 08
