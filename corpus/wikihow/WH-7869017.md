1. [step: WH-7869017-01] Download the Minecraft Forge install file from http://files.minecraftforge.net/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-7869017-02] Double-click the Minecraft Forge install file after you download it. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7869017-03] Ensure "Install Client" is selected and click Next. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7869017-04] Search for Minecraft mods on Google. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7869017-05] Go to a Minecraft mod website and click a mod. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7869017-06] Click the download link for the mod. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-7869017-07] Cut or copy the mod .jar or .zip folder in the Downloads folder. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-7869017-08] Click Go in the menu bar at the top. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-7869017-09] Hold the Options key and click Library. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-7869017-10] Click the Application Support folder. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-7869017-11] Open the Minecraft folder and then open or create a new folder called "mods" with a lower-case "m". requires: 10
12. [step: WH-7869017-12] Paste the mod file in the "mods" folder. requires: 11
13. [step: WH-7869017-13] Launch Minecraft. requires: 12
14. [step: WH-7869017-14] Select the "Forge" profile and click Play. requires: 13
