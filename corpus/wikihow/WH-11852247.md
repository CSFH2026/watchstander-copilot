1. [step: WH-11852247-01] Launch InShot on your phone or tablet. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11852247-02] Tap Video and select a video to add. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11852247-03] Tap Music in the menu in the middle. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11852247-04] Tap on the video timeline at the bottom to edit existing sound. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11852247-05] Tap Tracks or Effects to add extra sounds. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11852247-06] Tap the checkmark, then Save to save the video. requires: 05
