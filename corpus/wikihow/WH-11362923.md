1. [step: WH-11362923-01] Load a Sims 4 game and select a household to modify. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11362923-02] Enter Build Mode. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11362923-03] Click an object you want to move. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11362923-04] Hold "Alt" to freely move objects anywhere. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11362923-05] Switch to Sims 3 camera mode. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11362923-06] Hold Alt and click and drag an object to rotate it to any angle. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11362923-07] Click and object and press "[" or "]" to change the size of an object. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11362923-08] Press "Ctrl+Shift+C" to open the Command Console. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11362923-09] Type "bb.moveobjects on" and press "Enter" to turn on Move Objects mode. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-11362923-10] Use Move Objects mode to place objects anywhere, including on other objects. 11 Press "9" or "0" to raise objects off the ground in Move Objects mode. requires: 09
