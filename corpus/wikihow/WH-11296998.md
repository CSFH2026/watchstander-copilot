1. [step: WH-11296998-01] Open TikTok. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11296998-02] Tap the red and white camera icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11296998-03] Tap Effects and tap the effect you want. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11296998-04] Tap the top half of the screen to close the Effects panel. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11296998-05] Tap the red button to start recording. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11296998-06] Tap the red button again to stop recording. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11296998-07] Tap the red checkmark to finish recording. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11296998-08] Tap Next and Post to finish and post your video. requires: 07
