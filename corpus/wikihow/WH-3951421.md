1. [step: WH-3951421-01] Install Blender 3D from https://www.blender.org/download/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3951421-02] Download Minecraft Blender files at https://nilssoderman.com/downloads/minecraft-blender-rig/. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3951421-03] Open Blender 3D. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3951421-04] Click File followed by Append. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3951421-05] Select a Minecraft Blender file. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-3951421-06] Select all objects in the "Object" folder and click Append. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-3951421-07] Select the armature frame of a Minecraft character rig and switch to Pose Mode. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-3951421-08] Click a part of the character rig and move it. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-3951421-09] Place the timeline playhead at Frame 1 and click the Record button. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-3951421-10] Select an object you want to animate and place it to record the first keyframe. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-3951421-11] Move the playhead forward in the timeline and place the object where you want it to move t to record another keyframe. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-3951421-12] Continue for all keyframes in the animation. requires: 11
13. [step: WH-3951421-13] Repeat for all objects in the animation. requires: 12
14. [step: WH-3951421-14] Click Render. requires: 13
15. [step: WH-3951421-15] Click Render Animation. requires: 14
