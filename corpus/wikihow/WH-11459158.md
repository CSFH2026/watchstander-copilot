1. [step: WH-11459158-01] Close your current Spotify account by going to https://support.spotify.com/us/contact-spotify-support/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11459158-02] Click Account and I want to close my account permanently. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11459158-03] Follow the on-screen prompts to close your account. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11459158-04] Verify closing the account in your email. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11459158-05] Create a new account at https://www.spotify.com/us/signup/. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11459158-06] Fill in the form to sign up. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11459158-07] Click Sign Up. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11459158-08] Verify the new account in your email. requires: 07
