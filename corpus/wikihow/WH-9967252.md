1. [step: WH-9967252-01] Make sure your Android supports RCS. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9967252-02] Open Android Messages. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9967252-03] Tap the menu and select Settings. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9967252-04] Tap Chat features. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9967252-05] Toggle "Enable chat features" to On. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9967252-06] Toggle "Send read receipts" to On. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9967252-07] Text another Android Messages or Samsung Messages user while connected to Wi-Fi or data. requires: 06
