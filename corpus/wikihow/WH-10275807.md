1. [step: WH-10275807-01] Click the search or Cortana on the bottom-left. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10275807-02] Search and open Event Viewer. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10275807-03] Expand Windows Logs on the sidebar. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10275807-04] Click Application. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10275807-05] Click the Source tab. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10275807-06] Find and double-click an IIS event to view the log. requires: 05
