1. [step: WH-400803-01] Make sure your keyboard has the ten-key numeric keypad. requires: none
2. [step: WH-400803-02] Open the Ease of Access Center. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-400803-03] Click Make the keyboard easier to use. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-400803-04] Enable Mouse Keys and increase the pointer speed. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-400803-05] Use the numeric keypad to move the pointer. requires: 04
