1. [step: WH-5820478-01] Purchase a Battery replacement kit. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5820478-02] Remove the screws on both sides of the charging port (if present). requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5820478-03] Use the plastic pry tool to separate the back cover from the back. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5820478-04] Remove the tape from the battery and ribbon cable connectors. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5820478-05] Pop the tab on the front of the ribbon connectors and slide the ribbon cables out of the connectors. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-5820478-06] Unscrew the screws securing the battery in place. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-5820478-07] Detach the wires attached from the battery to the mainboard. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-5820478-08] Lift the battery to remove it. requires: 07
