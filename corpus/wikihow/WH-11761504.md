2. [step: WH-11761504-02] Play the song or video. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11761504-03] Press the Volume Up button on the side of your iPhone. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11761504-04] Make sure the volume bar says Volume instead of Ringer to adjust media volume. requires: 03
