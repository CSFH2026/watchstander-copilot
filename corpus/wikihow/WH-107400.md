1. [step: WH-107400-01] Enable NumLk (Number Lock) on the keyboard. requires: none
2. [step: WH-107400-02] Hold the Alt key. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-107400-03] Enter the alt code number (e.g., 0128). requires: 02
4. [step: WH-107400-04] Lift your finger from the Alt key. requires: 03
