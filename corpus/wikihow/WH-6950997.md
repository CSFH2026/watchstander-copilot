1. [step: WH-6950997-01] Search for stone blocks alongside mountains, cliffsides, underground, or in caves. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6950997-02] Look for coal blocks, which look like stone blocks with black spots on them. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6950997-03] Craft a pickaxe equip a pickaxe. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6950997-04] Stand close to a coal block and place the reticle in the center of the screen on it. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6950997-05] Attack the coal block with the pickaxe equipped until the coal block breaks apart. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-6950997-06] Walk over the black coal icons to collect the coal. requires: 05
