1. [step: WH-7081875-01] Download and install Steam Link on your computer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-7081875-02] Open Steam Link and tap Get Started. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7081875-03] Tap Other Computer in Steam Link. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7081875-04] Open Steam on your computer and go to Steam > Settings > Remote Play > Pair Steam Link. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7081875-05] Enter the 4-digit PIN from Steam Link in Steam. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7081875-06] Tap your computer. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-7081875-07] Tap the Steam icon in the center at the top of Steam Link. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-7081875-08] Select Library. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-7081875-09] Select a game. requires: 08
