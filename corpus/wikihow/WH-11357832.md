1. [step: WH-11357832-01] Open the Utilities folder in Finder. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11357832-02] Open the Keychain Access utility. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11357832-03] Click Passwords. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11357832-04] Search for the login information you want to find. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11357832-05] Double-click the login information you want to access. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11357832-06] Click to select the box next to "Show Password.". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11357832-07] Enter your password to see the login password. requires: 06
