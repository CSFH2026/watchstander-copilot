1. [step: WH-4556224-01] Check to make sure your phone supports SD card storage. requires: none
2. [step: WH-4556224-02] Power your phone off and remove the SIM card tray. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-4556224-03] Place the SD card in the proper slot in the SIM card tray and reinsert it. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-4556224-04] Power your phone back on. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-4556224-05] Tap Mount if prompted. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-4556224-06] Open the Settings menu if the SD card does not mount automatically. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-4556224-07] Locate the Storage menu in the Settings menu. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-4556224-08] Tap SD card. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-4556224-09] Tap Mount. requires: 08
