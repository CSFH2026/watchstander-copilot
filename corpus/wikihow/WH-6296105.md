1. [step: WH-6296105-01] Go to http://appleid.apple.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6296105-02] Enter your current ID and password. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6296105-03] Click the half-circle to the right of "Password.". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6296105-04] Click Change Password. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6296105-05] Enter your current password. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-6296105-06] Enter and confirm your new password. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-6296105-07] Click Change Password. requires: 06
