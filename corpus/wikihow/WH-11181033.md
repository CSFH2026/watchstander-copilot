1. [step: WH-11181033-01] Open Google Chrome. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11181033-02] Type "chrome://flags" in the address bar. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11181033-03] Select "Enable" next to "Webgl 2.0 Compute". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11181033-04] Click Relaunch Now. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11181033-05] Open Firefox. 6, Type "about:config" in the address bar and accept the risk. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11181033-06] Search "webgl.disabled" and double-click it if it says "True" under "Value". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11181033-07] Open Safari on Mac. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11181033-08] Open Preferences under the "Safari" menu. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11181033-09] Check ""Show Develop menu in menu bar" in the Advanced tab. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-11181033-10] Click Develop in the menu bar. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-11181033-11] Hover over "Experimental Features" and check "WebGL 2.0". requires: 10
