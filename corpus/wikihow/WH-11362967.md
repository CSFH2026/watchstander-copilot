1. [step: WH-11362967-01] Load a saved game of the Sims 4. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11362967-02] Press "Ctrl+Shift+C" to bring up the Console on the map screen. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11362967-03] Type "cas.fulleditmode" and press "Enter". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11362967-04] Click the Manage Households icon in the upper-right corner. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11362967-05] Click the household with the Sim you want to edit. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11362967-06] Click the Pencil Icon. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11362967-07] Click the Sim you want to edit. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11362967-08] Use the options in Create-a-Sim to edit your Sim. requires: 07
