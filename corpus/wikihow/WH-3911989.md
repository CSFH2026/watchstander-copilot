1. [step: WH-3911989-01] Open both images. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3911989-02] Use the lasso tool to outline the facial features on one photo. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3911989-03] Copy the selection. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3911989-04] Unlock the background layer of the second picture. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3911989-05] Paste the selection onto the second picture. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-3911989-06] Lower the opacity. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-3911989-07] Transform the selection to match the face. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-3911989-08] Blend both layers. requires: 07
