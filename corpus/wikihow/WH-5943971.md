1. [step: WH-5943971-01] Call 1-888-4-ONSTAR to cancel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5943971-02] Open the trunk. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5943971-03] Remove the trunk liner and spare tire. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5943971-04] Remove the sill plate and net. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5943971-05] Remove the right side panel. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-5943971-06] Remove the three cables from the OnStar module. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-5943971-07] Reassemble the vehicle. requires: 06
