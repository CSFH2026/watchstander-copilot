1. [step: WH-11644396-01] Open your image in Photoshop. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11644396-02] Click the Image tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11644396-03] Click Canvas Size. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11644396-04] Change the canvas size numbers. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11644396-05] Click the icon that looks like a half grey and half white circle. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11644396-06] Click Solid Color. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11644396-07] Set the color. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11644396-08] Drag and drop the frame layer to the back. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11644396-09] Save your image. requires: 08
