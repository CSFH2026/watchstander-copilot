1. [step: WH-11420219-01] Connect the Fitbit to a power source. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11420219-02] Press and hold button while removing cable. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11420219-03] Continue holding button for 7 to 9 more seconds. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11420219-04] Press and hold the button until you see "ALT.". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11420219-05] Press and hold the button until the Fitbit vibrates. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11420219-06] Press and hold the button until you see "ERROR.". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11420219-07] Press and hold the button until you see "ERASE.". requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11420219-08] Turn the Fitbit back on and set up as new. requires: 07
