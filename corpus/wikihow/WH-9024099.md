1. [step: WH-9024099-01] Open the Discord app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9024099-02] Tap the three horizontal lines icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9024099-03] Tap a server. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9024099-04] Tap Create Instant Invite. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9024099-05] Tap the invite link to copy it to clipboard. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9024099-06] Tap the Share icon next to the link. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9024099-07] Select an app to share your invite. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9024099-08] Select a friend. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-9024099-09] Send the invite link. requires: 08
