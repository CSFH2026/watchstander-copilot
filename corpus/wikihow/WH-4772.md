1. [step: WH-4772-01] Install Apache, PHP, and MySQL. requires: none
2. [step: WH-4772-02] Download phpMyAdmin from https://www.phpmyadmin.net. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-4772-03] Unzip the file. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-4772-04] Copy the folder. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-4772-05] Paste the folder into your htdocs folder. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-4772-06] Rename the folder "phpmyadmin.". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-4772-07] Rename "php.ini-production" to "php.ini" and open it. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-4772-08] Remove the semicolon to the left of "extension=php_mbstring.dll" and save. requires: 07
