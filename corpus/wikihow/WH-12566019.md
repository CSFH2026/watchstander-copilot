1. [step: WH-12566019-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12566019-02] Select and copy your data. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12566019-03] Paste the copied data to a Notepad or TextEdit file. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12566019-04] Use the "Find and Replace" tool to get rid of the spaces. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12566019-05] Add "_MULTIPLE _POINT" to the top of the list. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12566019-06] Save the file as an SCR file. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12566019-07] Open AutoCAD. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12566019-08] Type "SCR" and press Enter/Return. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-12566019-09] Navigate to and double-click your SCR file. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-12566019-10] Press Esc and double-click your mouse in the drawing area. requires: 09
