1. [step: WH-9950722-01] Open an Excel spreadsheet. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9950722-02] Double-click an empty cell. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9950722-03] Type =POWER(base,power). requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9950722-04] Replace "base" with your base number. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9950722-05] Replace "power" with your exponent. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9950722-06] Hit Enter on your keyboard. requires: 05
