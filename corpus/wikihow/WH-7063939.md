1. [step: WH-7063939-01] Check the display connection on your laptop and monitor. requires: none
2. [step: WH-7063939-02] Plug your laptop and monitor in. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7063939-03] Connect the mouse and keyboard to your laptop. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7063939-04] Connect the laptop to the monitor using an HDMI cable or HDMI cable and adapter. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7063939-05] Power on your computer and log in. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7063939-06] Click Windows Start followed by the Gear icon. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-7063939-07] Click System followed by Display in the panel to the left. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-7063939-08] Click Detect and set "Multiple Displays" to "Duplicate". requires: 07
9. [step: WH-7063939-09] Open the Control Panel. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-7063939-10] Click Hardware and Sound followed by Power Options. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-7063939-11] Click Choose what closing the lid does. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-7063939-12] Set the laptop to "Do nothing" when the lid is closed. requires: 11
