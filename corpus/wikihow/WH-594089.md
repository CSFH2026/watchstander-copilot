1. [step: WH-594089-01] Open an image in Photoshop Elements. requires: none
2. [step: WH-594089-02] Select the Magic Eraser, Background Eraser, or Eraser tool. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-594089-03] Select a hard brush. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-594089-04] Press [ or ] to adjust the brush size. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-594089-05] Use a large, solid brush to erase the bulk of the background. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-594089-06] Set the hardness of the brush to about 90%. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-594089-07] Zoom in and use single clicks with a small brush around the edge of the subject. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-594089-08] Save the image as a PNG, GIF, or PSD file when the background has been erased. requires: 07
