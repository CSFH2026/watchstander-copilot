2. [step: WH-883926-02] Beat the egg whites. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-883926-03] Mix 1/3 cups of sugar with the yolk. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-883926-04] Put 2 cups of milk in a pot and cook it to a simmer. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-883926-05] Pour the milk in with the sugar and yolk mixture. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-883926-06] Put the mixture back in the pot and cook while mixing until it has a thick custard-like texture. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-883926-07] Mix with the egg whites. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-883926-08] Add sea salt. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-883926-09] Put it in the fridge until room temperature. requires: 08
