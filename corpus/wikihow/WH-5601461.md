1. [step: WH-5601461-01] Open a web page or app you want to log in to. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5601461-02] Click or tap Log In, Sign In or something similar. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5601461-03] Enter your username or email address in the "Username" or "Email" field. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5601461-04] Click or tap Next if no password field is available. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5601461-05] Enter your password in the "Password" field. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-5601461-06] Click or tap Next, Submit, Log In, Sign In or something similar. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-5601461-07] Retrieve a verification code from your text messages if two-factor authentication is activated. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-5601461-08] Enter the verification code and click "Verify'. requires: 07
