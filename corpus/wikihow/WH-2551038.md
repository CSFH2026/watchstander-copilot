1. [step: WH-2551038-01] Power off your phone. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2551038-02] Press the button combination for your phone model to boot into the Bootloader menu. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2551038-03] Select Recovery mode. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2551038-04] Select Wipe data/factory reset. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2551038-05] Select Yes. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2551038-06] Reboot your phone. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-2551038-07] Go through the initial setup process. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-2551038-08] Log in to the same Google account you used before the factory reset. requires: 07
