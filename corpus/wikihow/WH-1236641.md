1. [step: WH-1236641-01] Remove the book binding with a utility knife or blow drier. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1236641-02] Use a utility knife to cut out pages. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1236641-03] Remove binding glue. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1236641-04] Keep pages in order. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1236641-05] Download Paper Port or something similar. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1236641-06] Feed the pages to the scanner one at a time. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1236641-07] Use color for both covers and black-and-white for pages. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1236641-08] Save as a PDF. requires: 07
