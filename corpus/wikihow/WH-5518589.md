1. [step: WH-5518589-01] Go to https://www.youtube.com/ in a web browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5518589-02] Click your profile image in the upper-right corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5518589-03] Click YouTube Studio. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5518589-04] Click Customization. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5518589-05] Click the Basic Info tab. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-5518589-06] Click Add Link. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-5518589-07] Add a title for the link in the first box. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-5518589-08] Add the link URL in the second box. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-5518589-09] Use the drop-down menu below "Links on banner" to select how many links to appear on your banner. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-5518589-10] Click Publish in the upper-right corner. requires: 09
