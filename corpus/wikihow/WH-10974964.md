1. [step: WH-10974964-01] Open your text messaging app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10974964-02] Tap on the conversation thread containing the message you want to forward. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10974964-03] Long press on the text message you want to forward. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10974964-04] Select Forward from the list of options. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10974964-05] Choose the contact you wish to forward the message to. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10974964-06] Tap Send. requires: 05
