1. [step: WH-11251264-01] Go to https://help.twitch.tv/s/contactsupport?language=en_US. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11251264-02] Fill in the information, such as your name, your Twitch name, your email, and your operating system. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11251264-03] Select "Account/Login Issues" under Category. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11251264-04] Select "Reactivate Account" under Sub Category. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11251264-05] Click Submit. requires: 04
