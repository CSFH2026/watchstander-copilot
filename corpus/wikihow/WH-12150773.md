1. [step: WH-12150773-01] Open Adobe Acrobat. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12150773-02] Open the PDF to which you want to add page numbers. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12150773-03] Click the arrow pointing left icon. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12150773-04] Click Edit PDF. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12150773-05] Click Header & Footer and Add. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12150773-06] Place your cursor in any Header text field and click Insert Page Number. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12150773-07] Click OK. requires: 06
