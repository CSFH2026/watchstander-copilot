1. [step: WH-1375380-01] Open Microsoft Word. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1375380-02] Click the Insert menu. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1375380-03] Click WordArt. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1375380-04] Type the letter you want to be the largest. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1375380-05] Select a font face and size. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1375380-06] Add two more WordArt letters. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1375380-07] Make the sizes of the second letters ½ the size of the first. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1375380-08] Arrange the letters as desired. requires: 07
