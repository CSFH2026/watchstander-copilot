1. [step: WH-1439697-01] Find the nominal impedance rating on the label. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1439697-02] Set a multimeter to measure resistance. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1439697-03] Remove speaker from the cabinet. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1439697-04] Remove power from the speaker. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1439697-05] Connect the multimeter leads to the speaker terminals. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1439697-06] Estimate the impedance from the resistance. requires: 05
