1. [step: WH-605920-01] Press C to open the Circle tool. requires: none
2. [step: WH-605920-02] Enter "100" as the number of sides. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-605920-03] Draw a circle. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-605920-04] Hover the cursor over the center and press the left arrow. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-605920-05] Click the center of the circle. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-605920-06] Click the edge of the first circle to draw the second circle. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-605920-07] Switch to the Select tool. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-605920-08] Click the first circle and press Delete. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-605920-09] Click the path of the first circle. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-605920-10] Open the Follow Me too. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-605920-11] Click the second circle to create a sphere. requires: 10
