1. [step: WH-11212478-01] Open the Terminal. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11212478-02] Type "sudo apt-get update, and press Enter. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11212478-03] Type "sudo apt-get install gnome-shell ubuntu-gnome-desktop.". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11212478-04] Press Enter. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11212478-05] Type "Y" and press Enter when prompted. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11212478-06] Select gdm3 or lightdm, and press Enter. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11212478-07] Restart your system at the end. requires: 06
