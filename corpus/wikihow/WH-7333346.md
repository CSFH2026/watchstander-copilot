1. [step: WH-7333346-01] Download and install CloudReady from https://www.neverware.com/freedownload/.. requires: none
2. [step: WH-7333346-02] Download Etcher from https://www.balena.io/etcher/. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7333346-03] Flash CloudReady to USB drive. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7333346-04] Restart your computer and boot from the USB drive. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7333346-05] Log in as a guest. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7333346-06] Press Ctrl+Alt+F2. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-7333346-07] Enter "sudo /usr/sbin/chromeos-install --dst /dev/sda". requires: 06
8. [step: WH-7333346-08] Enable Proprietary Services for Netflix. requires: 07
