1. [step: WH-9315762-01] Open the Google Sheets app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9315762-02] Tap the "+" icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9315762-03] Tap New spreadsheet. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9315762-04] Tap the A1 cell. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9315762-05] Type Word1_Word2 in the A1 cell. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9315762-06] Tap another empty cell. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9315762-07] Type =SPLIT(A1,"_"). requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9315762-08] Tap the checkmark in bottom-right. requires: 07
