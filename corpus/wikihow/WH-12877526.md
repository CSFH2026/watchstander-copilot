1. [step: WH-12877526-01] Open your project in Google Docs. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12877526-02] Click the Insert tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12877526-03] Hover over Drawing and click New. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12877526-04] Click the text box icon. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12877526-05] Drag and drop to create the text box. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12877526-06] Enter text into the box. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12877526-07] Click Save and close. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12877526-08] Click the text box to select it. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-12877526-09] Click the wrap or break text icons. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-12877526-10] Set a 0" margin. requires: 09
