1. [step: WH-1152652-01] Make sure your computer has an expandable hard drive slot or replaceable hard drive. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1152652-02] Back up all your data. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1152652-03] Purchase a hard drive that fits your needs and is the right size for your computer. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1152652-04] Shut down your computer. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1152652-05] Remove the side panel on your desktop tower or bottom panel on your laptop. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1152652-06] Disconnect the SATA cable and power supply cable from the old hard drive. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1152652-07] Remove any screws or the special enclosure holding the old hard drive in place. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1152652-08] Place the new hard drive in the enclosure, or hard drive slot. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1152652-09] Attach the SATA cable and power supply cable. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-1152652-10] Secure the hard drive in place with screws. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-1152652-11] Replace the side or bottom panel on your desktop tower or laptop. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-1152652-12] Power on your computer. requires: 11
13. [step: WH-1152652-13] Install a new operating system on your computer. requires: 12
