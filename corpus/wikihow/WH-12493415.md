1. [step: WH-12493415-01] Open Unity Hub. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12493415-02] Click the Installs tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12493415-03] Click the three dots and select Uninstall. Do this for all versions. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12493415-04] Quit Unity Hub. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12493415-05] Go to your Windows Settings > Apps. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12493415-06] Click Unity Hub and select Uninstall. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12493415-07] Click Uninstall to confirm. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12493415-08] Click Uninstall on the Unity Uninstall utilty. requires: 07
