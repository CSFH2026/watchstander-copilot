1. [step: WH-12816030-01] Go to https://etsy.com and sign in. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12816030-02] Click Shop Manager. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12816030-03] Click Orders & Shipping. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12816030-04] Click to select the order that has the address you need to ship to. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12816030-05] Click Download/Get Shipping Labels. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12816030-06] Purchase the label. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12816030-07] Click Print a shipping label. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12816030-08] Click the print icon and Print. requires: 07
