1. [step: WH-11853397-01] Open your project in Paint. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11853397-02] Click the down arrow under "Select.". requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11853397-03] Click Select all. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11853397-04] Click Resize. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11853397-05] Click to select if you want to resize the image in percentages or pixels. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11853397-06] Click OK. requires: 05
