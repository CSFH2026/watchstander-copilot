1. [step: WH-174242-01] Place a DVD in your disc drive and open VLC. requires: none
2. [step: WH-174242-02] Click Media in the menu bar. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-174242-03] Click Open Disc. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-174242-04] Check the "No disc menus" box. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-174242-05] Click the drop-down menu to the right of the Play button. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-174242-06] Select an MP4 format in the "Profile" drop-down menu. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-174242-07] Click the Browse button. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-174242-08] Type a file name for the video followed by ".mp4". requires: 07
9. [step: WH-174242-09] Select a save location and click Save. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-174242-10] Click Start. requires: 09
