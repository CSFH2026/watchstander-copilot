1. [step: WH-7052225-01] Click the Apple icon in the menu bar in the upper-left corner. requires: none
2. [step: WH-7052225-02] Click System Preferences. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7052225-03] Click the Parental Controls icon. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7052225-04] Click the lock icon in the lower-left corner of the Parental Controls window. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7052225-05] Enter the admin username and password. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7052225-06] Select a restricted account to the left. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-7052225-07] Click the Gear icon below the accounts list. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-7052225-08] Click Turn off Parental Controls. requires: 07
