1. [step: WH-12085699-01] Open the Alexa app on your phone or tablet. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12085699-02] Tap the three-line menu icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12085699-03] Tap Settings. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12085699-04] Tap Device Settings. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12085699-05] Tap to select your Echo Dot. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12085699-06] Tap Do Not Disturb. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12085699-07] Tap the switch to turn it on next to "Do Not Disturb.". requires: 06
