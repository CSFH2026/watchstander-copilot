1. [step: WH-6810238-01] Turn on your HDTV. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6810238-02] Change your TV's input to enable screen mirroring. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6810238-03] Unlock your Samsung Galaxy device. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6810238-04] Swipe down from the top of your screen with two fingers. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6810238-05] Tap Edit (or the pencil icon). requires: 04
6. [step: WH-6810238-06] Select Screen Mirroring (or Smart View). requires: 05
7. [step: WH-6810238-07] Select your TV from the list of compatible devices. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-6810238-08] Select Connect using PIN and type in the PIN shown on your TV if prompted to do so. requires: 07
