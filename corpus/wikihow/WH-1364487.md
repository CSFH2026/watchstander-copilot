3. [step: WH-1364487-03] Click the Apple menu. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1364487-04] Click System Preferences…. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1364487-05] Click Security & Privacy. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1364487-06] Click the lock. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1364487-07] Enter your password and click Unlock. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1364487-08] Click Open Anyway and then Open. requires: 07
