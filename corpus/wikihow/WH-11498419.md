1. [step: WH-11498419-01] Go to https://my.solidworks.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11498419-02] Click Sign Up. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11498419-03] Click Continue to create your ID. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11498419-04] Create a SolidWorks online account. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11498419-05] Navigate to the email from SolidWorks. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11498419-06] Click Verify Your Email. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11498419-07] Login to SolidWorks. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11498419-08] Click Try SOLIDWORKS. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11498419-09] Click to select the box next to "I agree and acknowledge the privacy policy agreement.". requires: 08
10. [step: WH-11498419-10] Select a language and click Launch. requires: 09
