1. [step: WH-1863925-01] Open or create a new Pro Tools session. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1863925-02] Click Track in the menu bar at the top. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1863925-03] Click New. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1863925-04] Click Track to add a new track. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1863925-05] Select the audio format the mix (i.e Mono, Stereo, 5.1, etc). requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1863925-06] Select Master Fader as the track type. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1863925-07] Add a name for the track (optional). requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1863925-08] Click Create. requires: 07
