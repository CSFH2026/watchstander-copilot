1. [step: WH-10156994-01] Unlock your iPhone. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10156994-02] Tell Siri to enable VoiceOver. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10156994-03] Connect an external keyboard to your iPhone. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10156994-04] Tell Siri to open "iCloud Settings.". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10156994-05] Press the right arrow key on your keyboard to navigate to iCloud Backup. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10156994-06] Press Ctrl + Alt + Space (Windows) or Ctrl + Option + Space (Mac). requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10156994-07] Find and select Back Up Now. requires: 06
