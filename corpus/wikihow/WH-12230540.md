1. [step: WH-12230540-01] Install iTunes and iCloud from Apple's website if you're using Windows. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12230540-02] Download AltServer from https://altstore.io and install it on your computer. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12230540-03] Enable Wi-Fi syncing for your iPhone/iPad. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12230540-04] Connect your iPhone/iPad to the computer. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12230540-05] Use AltServer on your computer to install AltStore on your iPhone. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12230540-06] Launch AltStore on your phone or tablet and close it when you see an error. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12230540-07] Trust your Apple ID in your phone or tablet's Device Management settings. requires: 06
