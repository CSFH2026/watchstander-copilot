1. [step: WH-271262-01] Press Ctrl + Windows Key + Enter to turn off Narrator. requires: none
2. [step: WH-271262-02] Type "ease of access" into the Windows Search bar. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-271262-03] Click Ease of Access Center. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-271262-04] Click Use the computer without a display. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-271262-05] Remove the checkmark from "Turn on narrator.". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-271262-06] Click Apply. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-271262-07] Click OK. requires: 06
