1. [step: WH-9048650-01] Open the Skype app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9048650-02] Tap a group chat. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9048650-03] Tap the message field. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9048650-04] Type a forward slash followed by "setrole username Admin" in the message field. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9048650-05] Replace "username" with the new admin's username. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9048650-06] Tap Send. requires: 05
