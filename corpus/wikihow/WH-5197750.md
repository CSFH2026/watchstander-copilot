1. [step: WH-5197750-01] Open a Word document. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5197750-02] Click a place in the document. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5197750-03] Click Insert. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5197750-04] Click the Object drop-down. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5197750-05] Choose the type of file to insert. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-5197750-06] Select a file to insert. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-5197750-07] Click OK. requires: 06
