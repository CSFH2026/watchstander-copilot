1. [step: WH-12506317-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12506317-02] Navigate to the cell with the HTML tags you want to delete. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12506317-03] Press Ctrl + H . requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12506317-04] Type the HTML tags in the cells that you want to delete in the "Find what" field. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12506317-05] Leave the "Replace with" field blank. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12506317-06] Click Replace All. requires: 05
