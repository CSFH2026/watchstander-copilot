1. [step: WH-10277324-01] Open Fotor Cliparts in your browser, and click Get Started. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10277324-02] Click + Import on the top-right, and upload an image. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10277324-03] Click Stickers on the left sidebar. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10277324-04] Select a category, and click a sticker. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10277324-05] Click and move the sticker around your image. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10277324-06] Click and move the sticker's corners to resize it. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10277324-07] Click Save on the top-right, and click Download. requires: 06
