1. [step: WH-16324-01] Note the make and model of your printer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-16324-02] Open the print cartridge compartment and note the manufacturer, color and number on the cartridges. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-16324-03] Purchase new cartridges. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-16324-04] Follow the instructions inside the printer compartment to remove the old cartridges. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-16324-05] Remove the new cartridges from their packaging and remove the protective adhesives and tabs. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-16324-06] Insert the new cartridges the reverse way they were removed. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-16324-07] Print a test page. requires: 06
