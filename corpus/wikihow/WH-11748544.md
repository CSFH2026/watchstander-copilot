1. [step: WH-11748544-01] Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11748544-02] Tap your name, then Name, Phone Numbers, Email. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11748544-03] Tap Edit next to "Reachable At". Delete your current Apple ID and replace with a new email address. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11748544-04] Tap Continue. Enter the new email address to use for your Apple ID. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11748544-05] Verify the new email address. requires: 04
