1. [step: WH-45861-01] Enable Solver in the "Add-ins" section of your Excel preferences if necessary. requires: none
2. [step: WH-45861-02] Open a spreadsheet with data you want to analyze. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-45861-03] Click Data, then click Solver. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-45861-04] Select a cell to use from the "Set Objective" field. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-45861-05] Check the "Value Of" box, then enter a desired value. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-45861-06] Click Solve. requires: 05
