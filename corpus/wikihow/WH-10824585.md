1. [step: WH-10824585-01] Open the Groupon app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10824585-02] Tap My Stuff on the bottom-right. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10824585-03] Tap My Groupons. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10824585-04] Tap ALL on the top-right. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10824585-05] Tap the deal you want to review. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10824585-06] Tap Write a Review above the Instructions. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10824585-07] Select a number of stars at the top. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-10824585-08] Enter your comments, and Add Photos (optional). requires: 07
9. [step: WH-10824585-09] Tap Submit on the top-right. requires: 08
