2. [step: WH-12695340-02] Click Accounts and Family & other users. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12695340-03] Click Add a family member. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12695340-04] Click Create one for a child (of the child does not have a Microsoft email already). requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12695340-05] Follow the on-screen instructions to create a Microsoft account. requires: 04
