1. [step: WH-11049529-01] Open the My Verizon app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11049529-02] Tap the three-lined menu icon on the top-left. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11049529-03] Tap Bill on the menu. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11049529-04] Tap Pay Bill. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11049529-05] Tap the payment amount to change it. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11049529-06] Tap the date to change the payment date. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11049529-07] Scroll and pick a payment method. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11049529-08] Tap Pay bill. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11049529-09] Tap OK. requires: 08
