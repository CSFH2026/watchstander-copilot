1. [step: WH-1375679-01] Turn on Windows File and Printer Sharing. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1375679-02] Right-click the folder you want to share. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1375679-03] Click Share with. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1375679-04] Click Specific people. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1375679-05] Select permissions for the person you're sharing with. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1375679-06] Click Share. requires: 05
