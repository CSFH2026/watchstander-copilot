1. [step: WH-1482579-01] Download and install KillDisk from http://killdisk.com/killdisk-freeware.htm. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1482579-02] Connect your external hard drive. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1482579-03] Open KillDisk. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1482579-04] Select your external hard drive from the list of connected hard drives. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1482579-05] Click Erase Disk at the top of the app. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1482579-06] Click Start. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1482579-07] Cllick Ok. requires: 06
