1. [step: WH-235890-01] Place the thread on the spool pin. requires: none
2. [step: WH-235890-02] Wind the bobbin with the bobbin winder. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-235890-03] Wrap the thread around the thread guide on the top of the sewing machine. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-235890-04] Pull the thread down through the guide and around the tension disks. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-235890-05] Loop the thread back up towards the second thread guide to create a “U” shape. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-235890-06] Wrap the thread around the takeup lever. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-235890-07] Guide the thread through the needle’s eye. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-235890-08] Place the bobbin in the compartment below the needle. requires: 07
