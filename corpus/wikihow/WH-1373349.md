1. [step: WH-1373349-01] Place the document on the scanner. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1373349-02] Open Windows Fax and Scan. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1373349-03] Click New Scan. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1373349-04] Select your scanner if not already selected. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1373349-05] Select either Photo or Documents. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1373349-06] Adjust other options and click Scan. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1373349-07] Click File. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1373349-08] Click Print... requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1373349-09] Select Microsoft Print to PDF as the printer. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-1373349-10] Name the file and click Save. requires: 09
