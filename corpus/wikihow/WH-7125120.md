1. [step: WH-7125120-01] Download and install Origin from https://www.origin.com/usa/en-us/store/download. requires: none
2. [step: WH-7125120-02] Open Origin and log in. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7125120-03] Click Origin in the menu bar at the top. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7125120-04] Click Redeem product code. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7125120-05] Enter the product code on the back of the game user's manual. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7125120-06] Click Next. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-7125120-07] Click My Games and select the Sims 3. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-7125120-08] Open The Sims 3 from inside Origin. requires: 07
