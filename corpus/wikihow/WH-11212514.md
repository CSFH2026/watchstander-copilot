1. [step: WH-11212514-01] Download "Rec Screen Recorder" from the App Store. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11212514-02] Open your Settings menu. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11212514-03] Tap Control Center, and then Customize Controls. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11212514-04] Tap the green "+" next to "Screen Recording.". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11212514-05] Open WhatsApp. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11212514-06] Swipe up from the bottom, and hold down the white dot icon . requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11212514-07] Select Rec, tap the gray mic icon, and tap Start Broadcasting. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11212514-08] Make your WhatsApp call. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11212514-09] Tap the red banner at the top, and select Stop to finish. requires: 08
