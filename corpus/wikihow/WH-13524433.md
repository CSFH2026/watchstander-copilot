1. [step: WH-13524433-01] Open the Instagram app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13524433-02] Tap the username of a profile you want to share. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13524433-03] Tap the icon with three dots in the upper-right corner. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13524433-04] Tap Copy profile URL. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13524433-05] Paste the URL in a message to someone you want to share the profile with. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-13524433-06] Send the message. requires: 05
