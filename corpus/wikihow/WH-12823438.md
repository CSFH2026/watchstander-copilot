1. [step: WH-12823438-01] Go to https://www.outlook.com in your computer's web browser or open the desktop app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12823438-02] Click New Message. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12823438-03] Enter the contents of your email. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12823438-04] Select the hyperlink text. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12823438-05] Click the three-dot menu icon in the formatting bar. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12823438-06] Click the chain link icon. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12823438-07] Enter the URL. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12823438-08] Click OK. requires: 07
