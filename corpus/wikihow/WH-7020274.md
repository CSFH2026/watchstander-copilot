1. [step: WH-7020274-01] Go to https://pokedit.com/editor/box-page.php in a web browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-7020274-02] Use the options on the web page to create a custom Pokémon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7020274-03] Click Apply Edits to the left. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7020274-04] Click Download PKM to the left. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7020274-05] Click download Shiny 2 and a half from https://github.com/Gannio/Shiny2-and-a-Half/releases/tag/1.5.1. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7020274-06] Launch Shiny2 and select your game under "GTS Operations.". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-7020274-07] Click DNS Settings and click Detect IP (LAN). requires: 06
8. [step: WH-7020274-08] Turn on your Nintendo 3DS and go to System Settings > Internet Settings > Connection Settings > Your wireless connection > Change Settings > DNS. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-7020274-09] Change the primary DNS to match your computer's IP address in Shiny2. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-7020274-10] Click Run in Shiny2 and click Initialize DNS followed by Initialized GTS. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-7020274-11] Launch your Pokémon and go to a Global Terminal. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-7020274-12] Talk to the lady and select Global Trade. requires: 11
13. [step: WH-7020274-13] Select GTS followed by Trade. requires: 12
14. [step: WH-7020274-14] Connect to Wi-Fi and receive your Pokémon. requires: 13
