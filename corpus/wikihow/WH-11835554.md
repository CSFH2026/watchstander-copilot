1. [step: WH-11835554-01] Open the Google Form on a browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11835554-02] Click Responses. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11835554-03] Click the 3 dots in the top right under "Responses". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11835554-04] Click Print all responses. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11835554-05] Set your print options and click Print. requires: 04
