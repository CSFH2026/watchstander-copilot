1. [step: WH-12318640-01] Go to https://www.linkedin.com/feed/ in a web browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12318640-02] Click your profile picture and click View Profile. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12318640-03] Click Add profile section. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12318640-04] Click Accomplishments. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12318640-05] Click Honors & Awards. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12318640-06] Add the details of your honor or award. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12318640-07] Click Save. requires: 06
