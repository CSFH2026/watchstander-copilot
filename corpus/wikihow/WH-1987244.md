1. [step: WH-1987244-01] Log into your Twitter account. Click on "Settings and privacy". requires: none
2. [step: WH-1987244-02] Scroll down to "Settings." Click on the "Privacy and safety" tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1987244-03] Scroll down to the privacy section. Check the "Protect my Tweets.". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1987244-04] Scroll down to the bottom of the page. Hit "Save changes". requires: 03
