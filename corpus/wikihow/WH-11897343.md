1. [step: WH-11897343-01] Make sure your printer is powered on. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11897343-02] Open the front cover. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11897343-03] Pull the current toner cartridge and drum unit assembly out. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11897343-04] Depress the green lever on the drum unit assembly. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11897343-05] Lift the current toner cartridge out of the drum unit assembly. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11897343-06] Remove the protective covering from the new toner cartridge. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11897343-07] Place the new toner cartridge into the drum unit assembly. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11897343-08] Slide the green tab left and right several times. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11897343-09] Insert the toner cartridge and drum unit assembly back into the printer. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-11897343-10] Shut the front cover. requires: 09
