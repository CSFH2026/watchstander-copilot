1. [step: WH-13020831-01] Have a friend look up your account. If it does not show up, you have likely been banned. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13020831-02] Log out and try to log back in. If you have been banned, a pop up should appear telling you your account has been disabled. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13020831-03] Look for pop-up messages on Instagram saying something like "You're temporarily blocked", then use the Tell us button to appeal. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13020831-04] Check for an email from Instagram informing you your account has been banned. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13020831-05] Test your internet connection to ensure you are not just experiencing bad connectivity. requires: 04
