1. [step: WH-2621082-01] Press and hold Alt and type "0176" in Windows. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2621082-02] Press Shift + Option + 8 on a Mac. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2621082-03] Press Shift + Ctrl + U and then type "00B0" and press Enter on Chromebook. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2621082-04] Press Shift + Ctrl + U and then type "B0" and press Enter on Linux. requires: 03
