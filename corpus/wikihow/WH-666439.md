1. [step: WH-666439-01] Download an official ISO image. requires: none
2. [step: WH-666439-02] Burn it to a CD or DVD (or make a bootable flash drive). requires: 01
3. [step: WH-666439-03] Boot from the disc or drive. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-666439-04] Select your settings and click OK. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-666439-05] Go to Menu > Setup > Puppy Installer. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-666439-06] Click Universal installer. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-666439-07] Select an install location and partition. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-666439-08] Choose a Frugal' or Full install. requires: 07
