1. [step: WH-8509961-01] Open the Snapchat app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8509961-02] Swipe down on the screen. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8509961-03] Tap Add Friends. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8509961-04] Tap Add by Username. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8509961-05] Search for another user. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8509961-06] Tap on the search result. requires: 05
