1. [step: WH-4881298-01] Log in to https://contacts.google.com/ and click a contact. requires: none
2. [step: WH-4881298-02] Click the icon with three dots (⋮) below the contact's name and click Export. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-4881298-03] Export the file as a "Google CSV" file. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-4881298-04] Open the Google CSV file in Excel and enter all your contact information in the correct column. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-4881298-05] Save the file as a CSV file. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-4881298-06] Log back in to https://contacts.google.com/ and click Import in the menu to the left. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-4881298-07] Import the CSV file you saved from Excel. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-4881298-08] Sync your Google account in the Settings menu on your Android phone. requires: 07
