1. [step: WH-10361502-01] Purchase a TV that supports 4K with a refresh rate of at least 60 Hz. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10361502-02] Connect your gaming device to your TV using an HDMI 2.1 cable. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10361502-03] Set your TV to "Game Mode" or "PC Mode". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10361502-04] Turn off all post-processing effects on your TV. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10361502-05] Make sure your gaming device is set to the resolution that matches your TV. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10361502-06] Set "RGB Range" to "Limited" on your gaming device. requires: 05
