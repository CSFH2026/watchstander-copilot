1. [step: WH-9951269-01] Open the image in Photoshop. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9951269-02] Click Image. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9951269-03] Click Image Size. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9951269-04] Select "Constrain Proportions.". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9951269-05] Enter the width and height. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9951269-06] Click OK. requires: 05
