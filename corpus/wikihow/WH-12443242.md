3. [step: WH-12443242-03] Click the Scatter icon. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12443242-04] Click the first Scatter chart. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12443242-05] Right-click one of the data points on the chart. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12443242-06] Click Add Trendline. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12443242-07] Select "Linear.". requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12443242-08] Check the box next to "Display equation on chart.". requires: 07
