1. [step: WH-8819968-01] Open Facebook on a browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8819968-02] Click Pages on the left panel. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8819968-03] Click the Your Pages tab. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8819968-04] Click on a page. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8819968-05] Scroll down and find the offer you want to hide. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8819968-06] Click the downward-facing arrow icon on the offer. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8819968-07] Click Hide from Timeline. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-8819968-08] Click Hide to confirm. requires: 07
