1. [step: WH-11174863-01] Open the Ubuntu Software Center. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11174863-02] Search for "GNOME Tweaks" and install the app. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11174863-03] Open the Terminal. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11174863-04] Type "sudo apt install gnome-shell-extension" in the terminal and enter your password. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11174863-05] Restart Ubuntu. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11174863-06] Search and Download Ubuntu themes from the internet. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11174863-07] Open the Archive Manager and click the icon with three horizontal lines. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11174863-08] Click Show Hidden Files. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11174863-09] Click Downloads in the sidebar to the left. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-11174863-10] Extract the downloaded theme files. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-11174863-11] Copy the extracted folder. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-11174863-12] Click Home. requires: 11
13. [step: WH-11174863-13] Paste extracted theme folders in the ".themes" folder, and the extracted icons themes in the ".icons" folder. requires: 12
