1. [step: WH-6289302-01] Go to https://pixlr.com/editor/ in a web browser and click Continue with Pixlr Editor. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6289302-02] Click Create a New Image. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6289302-03] Click the checkbox next to "Transparent". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6289302-04] Click Open Image as Layer under the "Layers" menu. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6289302-05] Use the Wand Tool to select the background. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-6289302-06] Press Delete to remove the select areas of the background. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-6289302-07] Use the Eraser tool to remove any remaining backround. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-6289302-08] Click Save under the "File" menu. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-6289302-09] Select PNG as the image format and click Ok. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-6289302-10] Click Save. requires: 09
