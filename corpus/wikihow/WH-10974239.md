1. [step: WH-10974239-01] Click on Settings in the top right corner of your Facebook Page. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10974239-02] Click Messages from the list of options. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10974239-03] Make sure the box is checked and click Save Changes. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10974239-04] Return to your page. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10974239-05] Click on + Add a Button beneath your cover image. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10974239-06] Complete the steps and click Finish to invite users to message your page. requires: 05
