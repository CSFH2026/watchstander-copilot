1. [step: WH-11616547-01] Open the PDF in a reader like Edge or Preview. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11616547-02] Save each page as its own individual PDF file. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11616547-03] Click the mouse in your Word document where you want to insert the pages. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11616547-04] Click Insert' > Object. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11616547-05] Insert the first PDF page. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11616547-06] Repeat for all other pages. requires: 05
