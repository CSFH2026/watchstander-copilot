1. [step: WH-4225660-01] Open Google Chrome. requires: none
2. [step: WH-4225660-02] Navigate to a web page you want to save. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-4225660-03] Click the icon with three dots in the upper-right corner. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-4225660-04] Click Print. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-4225660-05] Select Save as PDF in the drop-down menu next to "Destination". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-4225660-06] Click Save. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-4225660-07] Select a location for the PDF. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-4225660-08] Click Save in the lower-right corner. requires: 07
