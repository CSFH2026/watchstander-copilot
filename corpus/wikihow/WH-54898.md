1. [step: WH-54898-01] Fold a square piece of paper into a triangle. requires: none
2. [step: WH-54898-02] Position a drinking straws so that one end is level with the corner and one end extends past it. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-54898-03] Attach the straw to the paper with tape or glue. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-54898-04] Punch a small hole next to the end of the straw that’s level with the paper. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-54898-05] Tie a string around the hole. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-54898-06] Tape a paper clip to the bottom of the string so that it’s dangling 2 inches below the corner of the triangle. requires: 05
