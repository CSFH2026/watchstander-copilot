1. [step: WH-12103558-01] Go to https://account.microsoft.com/account in a web browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12103558-02] Click Sign In. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12103558-03] Enter your email, phone number, or Skype, then click Next. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12103558-04] Enter your password, then click Sign in. requires: 03
