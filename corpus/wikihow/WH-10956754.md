1. [step: WH-10956754-01] Go to https://www.facebook.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10956754-02] Click the black down arrow in the top right corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10956754-03] Click Settings. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10956754-04] Scroll down to the Your Facebook Information section. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10956754-05] Click View. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10956754-06] Click Create File and wait for Facebook to compile your data. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10956754-07] Click Available Files. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-10956754-08] Click Download. requires: 07
