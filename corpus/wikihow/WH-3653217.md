1. [step: WH-3653217-01] Download BlueStacks from https://www.bluestacks.com/download.html. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3653217-02] Open BlueStacks and sign in with your Google account. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3653217-03] Open the Google Play Store and search for a game. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3653217-04] Install a game from the Google Play Store. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3653217-05] Launch the game from the BlueStacks home screen. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-3653217-06] Use the mouse to tap and tap and drag. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-3653217-07] Use the arrow keys to zoom in and out. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-3653217-08] Tap the icon that resembles a keyboard in sidebar panel to the right. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-3653217-09] Use the keyboard mapping options to assign keyboard controls. requires: 08
