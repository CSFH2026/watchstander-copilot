1. [step: WH-8706258-01] Open Settings. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8706258-02] Tap Cellular or Mobile. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8706258-03] Slide "App Store" to the On position. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8706258-04] Tap the back button and tap App Store. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8706258-05] Tap App Downloads and select an option. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8706258-06] Disconnect from Wi-Fi and download an app. requires: 05
