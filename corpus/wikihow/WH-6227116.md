1. [step: WH-6227116-01] Download the computer client in addition to the mobile app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6227116-02] Open WO Mic on your phone and computer. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6227116-03] Tap the Settings icon in the mobile app. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6227116-04] Tap Transport and select a transport mode. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6227116-05] Return to the main screen of the mobile app. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-6227116-06] Tap the start icon in the mobile app. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-6227116-07] Click the Connection tab in the WO Mic computer client. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-6227116-08] Click Connect. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-6227116-09] Click to select a transport (connection) type (the same transport type you selected in the mobile app). requires: 08
