1. [step: WH-1519001-01] Connect your Android to your PC. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1519001-02] Open the Windows File Explorer to your Music folder. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1519001-03] Select music to add. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1519001-04] Click the Home tab. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1519001-05] Click Copy to. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1519001-06] Click Chose location. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1519001-07] Select your Android's Music folder. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1519001-08] Click Copy. requires: 07
