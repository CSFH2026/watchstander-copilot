1. [step: WH-286166-01] Install CCleaner on your computer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-286166-02] Open CCleaner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-286166-03] Select a category. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-286166-04] Uncheck the items you don't want to clean. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-286166-05] Click Analyze. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-286166-06] Review the files to be deleted. requires: 05

8. [step: WH-286166-08] Click Continue to confirm. requires: 07
