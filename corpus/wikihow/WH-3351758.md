1. [step: WH-3351758-01] Open your spreadsheet in Microsoft Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3351758-02] Select all data in the worksheet. A quick way to do this is to click the ""Select all"" button at the top-left corner of the worksheet. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3351758-03] Click the ""Home"" tab. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3351758-04] Click the ""Format"" button in the ""Cells"" section of the toolbar. A menu will expand. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3351758-05] Select ""Hide & Unhide"" on the menu. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-3351758-06] Click ""Unhide rows"" to make all hidden rows visible. requires: 05
