2. [step: WH-11623299-02] Open Tinder or go to https://tinder.com/. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11623299-03] Click or tap LOG IN WITH FACEBOOK or LOG IN WITH PHONE NUMBER. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11623299-04] Click or tap the appropriate boxes. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11623299-05] Enter your phone number. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11623299-06] Enter the code texted to your phone number. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11623299-07] Click or tap OK when prompted (Facebook only). requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11623299-08] Click or tap Allow when prompted. requires: 07
