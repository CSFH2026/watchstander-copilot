1. [step: WH-7393974-01] Download and install Prey from the App Store or Google Play Store. requires: none
2. [step: WH-7393974-02] Open Prey and sign up for a free account. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7393974-03] Tap Go to Permissions. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7393974-04] Allow Prey to access the phone features it needs to function. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7393974-05] Go to https://panel.preyproject.com/ in a web browser. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7393974-06] Sign in to your Prey account. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-7393974-07] Click the device that is missing. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-7393974-08] Click Set device to missing and confirm. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-7393974-09] Click Maps and actions to view the location of the phone. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-7393974-10] Click Reports to view the phone reports. requires: 09
