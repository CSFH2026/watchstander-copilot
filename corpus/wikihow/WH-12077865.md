1. [step: WH-12077865-01] Open Apple Music on your Mac. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12077865-02] Hover your mouse over Music and click Preferences. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12077865-03] Click Import Settings. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12077865-04] Click the menu next to "Import Using" and select MP3 Encoder. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12077865-05] Click OK. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12077865-06] Select the Voice Memo that you want to convert in your library. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12077865-07] Hover your mouse over File and Convert then click Create MP3 Version. requires: 06
