1. [step: WH-1473578-01] Highlight the data you want to format. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1473578-02] Click the arrow next to "Conditional Formatting.". requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1473578-03] Click New Rule. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1473578-04] Select Use a formula to determine which cells to format. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1473578-05] Enter this formula: =MOD(ROW(),2)=0. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1473578-06] Click Format. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1473578-07] Select a color on the Fill tab. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1473578-08] Click OK and then OK again. requires: 07
