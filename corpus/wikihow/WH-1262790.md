1. [step: WH-1262790-01] Sign in to https://docs.google.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1262790-02] Click the Upload icon and select Upload. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1262790-03] Click Select a file from your computer. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1262790-04] Select the PDF and click Open. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1262790-05] Click Open with Google Docs. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1262790-06] Edit as needed. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1262790-07] Click File and select Download as. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1262790-08] Click PDF Document. requires: 07
