1. [step: WH-8311178-01] Open your iPhone's Settings. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8311178-02] Scroll to the fourth group of options and select iCloud. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8311178-03] Tap iCloud Drive. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8311178-04] Scroll to the bottom of the page. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8311178-05] Slide the Use Cellular Data switch left to the "Off" position. requires: 04
