1. [step: WH-2670015-01] Open https://www.sprint.com/en/login.html in a web browser and log in. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2670015-02] Click My Sprint. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2670015-03] Click Permissions. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2670015-04] Click Block Voice or Block Text. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2670015-05] Click the line you want to block a number on. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2670015-06] Click Block only the following phone numbers for inbound and outbound calls. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-2670015-07] Type the number you want to block and click Add number. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-2670015-08] Wait 15 minutes. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-2670015-09] Turn your phone off and then back on again. requires: 08
