1. [step: WH-1400155-01] Right-click the internet connection on the host computer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1400155-02] Click Open Network and Internet Settings. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1400155-03] Click Network and Sharing Center. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1400155-04] Click Set up a new connection or network. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1400155-05] Click Set up a wireless ad hoc (computer-to-computer) network. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1400155-06] Enter the settings for your new wireless network. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1400155-07] Connect other computers to the new network. requires: 06
