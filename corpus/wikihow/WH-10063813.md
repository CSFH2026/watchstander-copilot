1. [step: WH-10063813-01] Open a spreadsheet, and click the chart you want to edit. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10063813-02] Click the Design or Chart Design tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10063813-03] Click Select Data on the toolbar. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10063813-04] Select a legend entry, and click Edit. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10063813-05] Enter a new name into the Series Name or Name box. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10063813-06] Enter a new value into the Y values box. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10063813-07] Click OK. requires: 06
