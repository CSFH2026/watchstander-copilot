1. [step: WH-11277811-01] Open Steam. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11277811-02] Click the Steam menu on the top-left. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11277811-03] Click Settings (Windows) or Preferences (Mac). requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11277811-04] Click Family on the left. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11277811-05] Check the Authorize Library Sharing on this computer box. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11277811-06] Check the box next to the friends you want to share with. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11277811-07] Click OK. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11277811-08] Click RESTART STEAM, if prompted. requires: 07
