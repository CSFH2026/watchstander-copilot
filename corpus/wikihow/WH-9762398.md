1. [step: WH-9762398-01] Open Google Sheets. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9762398-02] Click a spreadsheet. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9762398-03] Select a cell range. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9762398-04] Click the Format tab. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9762398-05] Select Conditional formatting. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9762398-06] Select Custom formula is on the "Format cells if" menu. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9762398-07] Type "=COUNTIF(A:A,A2)>1" into the formula box. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9762398-08] Click Done. requires: 07
