1. [step: WH-1410799-01] Write down the asset’s purchase price. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1410799-02] Estimate the salvage value, or how much the asset will be worth when it's no longer useful. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1410799-03] Calculate Depreciable Cost: purchase price - salvage value. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1410799-04] Estimate the asset's lifespan, which is how long you think the asset will be useful for. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1410799-05] Find the amount of Depreciation per Year by calculating depreciable cost/asset's lifespan. requires: 04
