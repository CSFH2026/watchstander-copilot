1. [step: WH-1294973-01] Type shutdown.exe -s -t 00 into Notepad. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1294973-02] Click File, then click Save As.... requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1294973-03] Click the "Save as type" drop-down box, then click All Files. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1294973-04] Type .bat at the end of the file's name in the "File name" text box. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1294973-05] Click Save. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1294973-06] Double-click the BAT file you just created to immediately shut down the computer. requires: 05
