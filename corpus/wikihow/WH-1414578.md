1. [step: WH-1414578-01] Open OBS Studio. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1414578-02] Go to the stream you want to save. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1414578-03] Click + in OBS. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1414578-04] Click Window Capture. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1414578-05] Enter a title and click Ok. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1414578-06] Select a window and click OK. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1414578-07] Click Start Recording. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1414578-08] Start the video stream. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1414578-09] Click Stop Recording when you're finished. requires: 08
