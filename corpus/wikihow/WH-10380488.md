2. [step: WH-10380488-02] Select Play as Survivor or Play as Killer. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10380488-03] Click the grid icon to select a character and then click Find a Match. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10380488-04] Repair generators as a survivor or damage survivors as a killer. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10380488-05] Find survivors and attack them as a killer. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10380488-06] Complete objectives. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10380488-07] Open the gates once all generators are repaired. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-10380488-08] Escape through the gates. requires: 07
