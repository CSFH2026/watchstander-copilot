1. [step: WH-5354125-01] Install Calibre Companion on your iPad. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5354125-02] Open Calibre Companion and tap the arrow. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5354125-03] Click Connect/Share in Calibre on your PC. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5354125-04] Click Start wireless device connection and select OK. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5354125-05] Tap CONNECT in Calibre Companion on your iPad. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-5354125-06] Tap as a Wireless Device on your iPad. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-5354125-07] Select a book in Calibre on your computer. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-5354125-08] Click Send to Device. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-5354125-09] Select the book in Calibre Companion and tap Read. requires: 08
