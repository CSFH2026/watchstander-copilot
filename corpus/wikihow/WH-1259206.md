1. [step: WH-1259206-01] Try turning Wi-Fi on using the keyboard key, Taskbar icon, or by clicking the toggle switch under "Network & Internet" in the Windows Settings menu. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1259206-02] Open the Windows Start menu and type "Troubleshoot.". requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1259206-03] Open Troubleshoot Settings and click Aditional Troubleshooters. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1259206-04] Click Internet Connection and click Run the troubleshooter. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1259206-05] Follow any instructions to correct any detected problems. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1259206-06] Click the Windows Start menu and type "Device Manager.". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1259206-07] Open Device Manager and expand "Network adapters". requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1259206-08] Right-click your wireless adapter and click Uninstall Driver. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1259206-09] Connect your computer the internet via an Ethernet cable. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-1259206-10] Restart your computer. requires: 09
