2. [step: WH-9410720-02] Sign in to a workspace. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9410720-03] Click the workspace name on the top-left. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9410720-04] Click Preferences. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9410720-05] Check the box next to Show All Unreads. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9410720-06] Click "X" and quit Preferences. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9410720-07] Click All Unreads on the left panel. requires: 06
