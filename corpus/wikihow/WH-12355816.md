1. [step: WH-12355816-01] Open SAP. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12355816-02] Type "IW21" into the bar at the top of your screen and press Enter or Return. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12355816-03] Click the entry next to "Notification type.". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12355816-04] Click Maintenance Request. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12355816-05] Click the green checkmark. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12355816-06] Enter a title for your notification next to "Notification.". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12355816-07] Enter the location of the object next to "Functional loc.". requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12355816-08] Click the icon to the right of "Functional loc.". requires: 07
9. [step: WH-12355816-09] Navigate to the equipment associated with the notification and double-click it. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-12355816-10] Enter a description of the notification in "Subject Long Text.". requires: 09
11. [step: WH-12355816-11] Click the save icon. requires: 10
