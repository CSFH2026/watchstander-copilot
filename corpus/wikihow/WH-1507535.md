1. [step: WH-1507535-01] Open PowerPoint. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1507535-02] Select the slide for your video. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1507535-03] Click the Insert tab. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1507535-04] Select Movie. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1507535-05] Click on Movie from File. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1507535-06] Locate the video on your hard drive. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1507535-07] Click Insert. requires: 06
