1. [step: WH-417130-01] Visit Craigslist.org. requires: none
2. [step: WH-417130-02] Select the state you live in. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-417130-03] Click "post to classifieds". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-417130-04] Click "for sale by owner". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-417130-05] Select a category that best describes your item. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-417130-06] Fill out the posting information. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-417130-07] Select "Add Images" to attach pictures of your item. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-417130-08] Click on "continue" then "publish" to post your listing. requires: 07
