1. [step: WH-8532490-01] Open Settings. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8532490-02] Tap Wi-Fi. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8532490-03] Tap the circled "i" icon next to your Wi-Fi name. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8532490-04] Copy the numbers next to "Router". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8532490-05] Paste the router's IP address into an address bar in your phone's web browser. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8532490-06] Sign into your router's page. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8532490-07] Navigate to your Wi-Fi settings. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-8532490-08] Locate the password. requires: 07
