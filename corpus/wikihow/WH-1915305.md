1. [step: WH-1915305-01] Open the Word document. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1915305-02] Find the comment you want to delete, right-click the comment, and click Delete Comment to delete a comment. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1915305-03] Click the Review tab, click Show Markup, and uncheck the Comments option to hide comments. requires: 02
