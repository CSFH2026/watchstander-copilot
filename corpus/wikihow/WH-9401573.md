1. [step: WH-9401573-01] Go to https://www.youtube.com/ in a web browser and sign in. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9401573-02] Tap Your Videos in the menu to the left. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9401573-03] Tap Filters above the list of videos. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9401573-04] Tap Copyright claims. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9401573-05] Hover over the Restrictions column next to a video. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9401573-06] Click Show details in the pop-up. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9401573-07] Click Select action to see how to resolve the copyright claim. requires: 06
