1. [step: WH-12777477-01] Open the Google Chrome browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12777477-02] Press Ctrl + O (Windows) or Cmd + O (Mac). requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12777477-03] Select the PDF and click Open. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12777477-04] Click the three-dot menu icon. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12777477-05] Click Print. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12777477-06] Click the drop-down menu next to "Destination" and select Save as PDF. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12777477-07] Click the drop-down menu next to "Pages.". requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12777477-08] Select Custom and enter the pages you want to extract. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-12777477-09] Click the Save button. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-12777477-10] Save your new PDF. requires: 09
