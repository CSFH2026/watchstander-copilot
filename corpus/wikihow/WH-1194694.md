1. [step: WH-1194694-01] Register at https://www.dropbox.com/register. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1194694-02] Log in to https://www.dropbox.com/home/. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1194694-03] Click My Files to browse files. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1194694-04] Click Upload to add a file. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1194694-05] Select a file and click Open. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1194694-06] Click Share to share a file. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1194694-07] Install the mobile app on your Android, iPhone, or iPad. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1194694-08] Install the desktop app on your PC or Mac. requires: 07
