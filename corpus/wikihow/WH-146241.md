1. [step: WH-146241-01] Purchase a replacement battery for the make and model of your computer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-146241-02] Power off your computer. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-146241-03] Unplug your computer. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-146241-04] Flip over your computer. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-146241-05] Press and hold the release latch for the battery. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-146241-06] Slide the battery out of its slot. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-146241-07] Insert a new battery into the slot. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-146241-08] Lock the battery in place. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-146241-09] Connect your laptop to an AC adapter. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-146241-10] Allow your laptop to charge. requires: 09
