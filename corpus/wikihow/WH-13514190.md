1. [step: WH-13514190-01] Press Command + Z on Mac. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13514190-02] Click Edit followed by Undo Typing… on Mac. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13514190-03] Tap the Undo button at the top on iPhone, iPad, or iCloud Web. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13514190-04] Swipe left with three fingers on iPhone and iPad. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13514190-05] Shake your iPhone or iPad, and tap Undo. requires: 04
