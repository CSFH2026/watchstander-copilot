1. [step: WH-11708748-01] Open the Fitbit app on your mobile device. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11708748-02] Tap the Today tab with the multi-dot icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11708748-03] Tap the plus (+) icon on the "Exercise" tile. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11708748-04] Tap Log Previous. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11708748-05] Tap an exercise or search for one. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11708748-06] Enter the activity details. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11708748-07] Tap Log It. requires: 06
