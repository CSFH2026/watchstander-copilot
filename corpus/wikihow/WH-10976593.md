2. [step: WH-10976593-02] Tap the money and pencil icon. requires: 01

4. [step: WH-10976593-04] Type in how much you want to pay them. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10976593-05] Tap Pay. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10976593-06] Tap the box above the green confirmation button. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10976593-07] Select your payment method and confirm the payment. requires: 06
