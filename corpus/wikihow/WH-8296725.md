1. [step: WH-8296725-01] Open your iPhone's Settings. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8296725-02] Tap Notifications in the second options group. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8296725-03] Scroll to Reminders and select it. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8296725-04] Slide the Allow Notifications button left to the Off position. requires: 03
