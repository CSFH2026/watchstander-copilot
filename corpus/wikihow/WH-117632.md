2. [step: WH-117632-02] Open the scan in an editing program. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-117632-03] Replace the photo with a new one. Keep the size and resolution. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-117632-04] Change the text fields. Try to use a similar font. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-117632-05] Print both sides of the scan on heavy cardstock. requires: 04
