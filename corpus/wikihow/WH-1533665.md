1. [step: WH-1533665-01] Label rows for Principal, Interest, Periods, and Payment. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1533665-02] Enter total value in the Principal row. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1533665-03] Enter the interest rate into the Interest row. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1533665-04] Enter the amount of remaining payments in the Periods row. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1533665-05] Click the first blank cell in the Payments row. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1533665-06] Type " =IPMT(B2, 1, B3, B1)" into the cell. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1533665-07] Press Enter. requires: 06
