2. [step: WH-8714536-02] Back up your iPhone. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8714536-03] Disable Find My iPhone. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8714536-04] Connect your iPhone to your computer. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8714536-05] Turn off your iPhone. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8714536-06] Boot your iPhone into recovery mode. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8714536-07] Select your iPhone in iTunes or Finder. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-8714536-08] Hold Alt (PC) or Option (Mac) as you click Restore. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-8714536-09] Select the IPSW file and click Open. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-8714536-10] Click Restore and follow the on-screen instructions. requires: 09
