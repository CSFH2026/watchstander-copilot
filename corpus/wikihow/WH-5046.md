2. [step: WH-5046-02] Right-click the folder. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5046-03] Click Get Info and note the size. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5046-04] Open Finder to Applications > Utilities. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5046-05] Double-click Disk Utility. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-5046-06] Click New Image. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-5046-07] Enter the details. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-5046-08] Click Create. requires: 07
