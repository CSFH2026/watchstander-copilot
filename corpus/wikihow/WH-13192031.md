1. [step: WH-13192031-01] To cross-list items from Poshmark to Facebook Marketplace, you'll need to use a cross-listing service. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13192031-02] Many cross-listing services exist, but few have free options. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13192031-03] ResellKit gives you 50 free listings per month, while Vendoo give you 5 free listings per month. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13192031-04] ResellKit automatically delists sold items from the other platform as long as you leave the app running at all times. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13192031-05] Vendoo's paid upgrade automatically delists sold items automatically and requires no app to be running. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-13192031-06] ResellKit doesn't have a subscription option, but you can purchase additional credits for more listings. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-13192031-07] Vendoo offers paid subscription options that vary depending on how many items you want to cross-list. requires: 06
