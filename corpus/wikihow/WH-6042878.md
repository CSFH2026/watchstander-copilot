1. [step: WH-6042878-01] Download and Open Kustom Live Wallpaper (KLWP). requires: none
2. [step: WH-6042878-02] Tap the Background tab at the bottom. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6042878-03] Tap Pick Image to select a background image. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6042878-04] Tap the plus (+) sign in the upper-right corner to select an object. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6042878-05] Tap an object to add to the live wallpaper. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-6042878-06] Use the menu items at the bottom to adjust the object. 7 Tap the floppy disk icon at the top to apply the live wallpaper. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-6042878-07] Tap Set Wallpaper. requires: 06
