1. [step: WH-11145810-01] Open Outlook. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11145810-02] Click the File menu. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11145810-03] Click Open & Export. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11145810-04] Click Import/Export. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11145810-05] Select Import from another program or file, and click Next. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11145810-06] Select Outlook Data File (.pst), and click Next. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11145810-07] Click Browse, select your PST, and click Next. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11145810-08] Select Import items into the same folder in. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11145810-09] Enter your Office 365 account, and click Finish. requires: 08
