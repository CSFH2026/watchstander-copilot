1. [step: WH-11291546-01] Open iTunes on your computer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11291546-02] Click Account at the top. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11291546-03] Click View My Account, and sign in. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11291546-04] Click Manage Payments next to "Payment Information.". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11291546-05] Click Add Payment. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11291546-06] Enter your payment details. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11291546-07] Click Done. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11291546-08] Hover over a payment method. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11291546-09] Click the blue, upward arrowhead to move it to the top. requires: 08
