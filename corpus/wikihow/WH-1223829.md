1. [step: WH-1223829-01] Go to https://www.youtube.com/ in a web browser or open the YouTube app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1223829-02] Sign in to your YouTube account, if needed. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1223829-03] click or tap your profile icon in the upper-right corner. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1223829-04] Tap Settings (mobile phones and tablets only only). requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1223829-05] Tap General (Android phones and tablets only). requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1223829-06] Tap "Restricted Mode'. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1223829-07] Tap the toggle switch to disable restricted mode. requires: 06
