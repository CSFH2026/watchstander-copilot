1. [step: WH-5471843-01] Swipe down from the top of the screen. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5471843-02] Swipe down again from the Quick Settings menu. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5471843-03] Tap the Auto Rotate/Portrait/Landscape icon. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5471843-04] Rotate your phone (if Auto Rotate is enabled). requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5471843-05] Rotate your phone and tap the manual rotate button (if Auto Rotate is disabled). requires: 04
