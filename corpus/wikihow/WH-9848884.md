1. [step: WH-9848884-01] Open Slack. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9848884-02] Tap the workspace icon on the top-left. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9848884-03] Tap a channel. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9848884-04] Type "#ChannelName" to mention a channel. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9848884-05] Type a "#keyword" to tag a message. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9848884-06] Search your "#keyword" to see all the tagged messages. requires: 05
