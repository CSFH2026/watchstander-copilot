1. [step: WH-12597448-01] Log in on the desktop app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12597448-02] Create a new playlist. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12597448-03] Search for the artist and click their page. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12597448-04] Click SEE DISCOGRAPHY. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12597448-05] Click the List View button at the top. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12597448-06] Click the three dots under an album. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12597448-07] Select Add to playlist and choose the playlist. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12597448-08] Repeat for all albums and singles. requires: 07
