1. [step: WH-9627986-01] Go to https://contacts.google.com/ in a web browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9627986-02] Make sure you are signed in to the Google account you use on your Android phone. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9627986-03] Hover over the contact you want to hide. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9627986-04] Click the More menu with three vertical dots to the right. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9627986-05] Click Hide from Contacts. requires: 04
