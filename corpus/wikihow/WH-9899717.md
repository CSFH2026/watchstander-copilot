1. [step: WH-9899717-01] Go to https://www.instagram.com/ in a web browser and log in (do not use the smartphone app). requires: none
2. [step: WH-9899717-02] Log in and find a post you want to embed. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9899717-03] Click or tap "⋯" in the upper-right corner above the post. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9899717-04] Click or tap Embed in the menu. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9899717-05] Click or tap Copy Embed Code. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9899717-06] Paste the embed code into a blog or website. requires: 05
