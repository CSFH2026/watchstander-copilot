1. [step: WH-1108883-01] Go to https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/block-website. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1108883-02] Click + Add to Firefox. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1108883-03] Click Add. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1108883-04] Check the box and click OK, Got It. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1108883-05] Open the menu and select Add-ons > Extensions. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1108883-06] Click the Block Site menu button and select Options. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1108883-07] Enter a web address and click Add. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1108883-08] Create a password and click Save. requires: 07
