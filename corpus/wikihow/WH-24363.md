1. [step: WH-24363-01] Select the section you want to print by clicking and dragging across it. requires: none
2. [step: WH-24363-02] Right-click a highlighted section of text and click Print on Chrome; press Ctrl+P or ⌘ Command+P on other browsers. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-24363-03] Fill out the "Print" form that appears, making sure to check the "Print Selection" or "Selection" option if possible. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-24363-04] Click Print. requires: 03
