1. [step: WH-1517596-01] Create a new document in InDesign. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1517596-02] Create a new layer for your background image. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1517596-03] Click File followed by Placed. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1517596-04] Select your background image. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1517596-05] Click and drag over your entire page. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1517596-06] Open the "Effects" menu in the "Window" menu at the top. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1517596-07] Lower the opacity of your background image to 50% or lower. requires: 06
