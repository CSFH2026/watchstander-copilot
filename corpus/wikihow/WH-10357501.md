1. [step: WH-10357501-01] Shut down your phone, tablet, or laptop. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10357501-02] Wrap a small amount of cotton around the end of a toothpick. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10357501-03] Spray short bursts of compressed air into the port. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10357501-04] Scrape the cotton-wrapped toothpick around the port's inner edges. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10357501-05] Use a very small amount of rubbing alcohol on the pad if necessary. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10357501-06] Spray the remaining dirt free with more compressed air. requires: 05
