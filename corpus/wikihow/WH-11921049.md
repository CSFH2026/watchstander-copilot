2. [step: WH-11921049-02] Connect your iPhone to your computer. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11921049-03] Click the iPhone icon. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11921049-04] Click Tones (Windows only) or if you're using Finder with Mac, make sure "Manually Manage Music, Movies, and TV shows" is enabled. requires: 03
