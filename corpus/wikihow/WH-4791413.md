1. [step: WH-4791413-01] Open the Gallery on your Android device. requires: none
2. [step: WH-4791413-02] Open the folder that contains the photos you want to move. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-4791413-03] Long-press the photo you want to move. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-4791413-04] Tap any other photos you want to move to select them. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-4791413-05] Tap the icon with three dots (⋮) in the upper-right corner. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-4791413-06] Tap Move to Album. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-4791413-07] Tap a folder with an SD card in the upper-right corner. requires: 06
