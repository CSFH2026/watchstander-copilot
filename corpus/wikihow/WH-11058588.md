1. [step: WH-11058588-01] Open the Twitch app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11058588-02] Tap a channel. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11058588-03] Tap the message box at the bottom. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11058588-04] Enter "/mods" and press Send to see the mod list. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11058588-05] Enter "/color " to change your name's color. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11058588-06] Enter "/me " to color your message. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11058588-07] Enter "/block " to block a user. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11058588-08] Enter "/disconnect" to disconnect. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11058588-09] Check out all the commands on Twitch Help. requires: 08
