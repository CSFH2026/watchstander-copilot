1. [step: WH-13166401-01] Make sure the other iPhone or Mac is within Bluetooth and Wi-Fi range. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13166401-02] Check that the receiving device has AirDrop on. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13166401-03] Open Photos on your iPhone. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13166401-04] Tap a photo you want to share. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13166401-05] Tap the blue share icon. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-13166401-06] Tap AirDrop. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-13166401-07] Tap to select the receiving device. requires: 06
