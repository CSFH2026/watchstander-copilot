1. [step: WH-11913665-01] Open your project in Google Slides. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11913665-02] Navigate to the slide you want to add audio to. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11913665-03] Click the Insert tab. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11913665-04] Click Audio. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11913665-05] Navigate to and click to select the audio file you want to use. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11913665-06] Click Select. requires: 05
