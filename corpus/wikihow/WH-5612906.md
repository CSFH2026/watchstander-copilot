2. [step: WH-5612906-02] Click Apps. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5612906-03] Click My apps. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5612906-04] Click an app you want to update. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5612906-05] Click Installed and select your Android. requires: 04
