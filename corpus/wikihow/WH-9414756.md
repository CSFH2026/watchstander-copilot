1. [step: WH-9414756-01] Open Facebook in an internet browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9414756-02] Request the desktop site in your browser. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9414756-03] Tap the downward arrow icon on the top-right. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9414756-04] Tap Settings on the menu, then Blocking on the left panel. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9414756-05] Tap Edit List next to Restricted List. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9414756-06] Tap the "X" on a restricted friend's picture to remove them. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9414756-07] Tap On This List and select Friends. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9414756-08] Select all the friends you want to restrict. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-9414756-09] Tap Finish. requires: 08
