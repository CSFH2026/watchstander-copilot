1. [step: WH-4795094-01] Download and install AxCrypt from https://www.axcrypt.net/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-4795094-02] Launch AxCrypt. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-4795094-03] Enter your email address and click Ok. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-4795094-04] Retrieve the verification code from your email and enter it in Axcrypt. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-4795094-05] Set a password you can remember and click Ok. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-4795094-06] Right-cllck a file or folder you want to encrypt. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-4795094-07] Hover over AxCrypt in the pop-up menu. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-4795094-08] Click Encrypt. requires: 07
