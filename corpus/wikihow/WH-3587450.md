1. [step: WH-3587450-01] Open iCloud and Back up all your local data. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3587450-02] Click Sign Out at the bottom. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3587450-03] Click the Windows Start button. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3587450-04] Type "Control Panel" and click Control Panel to open it. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3587450-05] Click Uninstall a program. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-3587450-06] Scroll down and click iCloud. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-3587450-07] Click Change at the top of the screen. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-3587450-08] Select "Remove" and click Next. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-3587450-09] Click Yes to confirm you want to uninstall iCloud. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-3587450-10] Click Finish. requires: 09
