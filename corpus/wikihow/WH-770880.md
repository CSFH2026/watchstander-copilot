1. [step: WH-770880-01] Open VLC. requires: none
2. [step: WH-770880-02] Click Media. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-770880-03] Click Open Disc. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-770880-04] Select No disc menus. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-770880-05] Click the down-arrow next to "Play.". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-770880-06] Click Convert. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-770880-07] Select Audio – MP3 as the output file type. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-770880-08] Click Browse. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-770880-09] Enter a file name and click Save. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-770880-10] Click Start. requires: 09
