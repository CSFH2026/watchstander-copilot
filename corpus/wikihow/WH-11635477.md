1. [step: WH-11635477-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11635477-02] Select a range of cells that includes the blank cells you want to fill. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11635477-03] Click Home and Find & Select. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11635477-04] Click Go To Special and click to select Blanks then OK. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11635477-05] Navigate to your first blank cell without changing the selection. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11635477-06] Enter "=F7". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11635477-07] Press Ctrl + Enter (Windows) or Cmd + Enter (Mac) on your keyboard. requires: 06
