1. [step: WH-6455257-01] Open a web browser and type the IP address for your router in the address bar. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6455257-02] Enter the username and password for the router. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6455257-03] Click Connected devices, Attached devices, or something similar. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6455257-04] Check the list of devices connected to your wireless network. requires: 03
