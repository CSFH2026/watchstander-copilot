1. [step: WH-5361660-01] Sign in to Yahoo Mail on your computer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5361660-02] Click the Contacts icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5361660-03] Click Lists. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5361660-04] Click Create List. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5361660-05] Enter a name for your list. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-5361660-06] Enter members from your contacts. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-5361660-07] Click Save. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-5361660-08] Add the list's name to the BCC field when emailing the group. requires: 07
