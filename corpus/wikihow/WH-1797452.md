1. [step: WH-1797452-01] Open App Store. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1797452-02] Tap your profile photo or initials. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1797452-03] Tap Update next to an app. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1797452-04] Tap UPDATE ALL to update all apps at once. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1797452-05] Manage automatic update preferences in Settings > App Store. requires: 04
