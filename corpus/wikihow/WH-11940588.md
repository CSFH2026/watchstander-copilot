1. [step: WH-11940588-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11940588-02] Select an empty cell where you want to display your quartile information. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11940588-03] Enter "=QUARTILE.INC(" or "=QUARTILE.EXC(". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11940588-04] Select the cells containing your data. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11940588-05] Enter ",1)" to finish the formula. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11940588-06] Press Enter (Windows) or Return (Mac). requires: 05
