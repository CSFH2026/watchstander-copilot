2. [step: WH-11180763-02] Click the Shape Tool icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11180763-03] Click a shape preset. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11180763-04] Click and hold the canvas to start your shape. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11180763-05] Drag and drop to end your shape. requires: 04
