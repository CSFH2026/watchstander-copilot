1. [step: WH-12528733-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12528733-02] Create a line graph with your data. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12528733-03] Create a new column next to your data set labeled "Max.". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12528733-04] Enter the following formula:=IF(B5=MAX($B$5:$B$16),B5,””)). requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12528733-05] Add the "Max" column to your chart. requires: 04
