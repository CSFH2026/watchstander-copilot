1. [step: WH-12780093-01] Open Foxit PDF Editor. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12780093-02] Open your PDF. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12780093-03] Tap Home and select Edit. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12780093-04] Tap the image icon in the editing menu at the top of your screen. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12780093-05] Tap the document to place your photo. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12780093-06] Insert a picture from your gallery or take a new picture using your camera. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12780093-07] Tap the three-dot menu icon and select Save As. requires: 06
