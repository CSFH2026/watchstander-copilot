1. [step: WH-12808339-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12808339-02] Go to the spreadsheet that contains the data for the pivot table. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12808339-03] Add or change your data. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12808339-04] Go back to the pivot table tab. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12808339-05] Select your pivot table. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12808339-06] Click Analyze tab (Windows) or PivotTable Analyze (Mac). requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12808339-07] Click Change Data Source. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12808339-08] Click Change Data Source. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-12808339-09] Select your data. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-12808339-10] Click Ok. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-12808339-11] Click Refresh. requires: 10
