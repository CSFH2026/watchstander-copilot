1. [step: WH-586802-01] Go to https://twitter.com/ and log in. requires: none
2. [step: WH-586802-02] Click your profile icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-586802-03] Click your profile name. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-586802-04] Find a tweet to track. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-586802-05] Look at the number next to Retweets to see how many people have retweeted. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-586802-06] Click Retweets to see who has retweeted. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-586802-07] Click the bar graph icon to see how many times your tweet has been seen. requires: 06
