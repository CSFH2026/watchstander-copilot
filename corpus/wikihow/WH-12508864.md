1. [step: WH-12508864-01] Open your document in Word. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12508864-02] Click where you want to add the symbol. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12508864-03] Click the Insert tab. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12508864-04] Click Symbol. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12508864-05] Click to select Wingdings 2 from the font drop-down menu. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12508864-06] Click to select the cross mark symbol and click Insert. requires: 05
