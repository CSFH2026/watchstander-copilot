1. [step: WH-13495285-01] Open the Mail app on your iPhone. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13495285-02] Tap the name of your current inbox in the upper-left corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13495285-03] Tap All Inboxes. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13495285-04] Tap "Edit' in the upper-right corner. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13495285-05] Tap Select All in the upper-left corner. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-13495285-06] Tap Mark in the lower-left corner. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-13495285-07] Tap Mark as read. requires: 06
