1. [step: WH-13071869-01] Go to http://www.paypal.com/login and log in. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13071869-02] Navigate to the PayPal buttons page. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13071869-03] Click to select the tile with Sell on Social. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13071869-04] Add a product from your Facebook shop. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13071869-05] Click Create. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-13071869-06] Click Get Link. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-13071869-07] Navigate to your Facebook Shop and click Edit on the item that you're selling. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-13071869-08] Paste the copied link in the "Checkout URL" field and click Save. requires: 07
