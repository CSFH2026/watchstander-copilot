1. [step: WH-10277024-01] Open the Voice Memos app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10277024-02] Tap the Record button. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10277024-03] Open the text you want to record. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10277024-04] Double-tap and highlight the text. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10277024-05] Tap Speak on the toolbar. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10277024-06] Open Voice Memos again. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10277024-07] Tap the Stop button. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-10277024-08] Tap Done on the bottom-right, and Save. requires: 07
