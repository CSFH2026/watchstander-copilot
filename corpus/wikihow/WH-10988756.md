1. [step: WH-10988756-01] Start a chat with a recipient. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10988756-02] Tap the camera icon and choose a video to send. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10988756-03] Drag the right side of the video timeline to the left. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10988756-04] Tap the Send icon to send that shortened beginning segment of the video. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10988756-05] Return to the video timeline and edit again to clip the next part. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10988756-06] Repeat until you've sent all segments. requires: 05
