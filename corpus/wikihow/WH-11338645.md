1. [step: WH-11338645-01] Download the PS4 Second Screen app from the Google Play Store or App Store. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11338645-02] Open the PS4 Second Screen app and tap Continue as [your name]. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11338645-03] Sign in to your PSN account. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11338645-04] Log in to your profile on your PS4. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11338645-05] Select Settings on the PS4 dynamic menu. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11338645-06] Select Mobile App Connection Settings in the PS4 Settings menu. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11338645-07] Select Add Device on the PS4. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11338645-08] Enter the 8-digit code on the Second Screen app on your mobile device. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11338645-09] Tap Second Screen on your mobile device. requires: 08
