1. [step: WH-1632548-01] Open Google Maps. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1632548-02] Tap Go. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1632548-03] Enter your starting location. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1632548-04] Tap "Choose destination" and enter your first destination. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1632548-05] Ensure that you have driving, cycling, or walking selected. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1632548-06] Tap the three-dot menu icon. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1632548-07] Tap Add stop. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1632548-08] Enter in the second destination. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1632548-09] Continue adding more stops (if needed). requires: 08
10. [step: WH-1632548-10] Tap Done and Start when you're done adding stops and are ready to go. requires: 09
