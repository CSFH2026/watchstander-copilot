1. [step: WH-12803229-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12803229-02] Click the Page Layout tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12803229-03] Click Print Tiles. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12803229-04] Enter the rows that you want to print on each page next to "Rows to repeat at top.". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12803229-05] Enter the columns that you want to print on each page next to "Columns to repeat at left.". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12803229-06] Click Ok. requires: 05
