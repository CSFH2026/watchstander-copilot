1. [step: WH-3370319-01] Switch off the Hold button. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3370319-02] Press and hold Play/Pause for about 10 seconds. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3370319-03] Lift your finger from the button when the screen turns off. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3370319-04] Switch on the Hold button to prevent accidental power-up. requires: 03
