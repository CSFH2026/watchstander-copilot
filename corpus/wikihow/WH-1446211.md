1. [step: WH-1446211-01] Open the Start Menu. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1446211-02] Click the settings icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1446211-03] Click Apps. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1446211-04] Click to select iTunes. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1446211-05] Click Uninstall. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1446211-06] Follow the on-screen prompts to complete the uninstall. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1446211-07] Uninstall the other Apple services: Apple Software Update, Apple Mobile Device Support, Bonjour, Apple Application Support (32 and/or 64 bit). requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1446211-08] Restart your computer. requires: 07
