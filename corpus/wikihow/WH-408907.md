1. [step: WH-408907-01] Right-click the Windows Start menu and click Disk Management. requires: none
2. [step: WH-408907-02] Right-click a drive and click Change Drive Letter and Paths. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-408907-03] Highlight the drive and click Remove. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-408907-04] Click Yes. requires: 03
