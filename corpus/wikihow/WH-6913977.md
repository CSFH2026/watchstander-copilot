1. [step: WH-6913977-01] Open iTunes. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6913977-02] Click File. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6913977-03] Click New and then Playlist. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6913977-04] Name the playlist. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6913977-05] Copy songs to the playlist. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-6913977-06] Insert a blank CD-R. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-6913977-07] Select your playlist and click File. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-6913977-08] Click Burn Playlist to Disc. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-6913977-09] Select Audio CD or MP3 CD. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-6913977-10] Click Burn. requires: 09
