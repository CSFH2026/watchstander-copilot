1. [step: WH-2387850-01] Open the Settings app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2387850-02] Tap Screen Time. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2387850-03] Tap Content & Privacy Restrictions. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2387850-04] Tap Turn on Screen Time and tap Continue. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2387850-05] Tap This is My iPad or This is My Child's iPad. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2387850-06] Follow the instructions to set Screen Time settings if using a child's iPad. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-2387850-07] Tap Content Restrictions. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-2387850-08] Tap Web Content. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-2387850-09] Tap Limit Adult Websites. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-2387850-10] Tap Add Website below "Never Allow". requires: 09
11. [step: WH-2387850-11] Enter the full web address for the site you want to block. requires: 10
