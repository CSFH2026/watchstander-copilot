1. [step: WH-146623-01] Wait for it to turn off on it's own. requires: none
2. [step: WH-146623-02] Cover the solar strip. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-146623-03] Press and hold "ON", "C", "CE", or "AC". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-146623-04] Press "2nd" followed by "On". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-146623-05] Press "Shift" followed by "AC". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-146623-06] Press "Shift" followed by "On". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-146623-07] Press the button combination specified in the user's manual. requires: 06
