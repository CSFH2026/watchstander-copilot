1. [step: WH-12713633-01] Open the Registry Editor. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12713633-02] Navigate to "HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\Tcpip\Parameters.". requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12713633-03] Right-click "IP Enable Router" and click Modify. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12713633-04] Change the "Value Data" field from "0" to "1.". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12713633-05] Click Ok, close the Registry Editor, and restart your computer. requires: 04
