1. [step: WH-12519912-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12519912-02] Select an empty cell where you want to display your decimal value. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12519912-03] Enter "=ABS(A1-TRUNC(A1))". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12519912-04] Press Enter (Windows) or Return (Mac). requires: 03
