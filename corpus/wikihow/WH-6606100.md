1. [step: WH-6606100-01] Open the App Store and tap Search. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6606100-02] Type "Barcode Scanner" in the search bar and tap Search. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6606100-03] Tap Get next to the Barcode Scanner app. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6606100-04] Tap the Barcode Scanner app on your home screen after it finished downloading. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6606100-05] Tap Ok to allow the Barcode Scanner app to use your camera. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-6606100-06] Hold the camera over a barcode you want to scan. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-6606100-07] Wait until it scans the barcode automatically. requires: 06
