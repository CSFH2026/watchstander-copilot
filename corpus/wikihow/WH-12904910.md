1. [step: WH-12904910-01] Go to https://snowball-io.io/ or open the app on mobile. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12904910-02] Type in a name and choose your skin (optional). requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12904910-03] Click Start to start the game. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12904910-04] Create snowballs and launch them. Create snowballs by clicking and holding on PC or tapping and holding on mobile. Let go or bump into someone else to release the snowball. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12904910-05] Avoid larger snowballs and people. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12904910-06] Stay away from red areas. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12904910-07] Finish the game and play again if you'd like. requires: 06
