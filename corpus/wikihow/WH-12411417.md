1. [step: WH-12411417-01] Download the Honey app from the Google Play Store (Android) or App Store (iOS). requires: none
2. [step: WH-12411417-02] Open Honey. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12411417-03] Tap the account tab. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12411417-04] Tap Log in or Register. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12411417-05] Tap Not a member? Join Honey. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12411417-06] Join using your Google, Facebook, PayPal, or Apple account information or tap Join with Email. requires: 05
