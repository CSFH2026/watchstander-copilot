1. [step: WH-321101-01] Click the Insert tab. requires: none
2. [step: WH-321101-02] Click Header or Footer. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-321101-03] Click a header or filter option, or select Edit Header/Footer to start from scatch. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-321101-04] Click Page Number to add automatic numbering. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-321101-05] Add text. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-321101-06] Insert dates, document info, quick parts, and/or images. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-321101-07] Click Close Header & Footer. requires: 06
