1. [step: WH-11667334-01] Go to https://unrealengine.com and log in. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11667334-02] Hover your cursor over your Epic Games account name. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11667334-03] Click Personal or Account. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11667334-04] Click Connected Accounts. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11667334-05] Click the blue Connect button under the Xbox logo. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11667334-06] Click Link Your Account. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11667334-07] Enter your Microsoft email address associated with your Xbox Live account and click Next. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11667334-08] Enter your password and click Sign In. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11667334-09] Click Yes. requires: 08
