1. [step: WH-581742-01] Back up your PC to an external drive. requires: none
2. [step: WH-581742-02] Download the installation image from archlinux.com/download. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-581742-03] Burn the image to a DVD. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-581742-04] Restart the computer. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-581742-05] Boot from the DVD. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-581742-06] Select Boot Arch Linux and press Enter. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-581742-07] Create main and swap partitions. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-581742-08] Use ″pacstrap /mnt base base-devel″ to install. requires: 07
