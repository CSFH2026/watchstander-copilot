1. [step: WH-11996030-01] Open FL Studio. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11996030-02] Click the Edit icon that looks like a pair of scissors. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11996030-03] Click the New icon that looks like a floppy disk. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11996030-04] Click Load Sample or click a suggestion from "Recent Projects". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11996030-05] Select the noise. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11996030-06] Right-click the noise-reduction tool. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11996030-07] Click the noise-reduction tool. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11996030-08] Make sure "Denoiser" is selected and "Declipper" and "Declicker" are off. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11996030-09] Set "Threshold" to 0db and "Amount" to 22. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-11996030-10] Click Preview. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-11996030-11] Click Accept. requires: 10
