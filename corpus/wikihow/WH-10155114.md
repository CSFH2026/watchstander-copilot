1. [step: WH-10155114-01] Open your project in iMovie. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10155114-02] Tap Edit. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10155114-03] Tap + and select Audio. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10155114-04] Select an audio track and tap +. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10155114-05] Tap the solid audio bar in the timeline. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10155114-06] Drag the left and right ends of the audio bar to the desired length. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10155114-07] Tap Done. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-10155114-08] Tap the sharing button and select Save Video. requires: 07
