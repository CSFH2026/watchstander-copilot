1. [step: WH-1994071-01] Press and hold the Power button to turn on the tablet. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1994071-02] Sign into (or create a) Google account. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1994071-03] Press the Home button to go to the home screen. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1994071-04] Tap an app icon to open it. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1994071-05] Tap Play Store to browse for and install new apps. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1994071-06] Swipe down from the top of the screen to see your notifications. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1994071-07] Manage your Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connections in the notification panel. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1994071-08] Use the hardware buttons to control the volume. requires: 07
