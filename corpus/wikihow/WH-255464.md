1. [step: WH-255464-01] Find the phone's carrier's domain at https://email2sms.info/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-255464-02] Open your email app or website. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-255464-03] Compose a new message. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-255464-04] Type the phone number followed by the carrier's domain into the "To" text field. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-255464-05] Write and send a message under 160 characters in length. requires: 04
