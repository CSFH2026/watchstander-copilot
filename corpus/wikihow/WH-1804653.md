1. [step: WH-1804653-01] Connect the black prove to the "COM" or "-" port. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1804653-02] Connect the red probe to the voltage "V" port. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1804653-03] Select AC or DC mode on the dial. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1804653-04] Select the appropriate voltage range you are testing. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1804653-05] Place the black probe on the negative terminal, ground, or neutral wire. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1804653-06] Place the red probe on the positive terminal or hot wire. requires: 05
