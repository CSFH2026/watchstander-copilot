1. [step: WH-12691141-01] Navigate to https://google.com in a web browser on your Mac or Windows computer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12691141-02] Enter a search into the search bar. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12691141-03] Click Videos and Tools. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12691141-04] Click the drop-down menu labeled "Any source" and select youtube.com. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12691141-05] Click the drop-down menu labeled "Any time" and select Custom range. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12691141-06] Select a date range and click Go. requires: 05
