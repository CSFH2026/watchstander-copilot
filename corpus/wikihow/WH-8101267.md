1. [step: WH-8101267-01] Open Snapchat. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8101267-02] Shoot a photo or video snap. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8101267-03] Tap the Stickers icon that resembles a sheet of paper. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8101267-04] Tap the time sticker in the stickers listed under the Star tab. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8101267-05] Tap and drag the sticker to where you want to place it. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8101267-06] Send the snap or save it to your phone. requires: 05
