1. [step: WH-316322-01] Plug the drive into a USB port. requires: none
2. [step: WH-316322-02] Open File Explorer. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-316322-03] Select the file and press Ctrl + C. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-316322-04] Double-click your flash drive. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-316322-05] Click a blank space on the drive. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-316322-06] Press Ctrl + V. requires: 05
