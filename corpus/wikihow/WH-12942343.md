1. [step: WH-12942343-01] Turn off your power supply unit and unplug it from the wall. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12942343-02] Open your computer case and disconnect all cables from your PSU. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12942343-03] Bend a paperclip into a "U" shape, then insert one end into the green pin and another end into a black pin of the PSU's 24 pin connector. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12942343-04] Plug your PSU in, turn it on, and check if the fan is moving. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12942343-05] For more extensive PSU testing, purchase a power supply testing unit, then turn off your PSU and unplug it from power. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12942343-06] Connect the PSU's 24 pin connector to the power supply testing unit, reconnect the PSU to power, and turn it back on. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12942343-07] Check that the voltage measurements are in the acceptable ranges. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12942343-08] To test your motherboard, turn on your computer, watch for lights on the motherboard, and listen for beep codes. requires: 07
