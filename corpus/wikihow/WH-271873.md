1. [step: WH-271873-01] Open Skype on a Windows computer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-271873-02] Click the person-shaped "Contacts" icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-271873-03] Search for a contact's name, email address, or phone number. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-271873-04] Select a contact, then click Type a message at the bottom of the window. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-271873-05] Enter an invitation message, then press ↵ Enter. requires: 04
