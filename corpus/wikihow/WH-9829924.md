1. [step: WH-9829924-01] Open your internet browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9829924-02] Go to my.slack.com/account/settings. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9829924-03] Scroll down and tap Deactivate your account. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9829924-04] Enter your password. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9829924-05] Tap Confirm password. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9829924-06] Tap Yes, deactivate my account. requires: 05
