1. [step: WH-10871538-01] Open eBay. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10871538-02] Click My eBay on the top-right. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10871538-03] Click the Account tab. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10871538-04] Click Personal Information on the left menu. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10871538-05] Find your card under "Financial Information.". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10871538-06] Click Remove next to the card. requires: 05
