1. [step: WH-11102797-01] Open the Gallery app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11102797-02] Open the video you want to edit. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11102797-03] Tap Edit. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11102797-04] Select Movie Maker. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11102797-05] Install and open Samsung Movie Maker. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11102797-06] Add the video you want to edit. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11102797-07] Tap the white plus-sign (+) icon. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11102797-08] Tap the Audio tab. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11102797-09] Select music to add to your video. requires: 08
