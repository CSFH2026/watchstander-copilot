1. [step: WH-270406-01] Download and install PortableApps from http://portableapps.com/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-270406-02] Run the installer and install PortableApps on your USB flash drive. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-270406-03] Click "Start.exe" on the USB flash drive. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-270406-04] Go to Apps followed by "Get More Apps" in PortableApps. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-270406-05] Click the checkbox next to the apps you want to install and click Next. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-270406-06] Launch the App from within it's folder on the USB flash drive. requires: 05
