1. [step: WH-11315440-01] Go to https://www.epicgames.com and login to your secondary account. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11315440-02] Navigate to the account page. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11315440-03] Click Connected Accounts. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11315440-04] Click Disconnect and Unlink in the tile with your secondary account's console. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11315440-05] Go to https://www.epicgames.com and login to your primary account. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11315440-06] Navigate to the account page. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11315440-07] Click Connected Accounts. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11315440-08] Click Connect next to the console you want to add to your account. requires: 07
