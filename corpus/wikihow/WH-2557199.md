1. [step: WH-2557199-01] Go to https://www.icloud.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2557199-02] Sign in with your Apple ID. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2557199-03] Click an app to use it over the web. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2557199-04] Click Photos to work with you photos. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2557199-05] Click iCloud Drive to access files. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2557199-06] Click Contacts to access your contacts. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-2557199-07] Click Calendar to view and create events. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-2557199-08] Click Find My iPhone to locate a missing phone. requires: 07
