1. [step: WH-11212471-01] Open https://www.latex-project.org. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11212471-02] Click the Get tab at the top. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11212471-03] Scroll down to the "TeX Distributions" heading. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11212471-04] Click one of the TeX links under your operating system. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11212471-05] Download and install the recommended TeX package for your system. requires: 04
