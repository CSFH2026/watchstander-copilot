1. [step: WH-1914349-01] Open Facebook. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1914349-02] Click the chat bubble-shaped "Messenger" icon in the top-right corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1914349-03] Click New Message. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1914349-04] Enter a friend's name in the "To" text box. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1914349-05] Type your message into the chat box, then press Enter. requires: 04
