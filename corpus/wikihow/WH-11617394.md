1. [step: WH-11617394-01] Connect your printer to the same Wi-Fi network as your phone. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11617394-02] Connect add your printer in the settings menu under "Connection Preferences" < "Connected Devices" < "Printing." (Android Only). requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11617394-03] Navigate to a picture you want to print. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11617394-04] Tap the icon in the upper-right corner. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11617394-05] Tap Print. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11617394-06] Tap Select Printer. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11617394-07] Tap your printer. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11617394-08] Tap the colored print icon. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11617394-09] Confirm that you want to use Cloud Print by tapping "OK.". requires: 08
