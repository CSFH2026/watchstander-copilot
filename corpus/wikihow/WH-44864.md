1. [step: WH-44864-01] Open the Windows Mail app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-44864-02] Click New Mail. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-44864-03] Enter a recipient, subject, and message. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-44864-04] Click Insert. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-44864-05] Click Pictures. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-44864-06] Open the Pictures folder. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-44864-07] Select a picture and click Attach. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-44864-08] Click Send. requires: 07
