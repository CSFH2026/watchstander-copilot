1. [step: WH-2373587-01] Open the Google News app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2373587-02] Tap For You. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2373587-03] Tap the three dots on any topic. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2373587-04] Select More stories like this to see more of this topic. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2373587-05] Tap Fewer stories like this to see less of this topic. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2373587-06] Select Hide all stories from to hide a source. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-2373587-07] Tap the Favorites tab to manage favorites. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-2373587-08] Search for a topic or source to add to Favorites. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-2373587-09] Tap the star to add it. requires: 08
