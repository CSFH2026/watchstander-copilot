1. [step: WH-11218446-01] Enable POP on your old email account. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11218446-02] Click the settings icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11218446-03] Click the Accounts and import tab and Add email account. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11218446-04] Type the old email address and choose Gmailify or POP3. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11218446-05] Sign in to your old email if prompted. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11218446-06] Click “Always use a secure connection (SSL) when retrieving mail and Label incoming messages. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11218446-07] Click Add Account. requires: 06
