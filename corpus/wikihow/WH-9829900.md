1. [step: WH-9829900-01] Create a free Apple ID. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9829900-02] Go to https://www.icloud.com. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9829900-03] Sign in with your Apple ID. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9829900-04] Click the Pages app. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9829900-05] Click the cloud icon. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9829900-06] Select the Pages file on your computer and click Open. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9829900-07] Double-click the Pages file to open it in Pages. requires: 06
