1. [step: WH-2592226-01] Go to https://www.facebook.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2592226-02] Find the non-cover photo picture that you want to save. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2592226-03] Click the picture. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2592226-04] Hover over the picture with the mouse. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2592226-05] Click Options in the bottom-right corner. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2592226-06] Click Download. requires: 05
