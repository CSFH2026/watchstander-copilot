1. [step: WH-4771567-01] Understand that Flash Player is no longer available from Adobe or supported by any major web browsers. requires: none
2. [step: WH-4771567-02] Use HTML5 instead of Flash. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-4771567-03] Browse classic Flash content at https://archive.org/details/softwarelibrary_flash. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-4771567-04] Download the BlueMaxima Flashpoint archive from https://bluemaxima.org/flashpoint/downloads/ to play classic Flash content. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-4771567-05] Download a Flash emulator called "Ruffle" from https://ruffle.rs/#. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-4771567-06] Follow the instructions on the web page to install Ruffle. requires: 05
