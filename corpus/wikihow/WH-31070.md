1. [step: WH-31070-01] Cross the longer end over the shorter end. requires: none
2. [step: WH-31070-02] Loop the long end under where the two ends cross. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-31070-03] Fold the dangling end to make a loop. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-31070-04] Drop the longer end over the skinny center of the bow. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-31070-05] Pinch the bow together in front of the long end. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-31070-06] Feed the middle of the dangling end back through the knot. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-31070-07] Pull at the loops and straighten the bow tie. requires: 06
