1. [step: WH-1051026-01] Open iTunes on a computer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1051026-02] Select a song from your library. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1051026-03] Right-click the song and select Get Info. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1051026-04] Click Options. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1051026-05] Set the start and stop times and click OK. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1051026-06] Right-click the song and select Create AAC Version. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1051026-07] Drag the file to the desktop. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1051026-08] Change the extension to ″.m4r.″. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1051026-09] Transfer the ringtone to your iPhone. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-1051026-10] Set the ringtone as your ringer. requires: 09
