2. [step: WH-7132888-02] Use HTML5 instead of Flash. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7132888-03] View classic Flash content at https://archive.org/details/softwarelibrary_flash. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7132888-04] Download BlueMaxima Flashpoint Flash archive from https://bluemaxima.org/flashpoint/downloads/. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7132888-05] Download the Ruffle Flash emulator from https://ruffle.rs/#releases. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7132888-06] Follow the instructions at https://ruffle.rs/# to install Ruffle as a browser extension or standalone app. requires: 05
