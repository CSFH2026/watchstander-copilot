2. [step: WH-565780-02] Click on the Print Layout tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-565780-03] Click the arrow in the bottom-right of the Page Setup box. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-565780-04] Change the multiple pages setting to Book fold. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-565780-05] Click OK. requires: 04
