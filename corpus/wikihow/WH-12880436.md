1. [step: WH-12880436-01] Close all open Discord windows. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12880436-02] Kill any running Discord processes. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12880436-03] Delete the "discord" and "Discord" folders from " %Appdata%" and " %LocalAppdata%.". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12880436-04] Restart your PC. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12880436-05] Download the latest installer. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12880436-06] Double-click the installer to run it. requires: 05
