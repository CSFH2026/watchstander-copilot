1. [step: WH-1491349-01] Go to https://images.google.com/ in your computer's web browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1491349-02] Click the camera icon, then select Paste image URL or Upload an image. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1491349-03] Paste in the address for your image or select an image from your computer. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1491349-04] Click Search by image or allow your image to finish uploading, then view the results. requires: 03
