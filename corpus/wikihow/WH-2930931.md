1. [step: WH-2930931-01] Tap and hold the Cydia app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2930931-02] Tap the X or Delete app. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2930931-03] Tap Delete to confirm. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2930931-04] Go to Settings > your Apple ID > iCloud. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2930931-05] Enable iCloud Backup. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2930931-06] Back up your iPhone to iCloud. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-2930931-07] Go to Settings > General. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-2930931-08] Tap Reset and follow the instructions to reset. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-2930931-09] Select Restore from iCloud Backup when prompted. requires: 08
