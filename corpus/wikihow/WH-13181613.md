1. [step: WH-13181613-01] Press the Oculus button on the right controller. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13181613-02] Select the Sharing button. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13181613-03] Select Take Photo. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13181613-04] Wait 5 seconds for a screenshot to take. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13181613-05] View your screenshots using the Files app on Oculus Quest. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-13181613-06] Share your screenshots in the Gallery using the Oculus mobile app. requires: 05
