1. [step: WH-1583330-01] Install Minecraft Forge for Windows. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1583330-02] Download a mod. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1583330-03] Extract the files from the downloaded package. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1583330-04] Copy the *.jar file from the mod folder. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1583330-05] Open Minecraft and click Launch Options. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1583330-06] Click Latest Release. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1583330-07] Open your install folder. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1583330-08] Open or create the folder called "mods.". requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1583330-09] Paste the copied *.jar into the "mods" folder. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-1583330-10] Play your mod. requires: 09
