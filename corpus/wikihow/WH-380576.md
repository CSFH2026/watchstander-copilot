1. [step: WH-380576-01] Choose an email provider like Gmail or Outlook.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-380576-02] Create an account and sign in. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-380576-03] Click the Compose or New button. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-380576-04] Enter the recipient's email address into the "To" field. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-380576-05] Enter a title into the "Subject" field. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-380576-06] Type your message into the larger typing area. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-380576-07] Click the Send button. requires: 06
