1. [step: WH-8290101-01] Open your iPhone's settings. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8290101-02] Scroll to the third group of options and tap General. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8290101-03] Scroll to the seventh group of options and tap Language & Region. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8290101-04] Scroll to the Region Formats section and tap Calendar. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8290101-05] Select your preferred format, then return to the Language & Region menu. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8290101-06] Tap Region. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8290101-07] Select your preferred region. requires: 06
