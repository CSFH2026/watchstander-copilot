1. [step: WH-543163-01] Open the Registry Editor. requires: none
2. [step: WH-543163-02] Go to HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-543163-03] Create a new key called Edge. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-543163-04] Create a new DWORD inside the key called AllowDeletingBrowserHistory. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-543163-05] Double-click the DWORD and type a zero (0) in the "Value data" box. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-543163-06] Click OK. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-543163-07] Restart Edge. requires: 06
