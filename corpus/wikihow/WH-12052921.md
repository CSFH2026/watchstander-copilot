2. [step: WH-12052921-02] Sign in to your account. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12052921-03] Click Events. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12052921-04] Hover your mouse over More and click Edit Event. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12052921-05] Edit the event. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12052921-06] Click Save. requires: 05
