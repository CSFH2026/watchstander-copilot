1. [step: WH-9518826-01] Log in to Twitter on a desktop PC or the mobile app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9518826-02] Click More in the menu to the left or tap the menu icon with three lines in the upper-left corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9518826-03] Click or tap Settings & privacy. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9518826-04] Click or tap Privacy and safety. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9518826-05] Click Content you see on PC or scroll down to "Safety" on mobile. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9518826-06] Click the checkbox or tap the toggle switch next to "Display media that may contain sensitive content.". requires: 05
