1. [step: WH-6847203-01] Drag a video to the timeline. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6847203-02] Click the video to select it in the timeline. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6847203-03] Click the Effects tab in the Project panel. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6847203-04] Click the Video Effects folder under the "Effects" tab. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6847203-05] Click Transform sub-folder. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-6847203-06] Drag the Crop tool to the video. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-6847203-07] Drag the number value next to Left, Right, Top, and Bottom in the Effects Control panel . requires: 06
