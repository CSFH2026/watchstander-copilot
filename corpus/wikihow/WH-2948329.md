1. [step: WH-2948329-01] Install the Layout app and link it to your Instagram account if necessary. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2948329-02] Open Instagram and tap the + icon at the bottom of the screen. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2948329-03] Tap the Layout icon on the right side of the screen. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2948329-04] Select up to 9 photos, then tap SAVE or NEXT. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2948329-05] Open Instagram if it doesn't open automatically, tap Next, and tap Share. requires: 04
