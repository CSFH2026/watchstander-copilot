1. [step: WH-11819346-01] Touch the Uconnect Apps button on your car's touchscreen. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11819346-02] Tap Start (if you haven't opened this app before) or scroll down and tap Uconnect Registration. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11819346-03] Select Register By Web. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11819346-04] Tap the text field with the text, "Touch to Enter Email.". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11819346-05] Type your email address and tap Ok. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11819346-06] Open the email on your computer, phone, or tablet. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11819346-07] Click or tap Click to Continue Registration in the email. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11819346-08] Fill out the registration form. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11819346-09] Tap Submit. requires: 08
