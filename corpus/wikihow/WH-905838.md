1. [step: WH-905838-01] Click Add followed by Mesh and select a shape to add. requires: none
2. [step: WH-905838-02] Click the shape to select it. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-905838-03] Use the "Move," "Rotate," and "Scale" tools to manipulate the object. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-905838-04] Use the drop-down menu in the upper-left corner to switch to Edit mode. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-905838-05] Select the Knife tool and drag a new cut in the shape and press Enter. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-905838-06] Subdivide shapes and faces by clicking Edges followed by Subdivide. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-905838-07] Use the "Move," "Rotate," and "Scale tools to move vertices, edges, and faces of a mesh. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-905838-08] Use the "Extrude" tool to extend faces of a mesh. requires: 07
