1. [step: WH-698021-01] Try another USB port. requires: none
2. [step: WH-698021-02] Try another computer. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-698021-03] Press Win + R. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-698021-04] Type "diskmgmt.msc" and press Enter. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-698021-05] Look for "Healthy" or "Unallocated" at the bottom. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-698021-06] If "Healthy," assign the drive a new letter. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-698021-07] If "Unallocated," format the drive. requires: 06
