1. [step: WH-9562192-01] Open Firefox. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9562192-02] Click ☰ in the upper-right corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9562192-03] Click Add-ons. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9562192-04] Use the search bar to search to search "Video Downloadhelper" and click on it. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9562192-05] Click Add to Firefox followed by Add to add the Video Downloadhelper extension. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9562192-06] Download and install the companion app from https://www.downloadhelper.net/install-coapp. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9562192-07] Restart Firefox. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9562192-08] Go to the website with the video you want to download in Firefox. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-9562192-09] Start playing the video. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-9562192-10] Click the icon with a red, blue, and yellow sphere in the upper-right corner. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-9562192-11] Click the download icon next to the video name. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-9562192-12] Locate the video in "C:\Users\[username]\dwhelper". requires: 11
