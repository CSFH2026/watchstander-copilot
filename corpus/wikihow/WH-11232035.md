1. [step: WH-11232035-01] Add Carl Bot to your server. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11232035-02] Create all of the roles you want to assign. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11232035-03] Drag the carl-bot role to the top of the role list. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11232035-04] Type " ?reactionrole make" into a text channel to activate Carl-bot. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11232035-05] Tell Carl-bot which channel to use. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11232035-06] Create a title and description. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11232035-07] Add each role by entering the corresponding emoji, followed by a space, and then the role name. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11232035-08] Type "done" to finish. requires: 07
