1. [step: WH-11513310-01] Undo the clips between the plastic lid and body. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11513310-02] Push the top plastic piece from the bottom of the hard drive. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11513310-03] Remove the single screw. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11513310-04] Peel up the foil at the connector. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11513310-05] Lift the drive out of the case. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11513310-06] Remove the SATA to USB Bridge. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11513310-07] Peel off the last of the foil. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11513310-08] Remove the four rubber pieces around the drive. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11513310-09] Remove the four screws. requires: 08
