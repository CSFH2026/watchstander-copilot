1. [step: WH-2260342-01] Click the plus icon (+) at the top of Chrome to open a new tab. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2260342-02] Click one of the tabs at the of the screen to switch to the tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2260342-03] Tap the icon that resembles a square with a number to view all open tabs on mobile. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2260342-04] Tap the thumbnail image of a tab on mobile to switch to that tab. requires: 03
