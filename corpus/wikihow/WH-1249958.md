1. [step: WH-1249958-01] Measure your screen diagonally from corner to corner to find the manufacturer's advertised size. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1249958-02] Measure the height and width of the TV (including the frame) to make sure it will fit on your stand or cabinet. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1249958-03] Measure how thick your TV is to find out if there is enough room on your shelf. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1249958-04] Allow a few inches on all sides or your TV for proper ventilation. requires: 03
