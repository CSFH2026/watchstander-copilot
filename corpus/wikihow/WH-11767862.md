1. [step: WH-11767862-01] Open the photo in Paint. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11767862-02] Click Resize. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11767862-03] Click to select if you want to resize your photo using percentages or pixels. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11767862-04] Change the numbers for the horizontal and vertical values. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11767862-05] Click Ok. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11767862-06] Press Ctrl + S when you're done resizing the photo. requires: 05
