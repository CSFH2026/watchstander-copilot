1. [step: WH-11753848-01] Open Instagram on your phone. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11753848-02] Tap on the camera in the top right to add to your story. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11753848-03] Take a photo of anything. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11753848-04] Tap on the scribble at the top. Select a background color by tapping on it. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11753848-05] Tap and hold the screen to fill it with the color. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11753848-06] Publish to your story. Tap Your Story. requires: 05
