1. [step: WH-12832738-01] For iPhone, disable rotation lock in Control Center and turn your phone sideways on a compatible app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12832738-02] For Android, enable auto-rotation in the Display category of Settings. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12832738-03] To rotate your home screen on Android, enable home screen rotation in the Home Settings menu. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12832738-04] For Windows Phone, disable rotation lock in Settings and turn your phone sideways. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12832738-05] If you are using an external display, rotate your phone horizontally and the display should automatically update. requires: 04
