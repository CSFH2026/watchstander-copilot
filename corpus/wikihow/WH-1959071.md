1. [step: WH-1959071-01] Go to the link you want to post. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1959071-02] Highlight the link, then press Ctrl+C or ⌘ Command+C to copy it. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1959071-03] Go to https://www.facebook.com/ and log in if necessary. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1959071-04] Click the "Make Post" text box near the top of the News Feed page. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1959071-05] Press Ctrl+V or ⌘ Command+V to paste in the link, then click Post. requires: 04
