1. [step: WH-3093312-01] Open the Phone app on your Android phone. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3093312-02] Tap the menu. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3093312-03] Tap the Blocked numbers option. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3093312-04] Tap the icon next to the number(s) you want to unblock. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3093312-05] Open the Settings app on your iPhone. 6 Tap the Phone option in the Settings menu. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-3093312-06] Tap Call Blocking & Identification. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-3093312-07] Tap the red icon with a minus sign (-) next to a blocked number. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-3093312-08] Tap Unblock next to the number at the top. requires: 07
