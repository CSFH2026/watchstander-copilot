1. [step: WH-239590-01] Scan the document you want to send. requires: none
2. [step: WH-239590-02] Open your email application or email website. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-239590-03] Compose a new email message. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-239590-04] Type the recipient's email address in the "To:" field. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-239590-05] Click the "attach files" button. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-239590-06] Locate and click the scanned document. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-239590-07] Click Open. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-239590-08] Send the message. requires: 07
