1. [step: WH-10505450-01] Back up your Chromebook. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10505450-02] Power it off. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10505450-03] Boot into recovery mode. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10505450-04] Press Ctrl + D and press Enter. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10505450-05] Press Ctrl + D to log in. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10505450-06] Open the console. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10505450-07] Type "sudo crossystem dev_boot_usb=1" and press Enter. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-10505450-08] Insert the drive and press Ctrl + U. requires: 07
