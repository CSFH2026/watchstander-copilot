1. [step: WH-1595552-01] Go to File > Print. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1595552-02] Select how many slides to print per page. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1595552-03] Click the Full Page Slides menu. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1595552-04] Select a layout from the "Handouts" section. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1595552-05] Click Edit header and footer to customize the header and footer. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1595552-06] Choose the reset of your printing preferences. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1595552-07] Click Print. requires: 06
