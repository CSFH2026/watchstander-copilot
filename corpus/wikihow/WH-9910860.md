1. [step: WH-9910860-01] Open the Settings app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9910860-02] Tap the search icon and search "emergency contacts". requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9910860-03] Open the Emergency Contacts Settngs menu. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9910860-04] Tap Medical Information. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9910860-05] Use the menu options to add your name, address, blood type, allergies, medications, blood doner status, and additional medical notes. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9910860-06] Navigate back to the previous screen. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9910860-07] Tap Add Emergency Contact. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9910860-08] Tap Contacts. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-9910860-09] Tap select a contact, or tap the menu icon in the upper-left corner. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-9910860-10] Tap Emergency contacts under groups. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-9910860-11] Tap the button to add a new contact. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-9910860-12] Enter the emergency contact's information and tap Save. requires: 11
