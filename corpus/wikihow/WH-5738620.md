1. [step: WH-5738620-01] Open Google Chrome. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5738620-02] Click the icon with three vertical dots in the upper-right corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5738620-03] Click Settings. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5738620-04] Scroll down the "Search engine" header. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5738620-05] Use the drop-down menu next to "Search engine used in address bar" to select "DuckDuckGo". requires: 04
