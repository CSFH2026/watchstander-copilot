1. [step: WH-13333143-01] Open the Facebook app on your smartphone. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13333143-02] Tap Reels on your personal or professional page. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13333143-03] Tap Create reel. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13333143-04] Tap the icon with two overlapping photographs in the lower-right corner. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13333143-05] Tap Select multiple in the upper-right corner to select multiple videos. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-13333143-06] Tap Edit in the lower-left corner to edit your video clips. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-13333143-07] Tap Next when you are finished editing your clips. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-13333143-08] Tap text, audio, and stickers to your reel. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-13333143-09] Tap Next. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-13333143-10] Add a description to your video. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-13333143-11] Tap Share now. requires: 10
