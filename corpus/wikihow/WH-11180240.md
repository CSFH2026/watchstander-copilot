1. [step: WH-11180240-01] Open https://www.icoconverter.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11180240-02] Click Choose File. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11180240-03] Select an image. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11180240-04] Drag over the image preview to crop the image. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11180240-05] Click a style(s) to select that style for the image. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11180240-06] Select the image format you want to use (i.e Icon for Windows, Favicon for websites, multi-size file). requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11180240-07] Click Convert ICO. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11180240-08] Click Download your icon(s). requires: 07
