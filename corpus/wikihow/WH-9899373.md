1. [step: WH-9899373-01] Download Samsung Smart Switch on both your phone and tablet. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9899373-02] Open Samsung Smart Switch on both your phone and tablet. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9899373-03] Tap Send data on the device you're sending data from. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9899373-04] Tap the connection type on the sending device: Wireless or Cable. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9899373-05] Tap Receive data on the device that's receiving the data. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9899373-06] Tap Galaxy/Android on the device that's receiving the data. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9899373-07] Tap the connection type on the receiving device: Wireless or Cable. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9899373-08] Select the data to copy on the receiving device. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-9899373-09] Tap the arrow pointing forward on the receiving device to continue the data transfer. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-9899373-10] Tap the checkmark when the process is finished. requires: 09
