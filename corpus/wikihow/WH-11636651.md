2. [step: WH-11636651-02] Click or tap the search bar. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11636651-03] Type "has:attachment". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11636651-04] Press Enter (Windows), Return (Mac), or the mobile equivalent. requires: 03
