1. [step: WH-1806449-01] Open Ubuntu Software Center. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1806449-02] Go to Edit > Software Sources. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1806449-03] Click the Ubuntu Software tab. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1806449-04] Check the "Software restricted by copyright or legal issues" box. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1806449-05] Search for "Pepper Flash Player.". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1806449-06] Click Install. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1806449-07] Run " sudo update-pepperflashplugin-nonfree - -install" at the prompt. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1806449-08] Restart Chromium. requires: 07
