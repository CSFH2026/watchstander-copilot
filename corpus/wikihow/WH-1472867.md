1. [step: WH-1472867-01] Open iMovie and open a project. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1472867-02] Drag and drop two video clips into the timeline at the bottom of the screen. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1472867-03] Click the Transitions tab at the top of the screen. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1472867-04] Click and drag a transition in between the two clips in the timeline. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1472867-05] Click the transition icon in between the two clips. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1472867-06] Enter how long you want the transition to last in seconds, and click Apply. requires: 05
