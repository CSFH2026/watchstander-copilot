1. [step: WH-2700373-01] Open Chrome. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2700373-02] Go to https://www.themebeta.com/chrome-theme-creator-online.html. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2700373-03] Name the theme. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2700373-04] Click Upload an Image to add a background image. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2700373-05] Click Images to add other images. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2700373-06] Click the Colors tab to add color to each element. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-2700373-07] Click the PACK tab. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-2700373-08] Click Pack and Download Zip File. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-2700373-09] Click Pack and Install. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-2700373-10] Click ADD TO CHROME. requires: 09
