2. [step: WH-11164499-02] Tap the profile icon on the bottom-right. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11164499-03] Sign in to your account. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11164499-04] Tap the "+" icon at the bottom. requires: 03

6. [step: WH-11164499-06] Enter a stream title. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11164499-07] Tap GO LIVE to start streaming. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11164499-08] Tap "X" on the top-right to stop streaming. requires: 07
