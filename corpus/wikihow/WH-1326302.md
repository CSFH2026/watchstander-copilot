1. [step: WH-1326302-01] Gather the host name, username, and password you'll need to connect. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1326302-02] Open FileZilla and enter the host details into the top panel. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1326302-03] Click Quickconnect. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1326302-04] The left panel displays the files on your computer, while the right shows files on the remote server. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1326302-05] Drag files to the right panel to upload files. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1326302-06] Drag files to the left panel to download files. requires: 05
