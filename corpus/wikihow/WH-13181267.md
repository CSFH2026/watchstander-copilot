1. [step: WH-13181267-01] Go to https://docs.google.com/ in a web browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13181267-02] Click a document to open it. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13181267-03] Select the text you want to apply the strikethrough effect to. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13181267-04] Click Format. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13181267-05] Hover your mouse over Text and click Strikethrough. requires: 04
