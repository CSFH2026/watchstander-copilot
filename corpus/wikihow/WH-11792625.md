1. [step: WH-11792625-01] Open Google Chrome on your Android. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11792625-02] Tap the 3 dots in the top right. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11792625-03] Tap Settings, then Site Settings. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11792625-04] Tap Ads. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11792625-05] Tap the switch to turn it off. requires: 04
