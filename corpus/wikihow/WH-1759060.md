1. [step: WH-1759060-01] Check your hard drive activity. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1759060-02] Note how long it takes your computer to boot. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1759060-03] Make note of program crashes. If it happens often, it may be infected. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1759060-04] Look for random popups and disappearing files. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1759060-05] Check your web browser for irregular activity. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1759060-06] Try opening the Task Manager. A virus may prevent you from opening it. requires: 05
