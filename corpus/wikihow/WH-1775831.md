1. [step: WH-1775831-01] Go to https://www.pdfescape.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1775831-02] Click Use Free. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1775831-03] Click Upload PDF to PDFescape. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1775831-04] Choose the PDF file. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1775831-05] Click Upload. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1775831-06] Click Whiteout to erase things. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1775831-07] Click Text to type. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1775831-08] Click the down-arrow to save the edited version. requires: 07
