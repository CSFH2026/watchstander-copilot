2. [step: WH-13069813-02] Tap where you want to add the image. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13069813-03] Tap Insert. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13069813-04] Tap Pictures and Photos. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13069813-05] Tap an image to select it. requires: 04
