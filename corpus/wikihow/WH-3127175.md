1. [step: WH-3127175-01] Press and hold the Home button until Siri offers to help you. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3127175-02] Say “Hey Siri” to launch the Siri interface without pressing any buttons. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3127175-03] On Apple Watches, hold your watch to your face and ask Siri a question. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3127175-04] For bluetooth headsets, press and hold the Call button. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3127175-05] If you use CarPlay, press the Voice button on your steering wheel. requires: 04
