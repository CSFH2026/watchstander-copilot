1. [step: WH-12733169-01] Take a screenshot of the video. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12733169-02] Go to https://docs.google.com/ and sign in if prompted. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12733169-03] Open the file that you want to add your video to. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12733169-04] Add a play icon to your image using the Draw tool. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12733169-05] Get the URL for the video you want to add to your Doc. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12733169-06] Right-click your image in Docs. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12733169-07] Click Link. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12733169-08] Paste the copied URL into the text field and click Apply. requires: 07
