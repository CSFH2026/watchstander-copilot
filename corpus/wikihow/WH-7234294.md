1. [step: WH-7234294-01] Open AutoCAD. requires: none
2. [step: WH-7234294-02] Click the A icon in the upper-left corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7234294-03] Click Open. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7234294-04] Use the drop-down menu at the top to select a folder containing your DWG file. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7234294-05] Click the DWG file you want to open. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7234294-06] Click Open. requires: 05
