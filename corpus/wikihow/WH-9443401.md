1. [step: WH-9443401-01] Click the row above or below where you want to insert new rows. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9443401-02] Hold the Shift key as you select the number of rows you want to insert. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9443401-03] Right-click the selection. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9443401-04] Click Insert (number) above or Insert (number) below. requires: 03
