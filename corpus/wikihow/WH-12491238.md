1. [step: WH-12491238-01] Install the Roku app on your phone or tablet. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12491238-02] Turn on your TV and Roku. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12491238-03] Open the Roku app and select your Roku. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12491238-04] Tap Home on the Roku app and select Settings. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12491238-05] Select Remotes & devices and then Set up a new device. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12491238-06] Select Remote. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12491238-07] Insert batteries into the remote. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12491238-08] Point the remote at the Roku to pair (if it's an IR remote). requires: 07
9. [step: WH-12491238-09] If it's an enhanced remote, press and hold the pairing button under the battery cover until the lights flash to pair. requires: 08
