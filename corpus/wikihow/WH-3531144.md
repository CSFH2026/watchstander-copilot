1. [step: WH-3531144-01] Press Esc while playing the game. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3531144-02] Go to Options and then Video Settings. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3531144-03] Switch "Graphics" to Fast. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3531144-04] Lower the "Render Distance" to 8 chunks or lower. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3531144-05] Switch "Clouds" to Fast or Off. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-3531144-06] Switch "Particles" to Decreased or Minimal. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-3531144-07] Turn "Entity Shadows" to Off. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-3531144-08] Turn "Smooth Lighting" to Off or Minimum. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-3531144-09] Change the game resolution to something lower. requires: 08
