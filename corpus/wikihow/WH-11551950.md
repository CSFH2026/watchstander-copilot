1. [step: WH-11551950-01] Go to https://curl.haxx.se/download.html in a web browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11551950-02] Click curl Download Wizard. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11551950-03] Click curl executable. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11551950-04] Click the drop-down under "Select Operating System" and select Windows 32 or 64. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11551950-05] Click Select. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11551950-06] Select what version you want (only available for certain downloads, like Win32). requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11551950-07] Click the arrow in the first result. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11551950-08] Click the build you want. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11551950-09] Save the downloaded file. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-11551950-10] Extract the files from the zipped folder. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-11551950-11] Open the unzipped folder and files and find "curl.exe.". requires: 10
12. [step: WH-11551950-12] Type "cmd" in the address bar of your file browser to launch a command window. requires: 11
13. [step: WH-11551950-13] Type "curl" into the command window and press Enter. requires: 12
