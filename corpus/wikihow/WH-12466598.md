1. [step: WH-12466598-01] Go to https://www.amazon.com/myh/households. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12466598-02] Invite an adult to join your household. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12466598-03] Have the other person accept your invitation. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12466598-04] Go to https://www.amazon.com/hz/mycd/digital-console/alldevices. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12466598-05] Click Content. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12466598-06] Click Show Family Library. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12466598-07] Select the Audible book you want to share. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12466598-08] Click Add to Library. requires: 07
