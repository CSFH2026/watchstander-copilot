1. [step: WH-10972436-01] Open your spreadsheet in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10972436-02] Click the Page Layout tab at the top. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10972436-03] Click Margins on the toolbar. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10972436-04] Click Custom Margins. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10972436-05] Find "Center on page" at the bottom. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10972436-06] Click and check Horizontally. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-10972436-07] Click and check Vertically. requires: 06
