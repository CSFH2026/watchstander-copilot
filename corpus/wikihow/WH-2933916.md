1. [step: WH-2933916-01] Sign in to https://calendar.google.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2933916-02] Click the gear and select Settings. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2933916-03] Click Calendars. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2933916-04] Select the calendar you want to add to Outlook. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2933916-05] Click ICAL next to "Private Address. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2933916-06] Right-click the URL and select Copy. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-2933916-07] Open Outlook and click Calendar. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-2933916-08] Click Open Calendar. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-2933916-09] Click From Internet. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-2933916-10] Paste the copied URL and click OK. requires: 09
