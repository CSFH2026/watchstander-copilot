1. [step: WH-8296152-01] Open a conversation in Messages. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8296152-02] Tap the text box. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8296152-03] Tap Return 5 times. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8296152-04] Tap 123 to open the numbers keyboard. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8296152-05] Tap #+= to open the Symbols keyboard. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8296152-06] Tap the ∙ to create the whale’s left eye. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8296152-07] Tap the _ 21 times to create the whale’s mouth. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-8296152-08] Tap the ∙ to create the whale’s right eye. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-8296152-09] Tap the arrow to send. requires: 08
