1. [step: WH-1445077-01] Download and install Ableton Live from https://www.ableton.com/en/trial/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1445077-02] Open Ableton Live. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1445077-03] Click New Live Set in the File menu to start a new project. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1445077-04] Click the icons with three lines in the upper-right corner to switch between session view and arrangement view. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1445077-05] Click and drag sound clips from the folders in the panel to the left to the tracks you want to add them to. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1445077-06] Right-click and select a track type to add a new track. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1445077-07] Click the play icon at the top to play your tracks. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1445077-08] Use an audio interface to connect a microphone or instrument. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1445077-09] Click the circle button on a track to arm it for recording. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-1445077-10] Click the circle button at the top to record. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-1445077-11] Click and drag effects from the "Audio Effects" folder in the panel to the left to add effects to a track. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-1445077-12] Switch to arrangement mode and use the controls at the bottom of each track to mix them. requires: 11
