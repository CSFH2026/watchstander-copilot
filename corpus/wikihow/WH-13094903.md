2. [step: WH-13094903-02] You'll have to open Bumble and start swiping through your matches to find the SuperSwiper. requires: 01

4. [step: WH-13094903-04] SuperSwipers will also appear in your Beeline with yellow hearts on their profiles. requires: 03
