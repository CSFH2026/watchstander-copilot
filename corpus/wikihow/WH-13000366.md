1. [step: WH-13000366-01] Open Instagram and go to the profile of the person who posted the photo. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13000366-02] Select the photo, then tap the three dots in the upper-right corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13000366-03] Choose Tag Options, then press Show On My Profile. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13000366-04] Go to your profile, refresh, and check your tagged photos to see if you were successful. requires: 03
