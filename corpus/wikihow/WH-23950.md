1. [step: WH-23950-01] Go to https://accounts.craigslist.org/login/home and sign in if prompted. requires: none
2. [step: WH-23950-02] Select a posting location in the upper-right side of the window, then click go. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-23950-03] Select a specific location, a type of posting, and a type of product or service. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-23950-04] Fill out the Craigslist ad form, then click continue. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-23950-05] Fill out any other requested information, then click publish. requires: 04
