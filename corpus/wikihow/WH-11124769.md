1. [step: WH-11124769-01] Open the My Files app and select the Images category. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11124769-02] Choose an image folder. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11124769-03] Long press on a photo to select it and then tap on any additional photos you want to move. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11124769-04] Tap the three vertical dots in the top right corner of the screen, then tap Move from the list of options. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11124769-05] Select SD card. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11124769-06] Choose a folder to move the photos to and then tap Done. requires: 05
