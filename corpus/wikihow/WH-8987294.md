1. [step: WH-8987294-01] Figure out whether you're blocked on Facebook. If you are, you're also blocked on Messenger. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8987294-02] Send a message to the person in Messenger and watch the "Sent" icon afterwards. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8987294-03] Wait a few days and check your message--did the icon change? If not, and the person's account is active, they've likely blocked you. requires: 02
