1. [step: WH-1386017-01] Open the Windows 10 Disk Management tool. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1386017-02] Convert your USB drive to NTFS if it's FAT32. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1386017-03] Right-click the USB drive and select Shrink Volume. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1386017-04] Enter the desired partition size and click Shrink. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1386017-05] Right-click the unallocated space and select New Simple Volume. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1386017-06] Adjust the partition size and click Next. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1386017-07] Enter a drive letter and click Next. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1386017-08] Choose your formatting preference sand click Next. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1386017-09] Create additional partitions as needed. requires: 08
