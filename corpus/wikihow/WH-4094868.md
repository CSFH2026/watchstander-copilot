1. [step: WH-4094868-01] Open the PDF in Chrome. requires: none
2. [step: WH-4094868-02] Press Command + P or Ctrl + P. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-4094868-03] Choose Save as PDF as the destination. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-4094868-04] Enter a page range for the new PDF. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-4094868-05] Click Save and then Save. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-4094868-06] Press Command + P or Ctrl + P. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-4094868-07] Enter the page range for the second PDF. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-4094868-08] Click Save and then Save. requires: 07
