1. [step: WH-9090731-01] Log in to https://www.twitter.com (even if you're using a phone or tablet). requires: none
2. [step: WH-9090731-02] Tap the search icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9090731-03] Type anything into the search bar and press Enter or Search. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9090731-04] Tap the three dots at the top-right. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9090731-05] Tap Advanced search. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9090731-06] Type the person's username into "From these accounts.". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9090731-07] Enter other search parameters. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9090731-08] Tap Search. requires: 07
