2. [step: WH-9779354-02] Tap and hold a file or folder. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9779354-03] Tap "⋮" on the top right. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9779354-04] Tap Zip. requires: 03

6. [step: WH-9779354-06] Tap Share. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9779354-07] Select an app. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9779354-08] Tap Send. requires: 07
