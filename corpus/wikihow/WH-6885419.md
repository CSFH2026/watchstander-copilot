1. [step: WH-6885419-01] Press "Ctrl + E" to open File Explorer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6885419-02] Right-click your USB drive. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6885419-03] Click Eject. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6885419-04] Wait for the prompt that says "Safe to remove hardware.". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6885419-05] Remove your USB drive. requires: 04
