1. [step: WH-11344333-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11344333-02] Click the cell you want to insert the object. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11344333-03] Click the Object icon in the Insert tab. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11344333-04] Click the Create from File tab. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11344333-05] Click Browse and navigate to and double-click the file. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11344333-06] Choose to "Display as icon" or not. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11344333-07] Click OK. requires: 06
