1. [step: WH-11702061-01] Open Outlook. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11702061-02] Tap the Settings icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11702061-03] Tap Signature. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11702061-04] Tap the switch to turn on or off the "Per Account" signature. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11702061-05] Tap in the text field under "Signature.". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11702061-06] Write your signature. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11702061-07] Tap the check mark icon. requires: 06
