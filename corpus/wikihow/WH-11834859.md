2. [step: WH-11834859-02] Click to download the appropriate version of Ditto. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11834859-03] Double-click the installed file (not in the app store). requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11834859-04] Run Ditto. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11834859-05] Highlight the text you want to copy and press Ctrl + C. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11834859-06] Open Ditto. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11834859-07] Double-click a pasted item in Ditto to paste it in your previous window. requires: 06
