1. [step: WH-11292851-01] Open your document. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11292851-02] Highlight the text you want to convert into subscript. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11292851-03] In Word or Notepad, press "Control" (Windows) or "Command" (Mac), and "+". requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11292851-04] In Google Docs, press "Control" (Windows) or "Command" (Mac), and ",". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11292851-05] On iPhone or iPad, download and use the "Character Pad" app. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11292851-06] On Android, download and use the "Engineering Keyboard.". requires: 05
