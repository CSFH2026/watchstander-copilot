1. [step: WH-1686438-01] Open your iPhone's Settings. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1686438-02] Scroll down and tap General. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1686438-03] Tap About. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1686438-04] Scroll down to the "Model" heading. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1686438-05] Review the model number's first letter: "M" or "P" means the iPhone is new, while "N" or "F" means it is refurbished. requires: 04
