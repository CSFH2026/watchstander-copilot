1. [step: WH-11943596-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11943596-02] Right-click an axis. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11943596-03] Click Select Data. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11943596-04] Click Edit. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11943596-05] Type the data currently in "Series Y Values" in the text field under "Series X Values.". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11943596-06] Type the data from "Series X Values" in the text field under "Series Y Values.". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11943596-07] Click Ok. requires: 06
