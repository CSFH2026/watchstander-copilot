1. [step: WH-12511285-01] Open your project in AutoCAD. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12511285-02] Click the Insert tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12511285-03] Click Set Location. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12511285-04] Click From Map. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12511285-05] Search for your location. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12511285-06] Right-click and select Drop Marker Here. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12511285-07] Click Next. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12511285-08] Set the coordinate system. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-12511285-09] Click Next and click your AutoCAD drawing space. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-12511285-10] Designate the "North" direction. requires: 09
