1. [step: WH-1081091-01] Sign into YouTube.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1081091-02] Go to http://www.youtube.com/my_videos. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1081091-03] Click the menu next to the video. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1081091-04] Click Subtitles and CC. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1081091-05] Click Add new subtitles or CC. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1081091-06] Select a language. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1081091-07] Play the video. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1081091-08] Pause where you want to insert a subtitle. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1081091-09] Enter the subtitle and select a length. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-1081091-10] Click Publish. requires: 09
