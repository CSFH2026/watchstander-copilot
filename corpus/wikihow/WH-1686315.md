1. [step: WH-1686315-01] Open Roblox Studio. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1686315-02] Insert a new block and name it "Deathblock". requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1686315-03] Right-click the deathblock and click Insert Object. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1686315-04] Insert a new script. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1686315-05] Write "function onTouch(Deathblock)" on the first line. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1686315-06] Write "local humanoid = Deathblock.Parent:FindFirstChild("Humanoid")" on the next line. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1686315-07] Write "if (humanoid ~= nil) then" on the next line. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1686315-08] Write "humanoid.Health = 0" on the next line. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1686315-09] Write "end" on the next line. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-1686315-10] Write "end" again on the next line. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-1686315-11] Write "script.Parent.Touched:connect(onTouch)" on the last line. requires: 10
