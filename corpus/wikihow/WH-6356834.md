1. [step: WH-6356834-01] Trim the excess around the border. requires: none
2. [step: WH-6356834-02] Place the smaller SIM card on top of the one you're cutting. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-6356834-03] Trace an outline around the smaller SIM. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-6356834-04] Cut around the outline. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-6356834-05] Try to put the card in the tray. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-6356834-06] File down the remaining plastic for a more secure fit. requires: 05
