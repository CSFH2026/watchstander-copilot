1. [step: WH-939842-01] Open your document in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-939842-02] Select the data in your Excel sheet that you want to copy and paste into Word. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-939842-03] Press Ctrl + C (Windows) or Cmd + C (Mac). requires: 02
4. [step: WH-939842-04] Open a document in Word. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-939842-05] Move your cursor in the Word document where you want to paste the Excel data. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-939842-06] Press Ctrl + V (Windows) or Cmd + V (Mac). requires: 05
7. [step: WH-939842-07] Choose your paste formatting. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-939842-08] Save your work. requires: 07
