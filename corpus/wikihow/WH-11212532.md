1. [step: WH-11212532-01] Install and configure Java JDK. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11212532-02] Download the Tomcat 9 installer from https://tomcat.apache.org/download-90.cgi. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11212532-03] Launch the installer. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11212532-04] Click Next, and then I Agree. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11212532-05] Select the Full install, and click Next twice. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11212532-06] Click the three-dot button to specify JRE location. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11212532-07] Select your jre folder, and click OK. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11212532-08] Click Next, and then Install. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11212532-09] Click Finish. requires: 08
