1. [step: WH-11610853-01] Open your project in Excel. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11610853-02] Press and hold Ctrl (Windows) or Cmd (Mac). requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11610853-03] Press PgDn (tab right) or PgUp (tab left). requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11610853-04] Release Ctrl (Windows) or Cmd (Mac). requires: 03
