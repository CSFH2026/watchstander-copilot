1. [step: WH-8852621-01] Open your iPhone’s Photos app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8852621-02] Tap on a photo. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8852621-03] Tap the three sliders icon. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8852621-04] Tap the dial knob icon. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8852621-05] Tap the arrow next to Color. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8852621-06] Tap Cast. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8852621-07] Swipe left to add yellow and magenta colors. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-8852621-08] Swipe right to add blue and cyan colors. requires: 07
