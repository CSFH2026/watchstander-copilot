1. [step: WH-2669732-01] Open your project in Excel or create a new project. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2669732-02] Click on the cell where you would like the answer to your division equation to appear. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2669732-03] Type = into the formula bar. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2669732-04] Enter the cell you would like to use as your numerator. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2669732-05] Type / into the formula bar. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2669732-06] Enter the cell you would like to use as your denominator. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-2669732-07] Press Enter/Return. requires: 06
