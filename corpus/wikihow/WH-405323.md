1. [step: WH-405323-01] Download DBAN from http://www.dban.org/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-405323-02] Burn the DBAN ISO file to a CD or USB drive. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-405323-03] Insert the CD or USB drive in the computer with hard drive you want to erase. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-405323-04] Boot the computer from the CD or USB drive. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-405323-05] Press Enter. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-405323-06] Select the drive(s) you want to erase and press Spacebar. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-405323-07] Press F10. requires: 06
