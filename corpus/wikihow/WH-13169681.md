1. [step: WH-13169681-01] Get the URL to the deleted video. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13169681-02] Go to https://archive.org/web/ in a web browser. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13169681-03] Paste the deleted video's URL into the address bar and click Browse History. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13169681-04] Navigate to a date when the video wasn't deleted. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13169681-05] Click a date. requires: 04
