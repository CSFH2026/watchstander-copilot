1. [step: WH-12765592-01] Go to https://www.facebook.com/ and log in. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12765592-02] Click Pages. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12765592-03] Click to select your page. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12765592-04] Click Settings. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12765592-05] Click the pencil icon next to "Page Visibility.". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12765592-06] Click to select "Page Unpublished" and click Save Changes. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12765592-07] Click Unpublish. requires: 06
