1. [step: WH-7159454-01] Go to https://www.instagram.com/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-7159454-02] Click the profile icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-7159454-03] Click Edit Profile. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-7159454-04] Click Temporarily disable my account. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-7159454-05] Choose a reason for disabling your account. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-7159454-06] Enter your Instagram password. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-7159454-07] Click Temporarily Disable Account, then click OK. requires: 06
