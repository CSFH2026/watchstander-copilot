1. [step: WH-1556651-01] Go to https://www.yahoo.com/mail and log in. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1556651-02] Click Settings. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1556651-03] Click More Settings. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1556651-04] Click the Writing email tab. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1556651-05] Click the white switch next to the account that you want to use. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1556651-06] Enter your signature's text. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1556651-07] Click Back to Inbox. requires: 06
