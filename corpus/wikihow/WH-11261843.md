1. [step: WH-11261843-01] Turn on your printer. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11261843-02] Check the LCD screen or press the maintenance button. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11261843-03] Open the printer cover. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11261843-04] Squeeze the tab on the cartridge's back, and lift it up. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11261843-05] Gently shake the new cartridge, and remove it from the package. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11261843-06] Insert the new cartridge into the printer head until it clicks. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11261843-07] Close the printer cover. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11261843-08] Check the LCD screen or press the maintenance button. requires: 07
