1. [step: WH-2952190-01] Open an image in a Photoshop, GIMP, or a photo editing app on your mobile phone. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2952190-02] Use the crop tool to remove excess background around the subject of the image. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2952190-03] Use an exposure or contrast filter or adjustment layer to lighten or darken an image. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2952190-04] Use a Saturation adjustment layer or filter to enhance the image colors. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2952190-05] Use a Color Balance adjustment layer or filter to make subtle color changes to an image. requires: 04
