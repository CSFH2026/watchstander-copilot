1. [step: WH-5492074-01] Go to https://mega.co.nz/ in a web browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5492074-02] Log in. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5492074-03] Click the icon that resembles a trash bin in the far left panel. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5492074-04] Click and drag over all the files you want to restore. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5492074-05] Click the icon with three dots (⋯) in the lower-right corner of each file. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-5492074-06] Click Restore in the drop-down menu. requires: 05
