1. [step: WH-4130547-01] Go to https://www.amazon.com/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-4130547-02] Click Account & Lists. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-4130547-03] Click Create Your Amazon Account. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-4130547-04] Enter your name in the first bar. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-4130547-05] Enter a valid email address in the second bar. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-4130547-06] Type your desired password in the third bar. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-4130547-07] Retype your password in the last bar. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-4130547-08] Click Create your account. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-4130547-09] Check your email. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-4130547-10] Look for and open the new email from Amazon.com. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-4130547-11] Note the six-digit one-time password in the email. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-4130547-12] Click back over to the registration page and enter the one-time password. requires: 11
13. [step: WH-4130547-13] Click Verify. requires: 12
