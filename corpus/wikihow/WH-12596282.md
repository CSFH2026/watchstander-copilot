1. [step: WH-12596282-01] Open Reddit. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12596282-02] Go to the subreddit on which you want to post. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12596282-03] Tap + or click Create Post. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12596282-04] Select Link or Link Post. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12596282-05] Enter a title and paste the link to the video. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12596282-06] Share your post. requires: 05
