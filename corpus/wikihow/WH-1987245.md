1. [step: WH-1987245-01] Open Twitter. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1987245-02] Click the Notifications tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1987245-03] Click the Settings icon. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1987245-04] Mute notifications from people you don't follow. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1987245-05] Return to the Notification Settings panel and click Preferences. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1987245-06] Click Email notifications. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1987245-07] Click the switch next to "Email notifications" to toggle it off. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1987245-08] Click the back arrow and select Push notifications. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1987245-09] Click the switch next to "Push notifications" to toggle it off. requires: 08
