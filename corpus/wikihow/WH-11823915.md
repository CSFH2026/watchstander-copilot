1. [step: WH-11823915-01] Make sure you have a compatible fingerprint reader (if you don't have one built-in). requires: none
2. [step: WH-11823915-02] Connect your external fingerprint reader to your computer (if you don't have one built-in). requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11823915-03] Turn on your computer (if it isn't already on). requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11823915-04] Open Settings. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11823915-05] Click Accounts. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11823915-06] Click Sign-in options. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11823915-07] Click Windows Hello Fingerprint. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11823915-08] Click Set up. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11823915-09] Follow the on-screen prompts to set up your fingerprint log-in. requires: 08
