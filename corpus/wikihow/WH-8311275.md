1. [step: WH-8311275-01] Open your iPhone's Settings. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8311275-02] Scroll to the fourth group of options and select iCloud. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8311275-03] Scroll to the Calendars option. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8311275-04] Slide the Calendars switch right to the On position. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8311275-05] Exit Settings and open your iPhone's Calendar. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8311275-06] Tap Calendars at the bottom of your screen. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8311275-07] Check All iCloud if it isn't already checked. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-8311275-08] Tap Done to view your iCloud Calendar. requires: 07
