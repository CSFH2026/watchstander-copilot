1. [step: WH-3817109-01] Install the Homebrew channel on your Wii. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3817109-02] Create a Wii flash drive. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3817109-03] Download the installation files to your SD card and plug it into your Wii. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3817109-04] Start Homebrew. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3817109-05] Follow the prompts to install IOS263, cIOSX Rev20b, and USB Loader GX. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-3817109-06] Chutdown your Wii, insert your drive, and turn the Wii back on. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-3817109-07] Select USB Loader GX. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-3817109-08] Insert a game to burn it onto your USB drive. requires: 07
