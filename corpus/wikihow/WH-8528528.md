1. [step: WH-8528528-01] Open Facebook. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8528528-02] Click the downward-facing arrow in the top right corner of the Facebook page. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8528528-03] Click your page's name. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8528528-04] Click Settings. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8528528-05] Click Page Visibility. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8528528-06] Check the box next to Unpublish Page. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8528528-07] Click Save Changes. requires: 06
