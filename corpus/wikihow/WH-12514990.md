1. [step: WH-12514990-01] Tap the roll selector to create or select a roll. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12514990-02] Tap the curved arrows to select the front or back camera. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12514990-03] Tap the lightning bolt to toggle flash on or off. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12514990-04] Swipe up and down on the dial to zoom in and out. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12514990-05] Tap the large round button to take a photo. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12514990-06] After 9 AM the next day, tap the photo icon to view your developed photo. requires: 05
