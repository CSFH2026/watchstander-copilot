1. [step: WH-834840-01] Go to https://store.steampowered.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-834840-02] Click Install Steam in the upper-right corner, and then Install Steam again on the next page. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-834840-03] Double-click the Steam install file and follow the onscreen instructions. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-834840-04] Open Steam and log in or create an account. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-834840-05] Browse the games and click a game title. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-834840-06] Scroll down and click Play game or Add to Cart. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-834840-07] Click Cart and click Purchase for myself. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-834840-08] Enter your payment information and click Continue. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-834840-09] Click the checkbox next to agree to the terms and click Purchase. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-834840-10] Click Library. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-834840-11] Click the game you want to download. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-834840-12] Click Install and follow the instructions. requires: 11
