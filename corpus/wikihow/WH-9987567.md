1. [step: WH-9987567-01] Open Google Sheets in a browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9987567-02] Click a spreadsheet. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9987567-03] Hover over the right-hand side of a column heading. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9987567-04] Click and drag the column heading to the right. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9987567-05] Hover over the bottom border of a row number. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9987567-06] Click and drag the row number downwards. requires: 05
