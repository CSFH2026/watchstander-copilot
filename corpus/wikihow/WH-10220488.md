1. [step: WH-10220488-01] Open the Verizon Cloud app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-10220488-02] Tap the Menu icon on the top-left. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-10220488-03] Tap Settings on the menu, and select Tools. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-10220488-04] Tap Content Restore. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-10220488-05] Select the content you want to restore. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-10220488-06] Tap RESTORE on the top-right. requires: 05
