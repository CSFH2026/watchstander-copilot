1. [step: WH-1014202-01] Open the command line. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1014202-02] Enter ls /etc/init.d or ls /etc/rc.d/. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1014202-03] Find the name of the service you want to restart. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1014202-04] Enter sudo systemctl restart service where service is the service name. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1014202-05] Enter your password. requires: 04
