1. [step: WH-1212100-01] Log in at https://www.roblox.com/. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1212100-02] Click Create at the top of the screen. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1212100-03] Click Edit next to your place. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1212100-04] Click an object to select it. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1212100-05] Use the Move and Rotate tools to position an object. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1212100-06] Click Toolbox in the panel at the top. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1212100-07] Click the Marketplace tab. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1212100-08] Use the search bar to search for objects you want to add to your place. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-1212100-09] Click and drag an object to add it to your place. requires: 08
