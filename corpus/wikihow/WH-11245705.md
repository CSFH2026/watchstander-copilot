1. [step: WH-11245705-01] Open your GIF in Photoshop or GIMP. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11245705-02] Select the layers you want to show your text. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11245705-03] Select the text tool. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11245705-04] Click anywhere on the canvas to start typing. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11245705-05] Type your text. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11245705-06] Save as a GIF file. requires: 05
