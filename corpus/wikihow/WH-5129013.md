1. [step: WH-5129013-01] Click the File menu and select Options. requires: none
2. [step: WH-5129013-02] Click Advanced. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-5129013-03] Select the sheet from the "Display options for this worksheet" menu. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-5129013-04] Check the box next to "Show gridlines.". requires: 03
5. [step: WH-5129013-05] Click OK. requires: 04
