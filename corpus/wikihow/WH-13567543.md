1. [step: WH-13567543-01] Open Steam. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13567543-02] Right-click MuliVersus in your game library. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13567543-03] Hover over Manage and click Browse local files. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13567543-04] Navigate to MultiVersus > Content > Paks. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13567543-05] Create a new folder called "~mods" in the "Paks" folder. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-13567543-06] Downloads a mod. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-13567543-07] Extract the mod from the zip or rar file. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-13567543-08] Copy the ".pak" file extracted from the zip or rar file to the "~mods" folder. requires: 07
