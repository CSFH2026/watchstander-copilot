1. [step: WH-1218384-01] Turn off and unplug your monitor or laptop. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1218384-02] Use a clean, soft, microfiber cloth to gently wipe the screen. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1218384-03] Dampen the cloth with a small amount of distilled water or screen cleaner. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1218384-04] Gently rub the screen in broad strokes. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1218384-05] Dry the screen with a soft microfiber cloth before turning it back on. requires: 04
