1. [step: WH-12892095-01] Open or create a new project in Google Docs. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12892095-02] Go to Insert. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12892095-03] Click Equation. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12892095-04] Click xb from the "Math operations" drop-down. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12892095-05] Type the first value. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12892095-06] Press the Tab key. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12892095-07] Type the exponent. requires: 06
