1. [step: WH-12795892-01] Log in to OneDrive or your preferred cloud storage account. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12795892-02] Click the Upload button. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12795892-03] Click Files. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12795892-04] Select the file and click Open. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12795892-05] Click the file and select Share. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12795892-06] Click Copy Link. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12795892-07] Paste the link into the email message and send. requires: 06
