1. [step: WH-12802093-01] Open the PDF in Reader. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12802093-02] Enable Page Thumbnails. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12802093-03] Click the thumbnail of the page you want to save. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12802093-04] Click File and select Print. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12802093-05] Choose the option to print or save as a PDF. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12802093-06] Click Print or Save. requires: 05
