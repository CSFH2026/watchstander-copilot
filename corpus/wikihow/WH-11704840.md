3. [step: WH-11704840-03] Open the Settings app and tap Phone. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11704840-04] Tap Call Blocking & Identification. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11704840-05] Enabled Hiya ー 1 and Hiya ー 2. requires: 04

8. [step: WH-11704840-08] Tap the toggle switch next to Block Nuisance Calls. requires: 07
