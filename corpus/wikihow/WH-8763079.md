1. [step: WH-8763079-01] Open Facebook on your browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8763079-02] Click on the Search field. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8763079-03] Search for a group. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8763079-04] Click on the group name. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8763079-05] Scroll down and click Edit or Add a Description next to "DESCRIPTION.". requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8763079-06] Enter a group description. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8763079-07] Click Save. requires: 06
