1. [step: WH-12556665-01] Open GParted. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12556665-02] Click the partition you want to resize from the menu in the upper right corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12556665-03] Right-click a partition and click Resize. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12556665-04] Click and drag the handles on either side of the bar to expand the space. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12556665-05] Click Resize/Move and Apply. requires: 04
