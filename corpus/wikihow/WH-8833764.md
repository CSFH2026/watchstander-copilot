1. [step: WH-8833764-01] Open your iPhone's Photos app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8833764-02] Tap on a photo. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8833764-03] Tap the three sliders icon at the bottom. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8833764-04] Tap the dial button at the bottom. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8833764-05] Tap the arrow icon next to Light. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8833764-06] Select Shadows. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8833764-07] Swipe left to make Shadows brighter. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-8833764-08] Swipe right to make Shadows darker. requires: 07
