1. [step: WH-3127061-01] Open the Notes app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3127061-02] Create a new note. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3127061-03] Tap the Camera icon. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3127061-04] Tap Scan document. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3127061-05] Hold your camera over the document and tap the capture button. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-3127061-06] Drag the corners of the outline to adjust the image cropping. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-3127061-07] Tap Keep scan. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-3127061-08] Repeat for additional pages. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-3127061-09] Tap Save. requires: 08
