1. [step: WH-1233353-01] Open Settings. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1233353-02] Tap Cellular. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1233353-03] Slide Cellular Data to the "On" position (if it isn't already). requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1233353-04] Tap Settings in the upper-left corner to go back to the previous menu. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1233353-05] Tap Messages. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1233353-06] Slide Send as SMS to the "On" position. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-1233353-07] Slide MMS Messaging to the "On" position. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-1233353-08] Slide Group Messaging to the "On" position. requires: 07
