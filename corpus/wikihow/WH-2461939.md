1. [step: WH-2461939-01] Dial *#06# to get your iPhone's IMEI number and write it down. requires: none
2. [step: WH-2461939-02] Go to https://www.att.com/deviceunlock/ and click Unlock your device. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-2461939-03] Fill out the form as completely as possible and click Submit. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-2461939-04] Check your email for a confirmation email from AT&T within 24 hours. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-2461939-05] Click Confirm in the confirmation email within 24 hours of receiving the email. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-2461939-06] Wait up to 48 business hours for the unlock to be processed. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-2461939-07] Back up and reset your iPhone to its factory settings. requires: 06
