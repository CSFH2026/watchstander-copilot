1. [step: WH-11354400-01] Go to the AmazonSmile Org Central page at https://org.amazon.com/ref=smi_se_saas_org_org. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11354400-02] Click Register Now. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11354400-03] Search for your organization by name of EIN. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11354400-04] Click Register next to your organization from the search results. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11354400-05] Create an Organization Administrator account. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11354400-06] Verify your email. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11354400-07] Enter your organization's bank account. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11354400-08] Verify the bank account information. requires: 07
