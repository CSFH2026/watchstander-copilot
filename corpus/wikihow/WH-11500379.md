1. [step: WH-11500379-01] Go to https://www.paypal.com/myaccount/summary and log in. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11500379-02] Click Activity. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11500379-03] Click the transaction you want to create a shipping label for. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11500379-04] Click Ship. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11500379-05] Edit the "Shipping Information" section. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11500379-06] Click Calculate Shipping Cost. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11500379-07] Click Confirm and Pay. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11500379-08] Click Print. requires: 07
