1. [step: WH-1385201-01] Measure the distance from your amplifier to your speaker, then buy enough wire. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1385201-02] Ensure that everything is unplugged. If it isn't, unplug it. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1385201-03] Split the wire down the center a few inches. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1385201-04] Insert the wires into the corresponding sockets, and fasten them into place. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1385201-05] Hide the wires or tape them to the floor. requires: 04
