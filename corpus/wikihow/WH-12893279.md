1. [step: WH-12893279-01] Open the camera app. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12893279-02] Click the circular icon (to take a still picture). requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12893279-03] Click Video (to switch to video mode). requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12893279-04] Click the record icon (to start recording). requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12893279-05] Click the record icon again (to stop recording). requires: 04
