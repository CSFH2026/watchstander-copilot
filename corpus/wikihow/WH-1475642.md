1. [step: WH-1475642-01] Convert your video to MP4. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1475642-02] Open iTunes. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1475642-03] Go to File > Add File to Library. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1475642-04] Select the video file. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1475642-05] Click the Movies area. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1475642-06] Click Home Videos. requires: 05
