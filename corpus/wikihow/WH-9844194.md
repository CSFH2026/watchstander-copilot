1. [step: WH-9844194-01] Open the Discord app. 2, Tap the Menu icon in the upper-left corner. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9844194-02] Tap a server that you own. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9844194-03] Tap the menu icon with three dots next to your server name. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9844194-04] Tap Emoji. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9844194-05] Tap Upload Emoji. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9844194-06] Tap Gallery, Files, or My Files. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9844194-07] Tap an image you want to upload. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9844194-08] Tap Upload. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-9844194-09] Tap the name next to the emoji. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-9844194-10] Enter a new name for the emoji. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-9844194-11] Tap the Camera icon in the lower-right corner. requires: 10
