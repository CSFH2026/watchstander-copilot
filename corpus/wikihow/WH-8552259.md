1. [step: WH-8552259-01] Tap and hold the Record button for more than 10 seconds (up to 60 seconds). requires: none
2. [step: WH-8552259-02] Tap the preview at the bottom-left corner. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8552259-03] Drag the handles to crop out parts you don't want. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8552259-04] Drag the scissors up to split the video at a certain point. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8552259-05] Tap separated video segments to edit them separately. requires: 04
