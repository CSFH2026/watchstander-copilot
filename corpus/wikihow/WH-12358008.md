1. [step: WH-12358008-01] Go to https://etsy.com and log in (if you're prompted). requires: none
2. [step: WH-12358008-02] Click Settings then Options and Download Data. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12358008-03] Click Download CSV. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12358008-04] Go to https://www.shopify.com/admin/apps/import-store and log in (if prompted). requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12358008-05] Click Apps and Import store. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12358008-06] Click the drop-down menu under "Import your data into Spotify" and select Etsy. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12358008-07] Click Add file and Continue Import/Import. requires: 06
