1. [step: WH-12489511-01] Go to https://anchor.fm/ and log in or create a new account. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12489511-02] Click the Money tab. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12489511-03] Click Activate Sponsorships or Activate Listener Support. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12489511-04] Follow the instructions to activate your Anchor Pay account. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12489511-05] Click the record button next to a sponsor in the Sponsorships box to record an ad for a sponsor. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12489511-06] Click Options next to "Listener Support" to create a customer Listener Support message. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12489511-07] Record the first segment of your podcast in under the Episodes tab. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12489511-08] Click Add Sponsored Segment to add an ad for a sponsor to your podcast. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-12489511-09] Publish your podcast. requires: 08
