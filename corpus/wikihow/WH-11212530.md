1. [step: WH-11212530-01] Open Settings, and tap your name at the top. requires: none
2. [step: WH-11212530-02] Tap iCloud, and slide iCloud Drive to On. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-11212530-03] Slide Viber to On. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-11212530-04] Open the Viber app. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-11212530-05] Tap More on the bottom-right. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-11212530-06] Tap Settings, select Account, and tap Viber Backup. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-11212530-07] Tap Back up now. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-11212530-08] Delete and re-install Viber. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-11212530-09] Open Viber, sign in with the same number, and tap Restore now. requires: 08
