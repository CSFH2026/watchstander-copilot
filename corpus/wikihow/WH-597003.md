1. [step: WH-597003-01] Open a PDF in Adobe Acrobat Pro DC. requires: none
2. [step: WH-597003-02] Click Add Text at the top of the page. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-597003-03] Click anywhere in the document and start typing to add a new text box. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-597003-04] Click an existing text box and start typing to edit an existing text box. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-597003-05] Use the menu options below "FORMAT" in the menu to the right to edit the format of the text. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-597003-06] Click Add Image at the top fo the page and select an image you want to add. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-597003-07] Click and drag to place the image. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-597003-08] Use the options below "Object" in the menu to the right to rotate and flip an object. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-597003-09] Click Fill & Sign in the menu to the right to add a signature. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-597003-10] Save your document in the File menu. requires: 09
