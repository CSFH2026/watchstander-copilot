1. [step: WH-1556543-01] Open or create a file in Adobe Illustrator. requires: none
2. [step: WH-1556543-02] Click on the Selection Tool. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-1556543-03] Click on the image you want to crop. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-1556543-04] Click on Crop Image. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-1556543-05] Click on and drag the crop marks in the corners and sides of the image. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-1556543-06] Click on Apply. requires: 05
