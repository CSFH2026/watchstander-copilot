1. [step: WH-3934981-01] Sign in to https://contacts.google.com. requires: none
2. [step: WH-3934981-02] Check the box next to each contact to delete. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-3934981-03] Click More or the three vertical dots. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-3934981-04] Click Delete contact(s) or Delete. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-3934981-05] Click DELETE if prompted to confirm. requires: 04
