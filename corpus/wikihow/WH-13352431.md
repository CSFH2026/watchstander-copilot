1. [step: WH-13352431-01] Sign up for an account with YouTube or Twitch. requires: none
2. [step: WH-13352431-02] Start a live stream on YouTube or Twitch. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-13352431-03] Tap the Share icon before you go Live. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-13352431-04] Tap the option to copy the link. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-13352431-05] Paste the link in the Facebook groups you want to share it with. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-13352431-06] Use Restream to stream a Facebook Live to multiple groups. requires: 05
