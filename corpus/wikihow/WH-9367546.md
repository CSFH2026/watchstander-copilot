1. [step: WH-9367546-01] Open the Soundcloud upload page in Safari. requires: none
2. [step: WH-9367546-02] Tap the blue share icon at the bottom. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-9367546-03] Swipe left on the bottom row and tap Request desktop site. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-9367546-04] Tap Upload your first track. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-9367546-05] Sign in and tap Choose a file to upload. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-9367546-06] Select Drive. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-9367546-07] Find and tap the file you want to upload. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-9367546-08] Enter a title. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-9367546-09] Tap Save. requires: 08
