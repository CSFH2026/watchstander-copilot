1. [step: WH-488769-01] Create a new Windows Installation Media from the tool at https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/software-download/windows10. requires: none
2. [step: WH-488769-02] Insert the USB Windows installation media and boot up your PC. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-488769-03] Press the stated button combination on-screen to boot into the BIOS or Boot menu. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-488769-04] Use the Boot order option or Boot menu to boot from the USB drive. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-488769-05] Select your language, country, and keyboard input and click Next. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-488769-06] Click Install Now. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-488769-07] Enter your Windows product key and click Next. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-488769-08] Select your Windows Edition and click Next. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-488769-09] Agree to the license terms and click Next. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-488769-10] Select "Custom Install" and click Next. requires: 09
11. [step: WH-488769-11] Select a drive with unallocated space and click Next. requires: 10
12. [step: WH-488769-12] Remove the USB drive when Windows is finished installing. requires: 11
13. [step: WH-488769-13] Reboot your PC and follow the instructions to complete the setup process. requires: 12
