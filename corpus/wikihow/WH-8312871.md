1. [step: WH-8312871-01] Open your iPhone's Settings. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8312871-02] Scroll to the fourth group of options and select iCloud. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8312871-03] Scroll to the Keychain option and select it. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8312871-04] Slide the iCloud Keychain switch left to the Off position. requires: 03
