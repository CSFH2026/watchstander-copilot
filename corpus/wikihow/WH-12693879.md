1. [step: WH-12693879-01] Open VLC and insert the DVD you want to rip. requires: none
2. [step: WH-12693879-02] Click Media and Convert/Save. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-12693879-03] Click the Disc tab and click DVD to select it. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-12693879-04] Click the box next to "No disc menus" to select it. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-12693879-05] Select the title and chapters you want to copy. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-12693879-06] Click Convert/Save. requires: 05
7. [step: WH-12693879-07] Click Browse, then choose a name and location for your ripped episodes and click Save. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-12693879-08] Click Start. requires: 07
