1. [step: WH-8853652-01] Open Facebook on your browser. requires: none
2. [step: WH-8853652-02] Click the Messenger icon. requires: 01
3. [step: WH-8853652-03] Click on a chat. requires: 02
4. [step: WH-8853652-04] Click their name. requires: 03
5. [step: WH-8853652-05] Click Open in Messenger. requires: 04
6. [step: WH-8853652-06] Click the arrow next to "Media, files and links.". requires: 05
7. [step: WH-8853652-07] Click Media. requires: 06
8. [step: WH-8853652-08] Find the image you want to save. requires: 07
9. [step: WH-8853652-09] Click on the image. requires: 08
10. [step: WH-8853652-10] Click the download icon that looks like two rectangles overlapping each other with an arrow pointing downward. requires: 09
