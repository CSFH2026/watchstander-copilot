1. [step: EOCC-FIRE-AFFF-01] Report the fire to the bridge, the Chief Engineer, and the DCC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FIRE-AFFF-02] Activate the bilge sprinkling for the affected machinery space at its group control valve or actuation station. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FIRE-AFFF-03] Verify that the AFFF station proportioner runs and that the concentrate tank level holds. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FIRE-AFFF-04] Back up the sprinklers with the AFFF hose reels on the fire. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FIRE-AFFF-05] Set fire boundaries and secure ventilation to the space. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FIRE-AFFF-06] If the fire is not controlled, carry out the main engine room fire procedure and use Halon 1301 with the AFFF. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-FIRE-AFFF-07] After the fire is out, leave the AFFF film undisturbed and set a reflash watch. requires: 06. danger: breaking the AFFF film lets oxygen reach hot fuel, and the fire can reflash.
8. [step: EOCC-FIRE-AFFF-08] Replenish the AFFF concentrate service tank. requires: 07
