- thrust bearing temperature high
- thrust shoe RTE reading high

- low MRG LO supply to the thrust bearing

1. [step: EOCC-SHFT-THRB-01] Verify the thrust bearing temperature from the thrust shoe RTE at the PACC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SHFT-THRB-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SHFT-THRB-03] Reduce speed on the affected shaft. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SHFT-THRB-04] Check the quantity and temperature of the MRG lube oil fed to the thrust bearing. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SHFT-THRB-05] Take samples of the lube oil and start the purifier on the MRG LO system. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SHFT-THRB-06] Operate the shaft at speeds that keep the thrust bearing temperature normal. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SHFT-THRB-07] Clean and inspect the LO strainers. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SHFT-THRB-08] When operating conditions permit, measure the thrust bearing clearance and inspect the shoes for scoring or wiping. requires: 07
