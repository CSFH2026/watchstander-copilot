- IC switchboard bus failure alarm
- gyrocompass alarm
- all ground detector lights out on the IC switchboard
- loss of power to the IC switchboard

- loss of ship's service power to the IC switchboard feeder
- power converter failure

1. [step: EOCC-ELEC-ICSW-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-ELEC-ICSW-02] Shift the IC switchboard to its alternate ship's service feeder with the transfer switch. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-ELEC-ICSW-03] Check the power supply from the main switchboard. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-ELEC-ICSW-04] Check the power supply fuses and circuit breakers. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-ELEC-ICSW-05] Check the power converter for proper operation. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-ELEC-ICSW-06] Change to the standby power converter or M-G set, if one is available. requires: 05
