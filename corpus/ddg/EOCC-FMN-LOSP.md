symptoms:
- low firemain pressure
- firemain pressure dropping
- low pressure alarm on the firemain loop
- weak stream at the fire hoses
- fire pump discharge pressure low
causes:
- fire pump failure
- too many hoses or services in use
- ruptured firemain piping
- restriction in pump suction or discharge
- partially closed valves
effects:
- reduced firefighting capability
- loss of cooling water to auxiliary machinery
- loss of flushing water

- EOCC-FMN-PUMP

1. [step: EOCC-FMN-LOSP-01] Verify low firemain pressure on the DCC firemain panel and at the fire pump discharge. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FMN-LOSP-02] Report the casualty to the DCC, the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FMN-LOSP-03] Start a standby fire pump from the DCC. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FMN-LOSP-04] Check that each running fire pump has its controller in remote and its discharge and sea suction valves open. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FMN-LOSP-05] Check the fire pump suction and discharge strainers. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FMN-LOSP-06] Look for a ruptured section, open fireplugs, or too many hoses in use, and secure services that are not needed. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-FMN-LOSP-07] Isolate a ruptured section by closing the cutout valves on each side of the break, and cross-connect or split the mains as the DCC directs. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-FMN-LOSP-08] Report firemain pressure restored to the DCC and the EOOW. requires: 07
