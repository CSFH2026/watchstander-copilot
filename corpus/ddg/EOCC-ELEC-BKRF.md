system: Electric plant
tier: taken
source: COMSCINST 3541.6A Engineering Casualty Control Exercises (Military Sealift Command, 15 May 1986), exercise E-5 "Short Circuit or Fault in Generator Main Circuit Breaker", https://web.archive.org/web/20050208220520id_/http://www.msc.navy.mil/instructions/pdf/m35416a.pdf
status: training copy, unclassified. Steps follow the action list of MSC E-5 in source order; equipment names are changed to DDG-51 names. Generated by scripts/build_ddg.py from corpus/ddg/src/electric.yaml; do not edit.
symptoms:
- flash and loud bang at the generator breaker
- arcing at the switchboard
- smell of burning insulation
- voltage drop
- fire at the generator breaker
causes:
- defective or dirty circuit breaker
- water leaking on the breaker
- insulation breakdown
- loose connections
- foreign object adrift
- arcing fault too low in current to trip the breaker
effects:
- loss of electrical power
- class C fire
- damage to the generator and switchboard
associated:
- EOCC-ELEC-SWFR
- EOCC-ELEC-LGEN
---
1. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BKRF-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BKRF-02] Open the generator circuit breaker, secure the voltage regulator, and trip the SSGTG of the affected generator. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BKRF-03] Put the standby SSGTG into service. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BKRF-04] Have CO2 or Halon extinguishers ready at the switchboard. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BKRF-05] Observe all safety precautions when the circuit breaker is replaced or repaired. requires: 04. danger: do not work on the breaker until it is isolated from every source of power.
6. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BKRF-06] Secure non-vital circuits to avoid overloading the remaining SSGTG. requires: 02
