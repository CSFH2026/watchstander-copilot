- SSGTG high turbine inlet temperature
- TIT high alarm
- SSGTG automatic shutdown on temperature
- start overtemperature shutdown
causes:
- compressor fouling or stall
- bleed air valve malfunction
- fuel control fault
- high inlet air temperature

- loss of the SSGTG
- turbine damage from overtemperature

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HTIT-01] Verify the TIT alarm or shutdown at the LOCOP and the EPCC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HTIT-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HTIT-03] If the SSGTG shut down, verify that the standby SSGTG came on line and that the remaining generators carry the load. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HTIT-04] Shed non-vital loads if the remaining generators are overloaded. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HTIT-05] Do not restart the affected SSGTG; check the bleed air valve, the fuel control, and the air inlet for the cause. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HTIT-06] Report the SSGTG ready for service to the EOOW after the cause is corrected. requires: 05
