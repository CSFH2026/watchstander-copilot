- sluggish rudder response
- steering hydraulic pressure reduced
- unable to get full rudder
- rudder slow to answer the helm
- replenishing tank level low

- air in the steering hydraulic system
- low oil level in the replenishing tank
- leaking bypass valve
- replenishing valve sticking or leaking

- loss of steerage

1. [step: EOCC-STR-PART-01] Verify the sluggish rudder response and the reduced hydraulic pressure at the steering gear gauges. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-STR-PART-02] Report the casualty to the bridge, the EOOW, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-STR-PART-03] Shift to the alternate bridge steering control, if installed. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-STR-PART-04] When the bridge directs, take local control of the steering gear in after steering. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-STR-PART-05] Ready the local hand pump for steering, if installed. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-STR-PART-06] Fill the replenishing tank and purge air from the system. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-STR-PART-07] Check the bypass valve; repair or replace it if it leaks badly. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-STR-PART-08] Check the operation of the replenishing valve; free it up and repair it as necessary. requires: 07
