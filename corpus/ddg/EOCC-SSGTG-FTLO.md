symptoms:
- SSGTG fails to light off
- SSGTG turns over but does not ignite
- fail to fire shutdown on start
- no turbine inlet temperature rise during start
causes:
- ignition malfunction
- loss of dc control power
- fuel booster pump failure
- accessory drive fuel pump failure
- governor not working
- no fuel or air-bound fuel system
- bad spray pattern at the primary fuel nozzle
- low control batteries
effects:
- SSGTG not available
- no standby generator

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FTLO-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FTLO-02] Inspect the igniter plugs; clean or renew them, and inspect their electrical connections. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FTLO-03] Inspect all electrical connections of the control circuits; check the control batteries and put them on charge. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FTLO-04] Inspect the fuel booster pump and motor for proper operation. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FTLO-05] Inspect the accessory drive fuel pump and its drive shaft. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FTLO-06] Examine the governor and its linkage for proper setting. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FTLO-07] Check the fuel service tank, strainers, and filters, and purge air from the fuel system. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FTLO-08] Only after all other causes are cleared, remove the fuel nozzle ring and renew the primary nozzle or the nozzle ring assembly. requires: 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07
9. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FTLO-09] Recharge or replace the batteries before the next start attempt. requires: 08
