symptoms:
- loss of fuel oil pressure on the SSGTG
- fuel pressure low
- SSGTG slows down or stops
- generator unable to carry load
- frequency drops
- fuel pump failure alarm
causes:
- low fuel in the service tank
- restriction in fuel piping
- fuel pump failure
- water in the fuel
- dirty fuel filters or strainers
- air in the fuel system
- remote fuel shutdown operated by accident
- faulty relief valve or pressure regulator
effects:
- reduced or lost generator output
- loss of the SSGTG

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-01] Verify low fuel pressure on the SSGTG at the LOCOP and the EPCC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-03] Start the standby SSGTG and parallel it to the bus. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-04] Shift the electrical load to the standby SSGTG. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-05] Secure the affected SSGTG if it cannot hold load; open its generator breaker, then stop the engine. requires: 04. danger: never before 04 while the affected SSGTG carries load the others cannot pick up.
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-06] Check the fuel level in the service tank. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-07] Check the valves and piping from the service tank to the SSGTG. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-08] Check the condition of the fuel pump. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-09] Check the fuel filters and strainers for restriction. requires: 08
10. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-10] Check the fuel for water. requires: 09
11. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-11] Bleed off air from the fuel system. requires: 10
12. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-12] Check the fuel pressure regulating valve. requires: 11
13. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP-13] Report the SSGTG ready for service to the EOOW after fuel pressure is restored and verified. requires: 12
