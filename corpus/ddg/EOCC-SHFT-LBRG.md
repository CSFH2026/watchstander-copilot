symptoms:
- hot line shaft bearing
- spring bearing temperature high
- smell of hot oil near the shaft
- abnormal temperature rise on a line shaft bearing
causes:
- oil disc not picking up oil
- low oil level in the bearing reservoir
- insufficient cooling
- dirt in the oil
effects:
- wiped line shaft bearing
- shaft damage
- loss of the shaft
associated:
- EOCC-SHFT-DMG
---
1. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LBRG-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LBRG-02] Reduce shaft speed. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LBRG-03] Check the oil level with the oil level rod, and check the oil disc operation through the sight cover. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LBRG-04] Flush out the bearing with clean new oil. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LBRG-05] Fill the bearing reservoir with 2190 TEP oil to the proper level. requires: 04
