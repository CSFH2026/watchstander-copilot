source: COMSCINST 3541.6A Engineering Casualty Control Exercises (Military Sealift Command, 15 May 1986), exercise D-18 "Reduction Gear Cooling Water Pump Fails", https://web.archive.org/web/20050208220520id_/http://www.msc.navy.mil/instructions/pdf/m35416a.pdf ; NAVEDTRA 14114 Gas Turbine Systems Technician (Electrical) 3/(Mechanical) 3, Volume 2 (Distribution Statement A), chapter 4 "CG and DD Seawater Systems", https://www.militarynewbie.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/NAVEDTRA-14114-Gas-Turbine-Systems-Technician-Volume-2.pdf
status: training copy, unclassified. Steps follow the action list of MSC D-18, NAVEDTRA 14114 ch 4 in source order; equipment names are changed to DDG-51 names. On a DDG-51 the reduction gear LO cooler takes seawater from the seawater service system, so the standby pump is a standby seawater service pump. Generated by scripts/build_ddg.py from corpus/ddg/src/firemain_seawater.yaml; do not edit.
symptoms:
- loss of seawater to the MRG lube oil cooler
- MRG LO cooler seawater pressure low
- MRG lube oil temperature rising
- seawater pump to the reduction gear tripped
causes:
- power failure to the pump motor
- blown fuses
- overload trip
- open circuit in the motor controller or motor
- seized pump or motor
- broken pump shaft
- restriction in the pump suction
effects:
- high MRG lube oil temperature
- reduced or lost power on the affected shaft
associated:
- EOCC-MRG-LLOP
- EOCC-SW-LSUP
---
1. [step: EOCC-SW-MRGC-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SW-MRGC-02] Reduce speed on the affected shaft until cooling water is restored. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SW-MRGC-03] Put the standby seawater service pump into service. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SW-MRGC-04] Check power to the controller of the failed pump. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SW-MRGC-05] Push the reset button and restart the pump. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SW-MRGC-06] Check the pump and motor for freeness in turning. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SW-MRGC-07] Check for an open in the circuit. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SW-MRGC-08] Operate the unaffected shaft at reduced speed until seawater pressure is restored to the affected MRG LO cooler. requires: 07
