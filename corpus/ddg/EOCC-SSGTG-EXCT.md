1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-EXCT-01] Verify the voltage swings on the EPCC and the SWBD. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-EXCT-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-EXCT-03] Shift the affected SSGTG voltage regulator to manual at the SWBD and hold rated voltage. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-EXCT-04] Start the standby SSGTG and shift the electrical load to it. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-EXCT-05] Check out the voltage regulator and the excitation system of the affected SSGTG. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-EXCT-06] Check for loose connections, shorts, or grounds. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-EXCT-07] Clean and adjust the generator and regulator according to the manufacturer's instructions. requires: 06
