symptoms:
- GTM stopped suddenly
- GTM flameout
- gas generator speed falling with no throttle change
- AUTO SHUT DOWN on the PACC
- GTM will not respond to the throttle
- unable to restart the GTM
causes:
- fuel supply obstructed
- water or air in the fuel
- main fuel control fault
- fast throttle reduction
- lube oil failure
- automatic shutdown from a protective switch
- false trip from a faulty switch
- inlet obstruction
effects:
- loss of the GTM
- reduced or lost propulsion on that shaft
- reduced maneuverability

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-FLMO-01] Verify on the PACC that the GTM has stopped, and note any auto shutdown alarm. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-FLMO-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-FLMO-03] Do not overload the other GTM on that shaft; reduce its speed if needed and tell the bridge the power now available. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-FLMO-04] Check the fuel supply to the GTM for obstruction, water, or air. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-FLMO-05] Check the main fuel control and the GTM controls. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-FLMO-06] Check the GTM lube oil system. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-FLMO-07] Check the overspeed trip, and reset or repair it. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-GTM-FLMO-08] Check the temperature and low LO pressure switches for a false trip. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-GTM-FLMO-09] Correct the cause and reset the trips before the restart. requires: 04, 05, 06, 07, 08. danger: do not restart the GTM before the cause is found and corrected.
