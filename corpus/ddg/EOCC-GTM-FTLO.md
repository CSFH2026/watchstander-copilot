symptoms:
- GTM fails to light off
- GTM turns over but does not ignite
- gas generator speed comes up on start air but no T5.4 rise
- start aborted with AUTO SHUT DOWN
- LM2500 will not light off
causes:
- igniter or ignition exciter malfunction
- loss of control power
- fuel service pump not running
- module fuel valve shut
- engine driven fuel pump failure
- main fuel control or PLA linkage fault
- air in the fuel system
- clogged fuel nozzles
effects:
- GTM not available
- reduced power on that shaft
associated:
- EOCC-GTM-NSTR
- EOCC-GTM-LFOP

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-FTLO-01] Verify on the PACC that the GTM gas generator turns on start air but T5.4 does not rise. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-FTLO-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-FTLO-03] Inspect the igniter plugs; clean or renew them, and inspect their electrical connections. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-FTLO-04] Inspect all electrical connections of the GTM control circuits and check control power. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-FTLO-05] Check that the fuel service pump runs and the module fuel valve is open. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-FTLO-06] Inspect the engine driven fuel pump and its accessory drive. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-FTLO-07] Examine the main fuel control and the PLA linkage for proper setting. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-GTM-FTLO-08] Check the fuel service tank, strainers, and filters, and purge air from the GTM fuel system with the purge valve. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-GTM-FTLO-09] Only after all other causes are cleared, renew the fuel nozzles. requires: 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08
10. [step: EOCC-GTM-FTLO-10] Motor the GTM to clear unburned fuel before the next start attempt. requires: 08. danger: do not attempt a new light off until unburned fuel is cleared from the engine.
