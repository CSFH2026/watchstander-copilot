symptoms:
- steering gear power failure alarm
- no rudder action
- rudder and rudder angle indicator do not move with the helm
- steering motors stopped
- loss of power in after steering
causes:
- power cables to after steering cut
- heavy shock from collision or explosion
- overload
- blown fuses
- tripped circuit breaker
effects:
- loss of steering control
associated:
- EOCC-STR-MOTR
- EOCC-ELEC-CPWR

1. [step: EOCC-STR-POWR-01] The helmsman reports the loss of steering to the officer of the deck; notify the EOOW and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-STR-POWR-02] Shift to the alternate steering motor if power is available. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-STR-POWR-03] Check the steering circuit breaker on the main switchboard; reset it if it has tripped. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-STR-POWR-04] Check the steering control panel for electrical power. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-STR-POWR-05] Check electric power to the standby steering motor. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-STR-POWR-06] Shift to the alternate supply cable. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-STR-POWR-07] Rig casualty power to the steering gear. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-STR-POWR-08] Operate the hand-operated hydraulic pump to the rams, if installed. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-STR-POWR-09] Rig chain hoists with suitable attachments to position the rudder. requires: 08
10. [step: EOCC-STR-POWR-10] Recommend to the bridge that shaft RPM be reduced until normal steering is restored. requires: 09
