symptoms:
- loss of chilled water to electronics
- chilled water flow low
- chilled water expansion tank level low
- chilled water temperature high
- electronics cooling alarm
causes:
- chilled water pump failure
- A/C plant trip
- chilled water leak
- low expansion tank level
- valve misalignment
effects:
- electronics overheating
- loss of combat system equipment
- loss of space cooling

1. [step: EOCC-AC-CHWL-01] Verify the chilled water alarms, flow, and temperature at the PACC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-AC-CHWL-02] Report the loss to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer, and tell the combat systems watch that electronics cooling is affected. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-AC-CHWL-03] Check that the chilled water pump runs, and start the standby pump if it has stopped. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-AC-CHWL-04] Check the chilled water expansion tank level, and look for a leak in the chilled water piping. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-AC-CHWL-05] Start the standby A/C plant if the on-line plant has tripped, and align chilled water to the loads. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-AC-CHWL-06] Report to the EOOW which electronics loads have lost cooling, so their operators can reduce or secure them. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-AC-CHWL-07] Restore normal chilled water flow and temperature, and report the plant status to the EOOW. requires: 06
