1. [step: EOCC-CRP-LHYD-01] Verify the CRP hydraulic pressure alarm at the PACC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-CRP-LHYD-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-CRP-LHYD-03] Read the HOPM pressure gauges; if all pressures are very low or zero, suspect the main relief valve or a major leak. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-CRP-LHYD-04] Verify that the electric CRP pump is running; start it if it is not. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-CRP-LHYD-05] Inspect the CRP hydraulic system, the HOPM, and the OD box for a major leak. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-CRP-LHYD-06] Isolate the leaking section if possible, and reduce speed on the shaft. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-CRP-LHYD-07] Check the sequencing valve and the main relief valve. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-CRP-LHYD-08] Check the oil level in the CRP sump and head tank, and replenish oil as needed. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-CRP-LHYD-09] If pitch control is lost, shift to manual at the OD box or set emergency pitch. requires: 08
