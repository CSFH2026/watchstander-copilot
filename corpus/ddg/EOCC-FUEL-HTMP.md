- fuel heater high temperature alarm
- fuel header temperature high
- HEATER TEMP HI
- fuel temperature above normal
causes:
- temperature-regulating valve failed open
- regulator sensing fault
- low fuel flow through the heater

- overheated fuel to the engines
- fuel vapor and fire hazard
- engine fuel control upset

1. [step: EOCC-FUEL-HTMP-01] Verify the fuel heater outlet temperature at the console and locally. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FUEL-HTMP-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FUEL-HTMP-03] Reduce or secure the heating supply to the fuel heater. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FUEL-HTMP-04] Check the temperature-regulating valve and control it manually if it has failed. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FUEL-HTMP-05] Report fuel temperature normal to the EOOW, and keep the heater in manual until the regulator is repaired. requires: 04
