symptoms:
- PT brake will not engage
- PT brake will not release
- power turbine keeps turning with the brake on
- no brake engaged indication
- brake air or oil pressure low alarm
causes:
- low brake air or oil pressure
- supply cutout valve closed
- blockage or leak in the supply line
- pressure regulator fault
- electrical control fault
- bad position indicator switch or light
- binding caliper
- weak return springs
- contaminated brake pads
- damaged brake disc
effects:
- power turbine not held for a shaft lock or a cold start
- power turbine dragged by the brake
- GTM not available
associated:
- EOCC-SHFT-LOCK

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-CLTH-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-CLTH-02] Check for sufficient brake air or oil pressure and for a supply pressure alarm at the PACC. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-CLTH-03] Try the brake on manual control; if manual control works, look for an electrical fault in the brake control. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-CLTH-04] If pressure is missing, check the pressure regulator, the supply cutout valve, and the supply line for a blockage or leak. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-CLTH-05] If there is no brake engaged indication, check the PT speed for a slowdown or stop before you suspect the indicator switch or light. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-CLTH-06] If the brake will not release or will not stop rotation, check the command signal, the actuating pressure, the caliper for binding, the return springs, the pads, and the disc. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-CLTH-07] Report the result and the PT brake status to the EOOW. requires: 06
