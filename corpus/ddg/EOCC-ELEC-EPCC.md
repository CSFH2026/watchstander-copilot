- console temperature high alarm
- no control of generators from CCS

- EPCC power supply failure

- loss of the machinery control system data link

- no remote control or monitoring of the electric plant from CCS

1. [step: EOCC-ELEC-EPCC-01] Verify the loss of EPCC control and indications in CCS. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-ELEC-EPCC-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-ELEC-EPCC-03] Station watchstanders at each main switchboard and at each on-line SSGTG. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-ELEC-EPCC-04] Shift breaker control to local at each switchboard, and verify the LOCAL CONTROL indicators. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-ELEC-EPCC-05] Monitor generator voltage, frequency, and load at the switchboards, and report them to CCS. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-ELEC-EPCC-06] Shift control back to the EPCC only after it is repaired and its indications agree with the switchboards. requires: 05
