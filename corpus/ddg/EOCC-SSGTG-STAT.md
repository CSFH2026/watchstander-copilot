- generator air temperature high
- the generator is overheating

- restricted ventilation
- generator overloaded
- generator not at design speed
- restricted cooling water to the air cooler
- short circuit or ground in the stator
- rotor rubbing the stator

- loss of electric power

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-STAT-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-STAT-02] Check the generator air passages and ventilation ducts. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-STAT-03] Check the flow of seawater through the generator air cooler. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-STAT-04] Check the cooling water inlet temperature. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-STAT-05] Check the generator for overloading. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-STAT-06] Check for a partial short circuit in one or more coils. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-STAT-07] Check for short circuits or grounds. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-STAT-08] Check the air gap and look for the rotor rubbing the stator. requires: 07
