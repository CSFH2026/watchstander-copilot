symptoms:
- LO purifier stopped
- purifier not discharging clean oil
- oil in the purifier water discharge
- purifier vibration
- LO heater temperature high
causes:
- purifier bowl dirty or unbalanced
- wrong lineup
- heater failure
- motor failure
effects:
- water and dirt stay in the MRG or CRP oil
- oil lost overboard through the water discharge
associated:
- EOCC-MRG-WTLO

1. [step: EOCC-LO-PURF-01] Verify the purifier condition locally. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-LO-PURF-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-LO-PURF-03] Secure the purifier and its heater, and close its suction and discharge valves. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-LO-PURF-04] Check the lineup to make sure the oil returns to the sump it came from. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-LO-PURF-05] Clean and inspect the purifier bowl, and check the heater and motor. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-LO-PURF-06] Restart the purifier and verify clean oil discharge with no oil at the water outlet. requires: 05
