- high head pressure on the A/C compressor

- high level on the receiver gauge
- A/C compressor trips on high pressure

- too little seawater through the condenser
- cooling water regulating valve malfunction

1. [step: EOCC-AC-HDIS-01] Verify high discharge pressure at the A/C plant gauges. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-AC-HDIS-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-AC-HDIS-03] Start the standby A/C plant and shift the chilled water load to it. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-AC-HDIS-04] Check that the seawater cooling supply to the condenser is in service. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-AC-HDIS-05] Check the condenser and the water regulating valve for enough cooling water flow; check the condenser tubes and clean them if necessary. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-AC-HDIS-06] Check the system for its designed amount of refrigerant. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-AC-HDIS-07] Pump down and purge the system to remove air or non-condensable gases. requires: 06. danger: if the relief valve lifts, refrigerant vents into the A/C machinery room; refrigerant vapor can displace the air in the space.
8. [step: EOCC-AC-HDIS-08] Set the controls so the compressor does not pull a vacuum on the system. requires: 07
