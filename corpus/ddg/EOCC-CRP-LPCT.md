symptoms:
- loss of pitch control
- pitch does not respond to the throttle lever
- pitch changes without a command
- pitch feedback lost at the PACC
- remote pitch control failed
causes:
- faulty electrohydraulic servo valve
- loss of command or feedback signal
- loose or broken cable plug
- faulty auxiliary relief valve
- faulty reducing valve
effects:
- loss of maneuvering from the bridge and the PACC
- pitch held at the last setting
associated:
- EOCC-CRP-EMPT
- EOCC-CRP-ASTN
- EOCC-CTL-PACC

1. [step: EOCC-CRP-LPCT-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW and keep the bridge informed of the bells the shaft can answer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-CRP-LPCT-02] If the normal pitch command or feedback system has failed, shift the CRP system to the alternate system. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-CRP-LPCT-03] If remote control is still lost, station personnel at the OD box and shift the two changeover valve levers on the manifold block from AUTO to MANUAL. requires: 01
4. [step: EOCC-CRP-LPCT-04] Change pitch locally with the control valve lever as the bridge orders, watching the local pitch indicator. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-CRP-LPCT-05] Check the HOPM pressure gauges to find the cause. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-CRP-LPCT-06] After the electronic control is repaired, make sure the remote pitch command matches the actual pitch, then shift both changeover levers to AUTO at the same time. requires: 05. danger: if the command and the actual pitch do not match, the pitch moves to the command as soon as the levers reach AUTO.
7. [step: EOCC-CRP-LPCT-07] If local manual control does not work, shift the changeover valves to OFF, secure the HOPM, and set emergency pitch under EOCC-CRP-EMPT. requires: 03
