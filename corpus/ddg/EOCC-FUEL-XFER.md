symptoms:
- fuel transfer pump will not run
- fuel transfer pump stopped
- unable to transfer fuel to the service tank
- service tank level falling with no transfer
causes:
- transfer pump motor or controller failure
- transfer pump mechanical failure
- loss of suction
effects:
- service tank cannot be refilled
- loss of fuel to the engines when the service tank runs low
associated:
- EOCC-FUEL-SVTK
---
1. [step: EOCC-FUEL-XFER-01] Line up the fuel purifier to supply the service tank as quickly as possible. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FUEL-XFER-02] Notify the EOOW and the Chief Engineer of the casualty. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FUEL-XFER-03] In an emergency, line up and use a hand-operated pump, if installed, to continue the transfer. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FUEL-XFER-04] At the earliest possible time, inspect and repair the fuel transfer pump. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FUEL-XFER-05] Report the results of the investigation and repairs to the Chief Engineer. requires: 04
