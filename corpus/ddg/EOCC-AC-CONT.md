symptoms:
- A/C compressor runs continuously
- chilled water temperature not coming down
- A/C plant low head pressure and high suction pressure
- A/C plant cannot keep up with the load
causes:
- low refrigerant charge
- leaking compressor suction or discharge valves
- blown head gasket
- low pressure switch not working
- leaking relief valve
- compressor overloaded
effects:
- chilled water too warm for the loads
- loss of cooling to spaces and equipment

1. [step: EOCC-AC-CONT-01] Verify that the A/C compressor runs without unloading and that chilled water temperature is not coming down. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-AC-CONT-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-AC-CONT-03] Check for the proper amount of refrigerant in the system, and add refrigerant if necessary. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-AC-CONT-04] Test the compressor valves; repair or replace them if they leak. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-AC-CONT-05] Replace the head gasket if it leaks between cylinders. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-AC-CONT-06] Adjust or replace the low pressure switch. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-AC-CONT-07] Repair the relief valve if it leaks. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-AC-CONT-08] Check the system lineup and the chilled water loads against the compressor capacity. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-AC-CONT-09] Start an additional A/C plant if required. requires: 08
