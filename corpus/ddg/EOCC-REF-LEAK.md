- refrigerant piping failure

1. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-01] Stop the spill and report it to the DCC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-02] Report the spill to the EOOW so the command can make the required spill report. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-03] Isolate the A/C machinery room. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-04] Break out equipment to retrieve injured personnel. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-05] Determine the refrigerant vapor concentration in the air, and select protective equipment and clothing based on the findings. requires: 04. danger: refrigerant vapor can displace the air in the space; a person down in the room means no one enters without the protective equipment chosen from this test.
6. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-06] Break out the spill cleanup equipment and stage it near the space. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-07] Dress out the cleanup team in the protective equipment from the spill cleanup kit. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-08] Remove injured personnel and give first aid. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-09] Contain the liquid spill, and separate contaminated materials for disposal. requires: 08
10. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-10] Clean up with absorbent pads until no liquid is visible and the vapor concentration is below 10 percent of the LEL. requires: 09
11. [step: EOCC-REF-LEAK-11] Containerize the contaminated materials, and decontaminate personnel and equipment. requires: 10
