symptoms:
- water in the fuel
- FILTER WATER HI
- water in the filter/separator sump
- fuel sample not clear and bright
- erratic fuel pressure
- fuel temperature fluctuating
- engine flames out or runs rough
causes:
- fuel contaminated with seawater
- fresh water leaking into the fuel from heating coils
- contaminated fuel received from the supply source
- service tank not stripped
effects:
- flameout of gas turbines or SSGTGs
- loss of propulsion and electrical power
- corrosion of the turbine hot section
- plugged fuel nozzles
associated:
- EOCC-FUEL-FSEP
- EOCC-GTM-FLMO
- EOCC-SSGTG-LFOP

1. [step: EOCC-FUEL-WATR-01] Shift fuel suction to the standby service tank holding uncontaminated fuel. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FUEL-WATR-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FUEL-WATR-03] Drain the water from all filters, strainers, the filter/separator sump, and the lines. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FUEL-WATR-04] Restart any engine that stopped, check its operation, and report it ready for normal operation to the EOOW and the bridge. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FUEL-WATR-05] Strip or drain the contaminated service tank and refill it with clean fuel through the fuel purifier. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FUEL-WATR-06] Sample the filter/separator discharge and test it for sediment and free water. requires: 05
