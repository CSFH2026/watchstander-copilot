1. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LOCK-01] Report to the EOOW and the bridge that the shaft must be locked, and the speed and bells available on the other shaft. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LOCK-02] Secure the GTMs on the affected shaft and set its pitch to zero. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LOCK-03] Ask the bridge to slow the ship; except at very low speed, the ship's way turns the shaft. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LOCK-04] Lock the shaft with the MRG turning gear only if it is designed or NAVSEA-approved for locking; otherwise use the prepared jury rig at a flanged coupling. requires: 03. danger: the PT brake holds only the power turbine, not the line shaft; never before the shaft has stopped turning.
5. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LOCK-05] Verify that the shaft is stopped and locked, and report the maximum speed available to the bridge. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SHFT-LOCK-06] Keep the shaft locked until repairs are complete, and unlock it under the EOSS before it is restarted. requires: 05
