symptoms:
- high vibration on the SSGTG
- SSGTG vibration alarm
- engine vibrates while coming up to speed
- abnormal noise from the SSGTG
causes:
- bleed valve inoperative or binding
- fouled compressor blading
- foreign matter in the air intake plenum
- loose foundation bolts
- worn bearings
- misalignment
effects:
- loss of the SSGTG
- structural damage to the engine

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HVIB-01] Verify the vibration reading on the LOCOP and report it to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HVIB-02] If the vibration is excessive, shut down the SSGTG at once. requires: 01. danger: excessive vibration during a start or at speed needs an immediate shutdown; do not wait for a load shift.
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HVIB-03] Start the standby SSGTG and parallel it to the bus. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HVIB-04] Shift the electrical load to the standby SSGTG and secure the affected unit. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HVIB-05] Examine the bleed valve and clean it to prevent binding; check the hose from the compressor to the bleed valve for blockage. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HVIB-06] Examine the air intake plenum and the compressor inlet for foreign matter, and remove it. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HVIB-07] Check the foundation bolts, take alignment readings, and take bearing clearances. requires: 06
