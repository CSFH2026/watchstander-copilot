- standby SSGTG did not start
- auto standby failed
- STANDBY GENERATOR NOT AVAIL
- one generator carrying the whole load after a generator trip
causes:
- standby SSGTG not set up for auto-standby
- start air not available
- start circuit or control power fault
- standby SSGTG out of commission

- one SSGTG overloaded
- load shedding
- risk of loss of all ship's service power

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-ASTB-01] Verify at the EPCC which SSGTGs are on line and the load on each. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-ASTB-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-ASTB-03] Shed non-vital loads to keep the on-line SSGTG within its rating. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-ASTB-04] Start the standby SSGTG manually from the EPCC or the SWBD, and parallel it to the bus. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-ASTB-05] If it will not start, start it locally at the LOCOP and report the cause to the EOOW. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-ASTB-06] Restore the shed loads after two SSGTGs are on line. requires: 05
