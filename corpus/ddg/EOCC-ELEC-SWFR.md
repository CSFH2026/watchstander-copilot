symptoms:
- smoke from behind the switchboard
- flames at the switchboard
- smell of burning insulation
- switchboard fire
- electrical fire at the SWBD
causes:
- short circuit in the switchboard
- loose connections arcing and overheating
- overloaded circuit with a breaker that failed to trip
- dirty switchboard
effects:
- loss of electrical power on the affected circuits
- major fire
- toxic fumes

- EOCC-FIRE-ELEC

1. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SWFR-01] Report the fire to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SWFR-02] De-energize the affected circuits and isolate the switchboard section, unless an extreme emergency needs uninterrupted power; then leave the circuits energized while the fire is put out. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SWFR-03] Shift the load to the standby supply, if one is available. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SWFR-04] Release CO2 or Halon from a portable bottle or a semi-portable hose reel and direct it at the base of the flame. requires: 03. danger: electrical fires create toxic fumes; in a confined space fight the fire only with a breathing apparatus on.
5. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SWFR-05] Wear a breathing apparatus for firefighting in a confined space. requires: 04
