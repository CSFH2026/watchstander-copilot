symptoms:
- ruptured hydraulic line to a steering ram
- hydraulic oil spraying in after steering
- ship fails to respond to the helm
- rudder angle indicator does not show rudder movement
- steering hydraulic pressure lost
causes:
- relief valve failed to lift
- causing excessive pressure and a ruptured pipe
- piping ruptured by outside force
effects:
- loss of steerage
- loss or reduced maneuverability
- damage to the steering pump or motor
associated:
- EOCC-STR-LOSS
- EOCC-STR-HRAM

1. [step: EOCC-STR-RAM-01] Report the casualty to the bridge, the EOOW, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-STR-RAM-02] Secure the affected ram. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-STR-RAM-03] Operate on one ram after securing the valves as the instructions posted in after steering direct. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-STR-RAM-04] Replenish the hydraulic oil lost and purge the system. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-STR-RAM-05] Repair or replace the relief valve and the ruptured piping. requires: 04
