1. [step: EOCC-FMN-PUMP-01] Verify the fire pump status and discharge pressure on the DCC firemain panel. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FMN-PUMP-02] Report the casualty to the DCC, the EOOW, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FMN-PUMP-03] Put a standby fire pump into service from the DCC. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FMN-PUMP-04] Close the discharge valve of the failed fire pump. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FMN-PUMP-05] Check power to the controller of the failed pump. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FMN-PUMP-06] Push the reset button and try to restart the pump. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-FMN-PUMP-07] Check the pump and motor for freeness in turning, and check for an open circuit. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-FMN-PUMP-08] Check the sea suction valve and the suction and discharge strainers. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-FMN-PUMP-09] Return the repaired pump to ready, with its controller in remote and its sea suction and discharge valves open, and report it to the DCC. requires: 08
