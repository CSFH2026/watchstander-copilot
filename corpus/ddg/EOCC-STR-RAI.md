symptoms:
- rudder angle indicator wrong
- rudder angle indicator does not match the helm
- no rudder angle indication on the bridge
- rudder angle indicator frozen
causes:
- blown fuse
- loss of power at the IC switchboard
- open circuit
- failed transmitter or indicator
- failed linkage from the rudder to the transmitter
effects:
- no remote indication of rudder position
associated:
- EOCC-ELEC-ICSW
- EOCC-STR-LOSS

1. [step: EOCC-STR-RAI-01] Report the casualty to the bridge, the EOOW, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-STR-RAI-02] Test the helm; observe the rudder post in after steering and the response of the ship. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-STR-RAI-03] Check the power supply to the rudder angle indicator system from the IC switchboard. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-STR-RAI-04] Check the mechanical operation of the indicator and transmitter. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-STR-RAI-05] Check for an open circuit. requires: 04
