symptoms:
- high power turbine inlet temperature
- TEMP HIGH on the PACC
- T5.4 high on the GTM
- PT INLET GAS TEMP HI
- GTM auto shutdown on temperature
causes:
- compressor stall
- fouled compressor
- main fuel control fault
- bleed air valve malfunction
- thermocouple fault
effects:
- turbine damage from overtemperature
- loss of the GTM
associated:
- EOCC-GTM-STAL
- EOCC-GTM-ENCT

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-HTIT-01] Verify T5.4 on the PACC PWR TURB INLET TEMP meter and compare it with the gas generator speed and the throttle setting. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-HTIT-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-HTIT-03] Reduce the throttle on the affected GTM until T5.4 is back below the alarm point. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-HTIT-04] If T5.4 keeps rising, stop the GTM. requires: 03. danger: a T5.4 that keeps rising with the throttle reduced needs a stop, not a wait.
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-HTIT-05] Check the bleed air valve position, the main fuel control, and the T5.4 thermocouple signal for the cause. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-HTIT-06] Report the GTM ready for service to the EOOW after the cause is corrected. requires: 05
