1. [step: EOCC-LO-STDP-01] Verify the filter differential pressure at the PACC and at the filter. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-LO-STDP-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-LO-STDP-03] Equalize pressure between the filter chambers with the balancing valve. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-LO-STDP-04] Shift the changeover lever to put the clean filter chamber in service. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-LO-STDP-05] Verify that MRG LO header pressure is normal. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-LO-STDP-06] Vent and drain the dirty chamber, then remove and clean the filter elements. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-LO-STDP-07] Inspect the elements and their magnets for metal flakes; if metal is found, take an LO sample and report it to the EOOW. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-LO-STDP-08] Reinstall the elements and fill the cleaned chamber with the changeover assembly so it stands by. requires: 07
