symptoms:
- major fuel oil leak
- fuel spraying in the engine room
- fuel on the deck plates
- drop in fuel pressure
- fuel leak at a strainer cover or drain valve
causes:
- rupture in fuel piping
- strainer cover or drain valve loosened by vibration
- wrong strainer changeover
- blown gasket

- fire hazard
- fire in the machinery space
- loss of fuel pressure to the engines

1. [step: EOCC-FUEL-MLEK-01] Report the leak to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FUEL-MLEK-02] Secure the fuel booster pump and the service tank suction valve feeding the leak. requires: 01. danger: this stops fuel to the gas turbines and SSGTGs on that service system; report the loss of fuel to the EOOW at once.
3. [step: EOCC-FUEL-MLEK-03] Alert the ship and ready firefighting equipment. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FUEL-MLEK-04] Correct the cause of the leak and remove the fire hazard. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FUEL-MLEK-05] If fuel pressure is lost to running engines, carry out the loss of fuel pressure procedure. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FUEL-MLEK-06] If fuel is on the deck, throw sand or absorbent on the spill to contain it and to prevent slipping. requires: 03
7. [step: EOCC-FUEL-MLEK-07] Clean up the spill before any equipment near it is restarted. requires: 06. danger: do not restart equipment near the spill until the fuel is cleaned up.
8. [step: EOCC-FUEL-MLEK-08] Put the affected service system and its equipment back in service. requires: 04, 07
