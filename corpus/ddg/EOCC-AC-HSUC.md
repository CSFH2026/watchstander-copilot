symptoms:
- A/C plant suction pressure high
- high suction pressure on the A/C compressor
- knocking noise from the A/C compressor
- liquid refrigerant flooding back to the compressor
- chilled water temperature rising
causes:
- expansion valve overfeeding
- thermal bulb loose or not insulated
- leaking suction valves or piston rings
- low pressure control switch out of adjustment
- leaking discharge valves
- faulty gauge
effects:
- serious compressor damage if liquid floods back
- loss of chilled water cooling
associated:
- EOCC-AC-CHWL

1. [step: EOCC-AC-HSUC-01] Verify high suction pressure at the A/C plant gauges and listen for knocking at the compressor. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-AC-HSUC-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-AC-HSUC-03] Start the standby A/C plant and shift the chilled water load to it. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-AC-HSUC-04] If the compressor knocks, secure it at once. requires: 03. danger: knocking means liquid refrigerant is flooding back to the compressor, which can seriously damage it.
5. [step: EOCC-AC-HSUC-05] Regulate the expansion valve; check the thermal element attachment and the insulation around the bulb. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-AC-HSUC-06] Readjust or replace the low pressure control switch. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-AC-HSUC-07] Examine the suction and discharge valves and the piston rings, and replace them if necessary. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-AC-HSUC-08] Replace any defective gauges. requires: 07
