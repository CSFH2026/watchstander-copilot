symptoms:
- SSGTG frequency hunting
- generator frequency unstable
- governor not responding to load changes
- real load oscillation between paralleled generators
- faulty governor alarm
causes:
- governor electronic control failure
- loss of power to the governor control
- low or dirty oil in the governor actuator
- binding or loose governor linkage
- wrong droop setting
effects:
- overspeed
- loss of power
- load swings between generators
associated:
- EOCC-SSGTG-OSPD
- EOCC-ELEC-FREQ

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-GOVF-01] Verify the frequency and load swings on the EPCC and the SWBD. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-GOVF-02] If the SSGTG starts to overspeed, stop it with the emergency stop control. requires: none. danger: an overspeeding SSGTG must be stopped at once, without waiting for a load shift.
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-GOVF-03] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-GOVF-04] Start the standby SSGTG and shift the electrical load to it. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-GOVF-05] Check power and fuses to the governor control, and check for an open circuit or dirty contacts. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-GOVF-06] Check the governor actuator oil level; flush and refill it with the proper oil if the oil is dirty. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-GOVF-07] Check the governor actuating linkage for binding or loose parts. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-GOVF-08] Check the droop settings and correct them according to the manufacturer's instructions. requires: 07
