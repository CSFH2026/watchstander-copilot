symptoms:
- water in MRG lube oil
- cloudy or milky lube oil sample
- purifier discharging water
- MRG sump level rising
- free water in the LO sample
causes:
- leaking tube or gasket in the MRG LO cooler
- seawater leak into the sump
- condensation
- contaminated replenishment oil
effects:
- wear and scoring of gears and bearings
- loss of lubrication
- loss of the shaft while the cooler is repaired

1. [step: EOCC-MRG-WTLO-01] Verify water in the MRG lube oil by taking a sample. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-MRG-WTLO-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-MRG-WTLO-03] Start the purifier on the MRG sump and run it until no visible water remains in the oil and no water is discharged from the purifier. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-MRG-WTLO-04] Open the LO cooler test cock and check for seawater in the oil side or oil in the seawater side. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-MRG-WTLO-05] Take samples of the lube oil to follow the water content. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-MRG-WTLO-06] If the LO cooler leaks, secure the affected shaft for repair. requires: 02, 04
7. [step: EOCC-MRG-WTLO-07] Pressure test the LO cooler; plug the leaking tube, renew the gaskets, or replace the cooler. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-MRG-WTLO-08] If the oil is badly contaminated, transfer it to the settling tank for batch purification and refill the sump with clean oil from the storage tank through the purifier. requires: 07
