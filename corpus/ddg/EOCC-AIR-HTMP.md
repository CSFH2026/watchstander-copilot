symptoms:
- air compressor high temperature
- smell of hot oil at the compressor
- intercooler relief valve lifting
- compressor oil pressure low
- compressor shut down on high air temperature
causes:
- loss of cooling water to the compressor
- dirty cooler fins or cooling fan failure
- dirty or broken intake and discharge valves
- insufficient lubrication
- unloader or control failure letting the compressor overpressurize
effects:
- reduced or lost air capacity
- danger of explosion or fire in the compressor
- loss of air-operated control systems
associated:
- EOCC-AIR-LOSP
- EOCC-SW-LSUP

1. [step: EOCC-AIR-HTMP-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-AIR-HTMP-02] Secure the affected compressor immediately. requires: 01. danger: a compressor left running at high temperature can explode or catch fire; secure it before anything else.
3. [step: EOCC-AIR-HTMP-03] Start the standby compressor and put it into service, following the manufacturer's instructions for starting an idle compressor. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-AIR-HTMP-04] Check the cooling water to the unit, and clean the coolers and water jackets if they are restricted. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-AIR-HTMP-05] Check the coolers for air leaks, and plug them if they leak. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-AIR-HTMP-06] On an air-cooled unit, clean the cooler fins and check that the cooling fan runs. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-AIR-HTMP-07] Check the intake and discharge air valves for leaks or breaks, and clean or replace them. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-AIR-HTMP-08] Check the compressor oil pressure and level. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-AIR-HTMP-09] Check the unloaders and the pressure switch control for proper operation and settings. requires: 08
