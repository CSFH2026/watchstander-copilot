1. [step: EOCC-GTM-PTOS-01] Verify the PT overspeed trip and the AUTO SHUT DOWN indication on the PACC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-PTOS-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-PTOS-03] Bring the programmed control lever for that shaft to stop or slow speed until the cause is known. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-PTOS-04] Check for a sudden loss of load on the shaft, such as a propeller, shaft, or clutch casualty, and report it to the bridge. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-PTOS-05] Check the main fuel control and its PLA linkage and fuel metering linkage for freeness. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-PTOS-06] Examine the overspeed circuits and the PT speed pickups, and check their electrical connections. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-PTOS-07] Renew any worn part and reset the overspeed protection to its proper setting. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-GTM-PTOS-08] Test the overspeed protection per PMS before the GTM is restarted. requires: 07. danger: do not restart the GTM with an untested or defective overspeed trip.
