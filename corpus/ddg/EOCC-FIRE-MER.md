1. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-01] Notify the bridge and the Chief Engineer, and reduce speed. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-02] Report the fire to the DCC and call away the repair party. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-03] Secure ventilation to the engine room. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-04] Fight the fire with the equipment the fire calls for; for a fuel or oil fire use AFFF, then PKP. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-05] Set fire boundaries, close openings in the fire area, de-energize electrical circuits in the fire area, and cool the adjacent bulkheads with water. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-06] If the fire is out of control, evacuate the space at once and muster the personnel from the space. requires: 05. danger: account for every person from the space before the Halon 1301 is released.
7. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-07] Shift propulsion and generator load to the other engine room. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-08] Secure the plant in the space from the remote station. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-09] Make all closures, verify the ventilation is secured, and release the Halon 1301. requires: 08. danger: release only after the space is evacuated and the closures are made; Halon decomposition products are lethal in large concentrations.
10. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-10] Hold the Halon in the space for the soak period, at least 15 minutes, while the boundaries stay manned. requires: 09
11. [step: EOCC-FIRE-MER-11] Reenter only in breathing apparatus after the soak period, then overhaul the fire and set a reflash watch. requires: 10. danger: do not reenter without breathing apparatus until the atmosphere is certified safe; opening the space early lets air reach hot surfaces and the fire can reflash.
