symptoms:
- unsafe condition on a running GTM
- GTM must be stopped now
- emergency stop the gas turbine
- GTM did not shut down on an alarm
causes:
- fire in the module
- overspeed or overtemperature with no automatic shutdown
- battle override blocking an automatic shutdown
- loss of lube oil pressure
effects:
- loss of the GTM
- reduced power on that shaft
- no cool-down period for the engine
associated:
- EOCC-GTM-FIRE
- EOCC-GTM-LLOP
- EOCC-GTM-HTIT

1. [step: EOCC-CTL-ESTP-01] Tell the EOOW the GTM and the unsafe condition. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-CTL-ESTP-02] Press the guarded EMER STOP push button for the GTM at the PACC. requires: 01. danger: EMER STOP skips the cool-down period; use NORM STOP/CL DN when the condition allows a normal stop.
3. [step: EOCC-CTL-ESTP-03] Verify the GTM goes to off and the gas generator speed falls. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-CTL-ESTP-04] Close the module fuel valve. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-CTL-ESTP-05] Report the shaft power now available to the bridge, and the casualty to the Chief Engineer. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-CTL-ESTP-06] Find the cause before the GTM is restarted, and report it to the EOOW. requires: 05
