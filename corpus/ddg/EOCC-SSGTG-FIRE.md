1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FIRE-01] Verify the fire alarm and the CO2 release alarm at the EPCC and the DCC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FIRE-02] Report the fire to the EOOW, the bridge, the DCC, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FIRE-03] Verify that the SSGTG stopped, its fuel valve closed, its cooling fan stopped, and its fire dampers closed. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FIRE-04] Verify that the standby SSGTG is on line and carries the load. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FIRE-05] If the primary CO2 bank did not release, release it manually at the bank or from the pull box. requires: 04. danger: CO2 is a suffocation hazard; make sure no one is in the enclosure, and keep the enclosure doors shut.
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FIRE-06] Keep the enclosure doors closed; CO2 discharge cannot be stopped once started. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FIRE-07] If the fire is not out, release the secondary CO2 bank manually at the bank. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FIRE-08] Set fire boundaries around the space and report the status to the DCC. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-FIRE-09] Do not open the enclosure until the fire is out, the enclosure has cooled, and the atmosphere is tested. requires: 08. danger: opening the enclosure early can let the fire reflash and let CO2 into the space.
