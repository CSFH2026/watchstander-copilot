symptoms:
- excessive shaft vibration
- propeller damage
- abnormal noise from the shaft carried to the MRG
- shaft vibrates at speed
- line shaft bearings heating up after a grounding or a strike
causes:
- bent or broken propeller blade
- bent shaft
- shafting misalignment
- loose bearing hold-down bolts
- wiped line shaft bearing
- fouled propeller
- loss of stern tube bearing lubrication
- propeller striking ground
effects:
- reduced or lost propulsion power
- damage to shafting
- bearings
- and the MRG
associated:
- EOCC-SHFT-LOCK
- EOCC-SHFT-LBRG
- EOCC-SHFT-THRB

1. [step: EOCC-SHFT-DMG-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SHFT-DMG-02] Reduce speed on the affected shaft to minimize vibration. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SHFT-DMG-03] If the vibration continues, stop and lock the shaft. requires: 02. danger: a shaft that keeps vibrating can damage the MRG and the bearings; stop it rather than run it.
4. [step: EOCC-SHFT-DMG-04] Check along the line shaft to find the cause of the vibration. requires: 02
5. [step: EOCC-SHFT-DMG-05] If the cause is the shafting or the bearings, make temporary repairs if conditions permit. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SHFT-DMG-06] If repairs cannot be made, operate the shaft at the speed that keeps vibration lowest and keep an alert watch. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SHFT-DMG-07] Clear a fouled propeller when conditions permit. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SHFT-DMG-08] Correct any lubrication problem in the stern tube bearing. requires: 07
