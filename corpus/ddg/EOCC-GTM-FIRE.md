1. [step: EOCC-GTM-FIRE-01] Verify the module fire alarm on the PACC and look through the module window if it is safe to do so. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-FIRE-02] Report the fire to the EOOW, the bridge, the DCC, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-FIRE-03] Verify the GTM is stopped with EMER STOP, the module fuel valve is closed, the module cooling fan is off, and the vent damper and bleed air valve are closed. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-FIRE-04] Make sure no one is in the module before any release. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-FIRE-05] If the release has not started, press PRI HALON RLSE on the PACC and verify PRI HALON ACTUATED on the plasma display. requires: 04. danger: never release with personnel in the module; Halon displaces air and the fire makes toxic products.
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-FIRE-06] If the fire is not out, press RSV HALON RLSE for the reserve release. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-FIRE-07] Bring the other GTM on that shaft on line if the bridge needs the power, and report the shaft status. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-GTM-FIRE-08] Keep the module closed for at least 10 minutes, set fire boundaries, and report the status to the DCC. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-GTM-FIRE-09] Do not open the module until the fire is out, the module has cooled, and the atmosphere is tested. requires: 08. danger: opening the module early can let the fire reflash.
