symptoms:
- switchboard frequency unstable
- voltage fluctuating
- voltmeter needle swinging
- lights flickering
- frequency varying
- generator governor hunting
causes:
- voltage regulator malfunction
- governor malfunction
- exciter malfunction
- loose connections
- dirt and oil in the generator
effects:
- reduced or loss of electrical power
associated:
- EOCC-SSGTG-GOVF
- EOCC-SSGTG-EXCT
---
1. [step: EOCC-ELEC-FREQ-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-ELEC-FREQ-02] Start the standby SSGTG and shift the electrical load to it. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-ELEC-FREQ-03] Check the voltage regulator of the affected SSGTG when only current and voltage fluctuate. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-ELEC-FREQ-04] Check the governor of the affected SSGTG when the frequency oscillates. requires: 02
5. [step: EOCC-ELEC-FREQ-05] Check the excitation of the affected generator. requires: 02
6. [step: EOCC-ELEC-FREQ-06] Check for loose connections, shorts, or grounds in the system. requires: 02
7. [step: EOCC-ELEC-FREQ-07] Clean the generator and regulator, and adjust them according to the manufacturer's instructions. requires: 06
