1. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SPLT-01] Report the order to split the plant to the EOOW and the Chief Engineer, and pass it to each engine room. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SPLT-02] Line up each engine room's machinery so that it drives its own shaft, complete in itself. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SPLT-03] Line up the forward fuel service pumps with the forward service tanks and the after pumps with the after service tanks, and close the fuel transfer cross-connection valves. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SPLT-04] Split the starting air, cooling water, and firemain systems as the EOOW orders. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SPLT-05] Set the electric plant in the split-plant configuration, two SSGTGs on line and not paralleled, with only the bus tie that feeds the third switchboard energized. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-ELEC-SPLT-06] Report to the EOOW that the plant is split, with the line-up of each engine room. requires: 05
