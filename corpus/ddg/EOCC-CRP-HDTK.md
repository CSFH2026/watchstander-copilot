symptoms:
- CRP head tank level low
- OIL HEAD TK LEVEL LO
- CRP sump level low
- hub head tank low
causes:
- leak in the CRP hydraulic system
- oil lost at the OD box or hub seals
- low sump level
effects:
- air in the hub servo
- loss of pitch control

- EOCC-CRP-LHYD

1. [step: EOCC-CRP-HDTK-01] Verify the head tank level locally after the OIL HEAD TK LEVEL LO alarm. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-CRP-HDTK-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-CRP-HDTK-03] Check the oil level in the CRP sump tank. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-CRP-HDTK-04] Inspect the CRP hydraulic system for leaks. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-CRP-HDTK-05] Replenish oil from the LO storage tank, operating the system until the head tank level returns to normal. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-CRP-HDTK-06] Sample the oil for water contamination. requires: 05
