symptoms:
- hot MRG bearing
- MRG bearing temperature high
- reduction gear bearing overheating
- abnormal bearing temperature on the reduction gear
- high oil temperature to the cooler
causes:
- foreign matter in the lube oil
- LO pump failure or restriction in the system
- worn or misaligned bearing
- loss of seawater to the LO cooler
effects:
- reduced or lost propulsion power
- damaged bearings
- misalignment

- EOCC-MRG-LLOP

1. [step: EOCC-MRG-HBRG-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-MRG-HBRG-02] Reduce speed on the affected shaft. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-MRG-HBRG-03] Check the quantity and temperature of the oil fed to the bearing at its sight glass and thermometer. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-MRG-HBRG-04] Take samples of the lube oil and start the purifier on the MRG LO system. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-MRG-HBRG-05] Operate the shaft at speeds that keep the bearing temperature normal. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-MRG-HBRG-06] When operating conditions permit, inspect the bearing for wear or misalignment. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-MRG-HBRG-07] Clean and inspect the LO strainers. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-MRG-HBRG-08] Inspect the flexible coupling for dirt and wear. requires: 07
