1. [step: EOCC-FMN-RUPT-01] Report the rupture and its location to the DCC, the EOOW, and the bridge. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FMN-RUPT-02] Start a standby fire pump from the DCC if pressure in the rest of the main is low. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FMN-RUPT-03] Close the cutout valves on each side of the break to isolate it. requires: 01
4. [step: EOCC-FMN-RUPT-04] If the hole is small, patch it with a jubilee pipe patch or a soft patch and restore the section. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FMN-RUPT-05] If the section must come out, remove it, bolt in hose flange adapters, and connect a length of fire hose between them. requires: 03
6. [step: EOCC-FMN-RUPT-06] If the section cannot be removed, join the fireplugs nearest the closed cutout valves with hose and a double female coupling, then open both fireplugs. requires: 03
7. [step: EOCC-FMN-RUPT-07] Repair the damaged section of firemain as quickly as possible and report the main restored to the DCC. requires: 03
