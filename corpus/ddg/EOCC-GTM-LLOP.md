symptoms:
- low lube oil pressure on the GTM
- PRESS LOW on the PACC
- LUBO SPLY PRESS low
- GTM lube oil supply pressure dropping
- low oil pressure auto shutdown
causes:
- low oil level in the LOSCA tank
- clogged lube supply filter
- lube and scavenge pump failure
- broken lube oil line
- fuel dilution of the lube oil
- high lube oil temperature
effects:
- burned or seized engine bearings
- loss of the GTM
- reduced power on that shaft

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-LLOP-01] Verify low LO supply pressure on the PACC LUBO SPLY PRESS meter and at the local gauge. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-LLOP-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-LLOP-03] Bring the other GTM on that shaft on line if the bridge needs the power. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-LLOP-04] Secure the affected GTM. requires: 02. danger: MSC D-15 says secure the engine immediately; do not wait for 03 if pressure keeps falling or bearing noise is heard, and stop it by hand if battle override has blocked the automatic shutdown.
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-LLOP-05] Check the oil level in the LOSCA tank sight glasses. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-LLOP-06] Check the lube supply duplex filter, shift to the clean element if it is clogged, and check the lines. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-LLOP-07] Check the lube and scavenge pump. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-GTM-LLOP-08] Test the lube oil for fuel dilution. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-GTM-LLOP-09] Correct the cause of any high oil temperature. requires: 08
10. [step: EOCC-GTM-LLOP-10] Report the GTM ready for service to the EOOW only after the cause is corrected. requires: 09. danger: do not restart the GTM before the cause is found and corrected.
