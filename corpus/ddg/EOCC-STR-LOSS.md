1. [step: EOCC-STR-LOSS-01] Verify that the rudder does not answer the helm, using the rudder angle indicator and the ship's head. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-STR-LOSS-02] Report the casualty to the bridge, the EOOW, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-STR-LOSS-03] Shift to the alternate bridge steering control, if installed. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-STR-LOSS-04] When the bridge directs, take local control of the steering gear in after steering and steer by orders from the bridge. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-STR-LOSS-05] If local control fails, ready the local hand pump for steering, if installed. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-STR-LOSS-06] Restore remote control from the bridge after the fault is repaired and the bridge directs. requires: 04
