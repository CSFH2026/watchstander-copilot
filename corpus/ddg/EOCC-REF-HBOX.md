symptoms:
- reefer box temperature high
- freezer box warm
- chill box temperature rising
- high temperature on the reefer box thermometer
causes:
- thermostatic controls not working or out of adjustment
- heavy frost on the cooling coils
- recirculating fans stopped
- stores stowed against the air flow
- moisture freezing in the expansion valve
- low refrigerant
- blocked air duct screens
- too much traffic in and out of the boxes
- compressor overloaded
effects:
- spoiled stores
associated:
- EOCC-REF-LEAK

1. [step: EOCC-REF-HBOX-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-REF-HBOX-02] Check the thermostatic controls; repair or replace them, and use hand bypass regulation of refrigerant if necessary. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-REF-HBOX-03] Check the reefer box coils for heavy frost and the stores for proper stowage; defrost if necessary. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-REF-HBOX-04] Check the expansion valve for moisture at the orifice; adjust or clean it, and put the dehydrator in operation if moisture is present. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-REF-HBOX-05] Check the recirculating air fans; repair or replace them if necessary. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-REF-HBOX-06] Check the system for the proper amount of refrigerant, and recharge it if it is low. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-REF-HBOX-07] Check the refrigeration compressor for leaking valves, a blown head gasket, or overload. requires: 06
