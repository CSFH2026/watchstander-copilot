symptoms:
- low fuel pressure on the GTM
- fuel oil PRESS LOW on the PACC
- GTM slows down
- GTM unable to carry load
- fuel service pump failure alarm
causes:
- low level in the fuel service tank
- fuel service pump failure
- restriction in fuel piping or valves
- dirty fuel filters or strainers
- water in the fuel
- air in the fuel system
- faulty fuel pressure regulating valve
effects:
- reduced or lost propulsion power
- GTM flameout

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-01] Verify the fuel PRESS LOW alarm on the PACC and the fuel pressure at the local gauge. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-03] Reduce the throttle on the affected GTM while pressure is low. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-04] Shift to the standby fuel service pump. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-05] Check the fuel level in the service tank, and shift the fuel suction to another service tank if the level is low or the fuel has water. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-06] Check the valves and piping from the service tank to the GTM, and the module fuel valve. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-07] Clean the fuel strainers and check the filters. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-08] Check the fuel for water. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-09] Bleed off air from the fuel system using the purge valve. requires: 08
10. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-10] Check the fuel pressure regulating valve. requires: 09
11. [step: EOCC-GTM-LFOP-11] Restore normal throttle and report the fuel system status to the EOOW. requires: 10
