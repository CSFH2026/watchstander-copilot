1. [step: EOCC-FIRE-ELEC-01] Report the fire to the bridge, the Chief Engineer, the EOOW, and the DCC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FIRE-ELEC-02] De-energize the affected circuits. requires: 01. danger: do not use water or fog on equipment that is still energized.
3. [step: EOCC-FIRE-ELEC-03] Shift vital loads fed from the affected circuits to their alternate supply. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FIRE-ELEC-04] Attack the fire with a portable CO2 extinguisher or the CO2 hose and reel; close the horn valve, open the cylinder valve, run the horn to the fire, open the horn valve, and aim at the base of the fire. requires: 03. danger: the frost on a CO2 horn conducts electricity; never let the horn touch electrical components. Wear breathing apparatus when CO2 is used below decks.
5. [step: EOCC-FIRE-ELEC-05] If CO2 does not put the fire out, use Halon 1301, then PKP only in an emergency, then high-velocity fog no closer than 4 feet. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FIRE-ELEC-06] Overhaul the fire and set a reflash watch; CO2 smothering is temporary and hot embers can rekindle. requires: 05
