symptoms:
- GTM does not respond to the throttle
- gas turbine speed does not follow the programmed control lever
- GTM went to idle by itself
- PLA system fail alarm
- throttle jammed
causes:
- PLA actuator command loss or malfunction
- fail-to-idle circuit operated
- PLA linkage adrift or binding
- loss of control power
- programmed control lever fault
effects:
- reduced or lost maneuverability
- possible collision or grounding

- EOCC-CTL-PACC

1. [step: EOCC-CTL-THRT-01] Verify on the PACC that the GTM speed does not follow the throttle order. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-CTL-THRT-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer; tell the bridge the shaft power you can answer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-CTL-THRT-03] Shift to manual control at the SCU for the affected shaft. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-CTL-THRT-04] Check for a PLA system fail or fail-to-idle condition on the affected GTM. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-CTL-THRT-05] Check and repair the PLA actuator linkage to the main fuel control. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-CTL-THRT-06] Check control power to the PLA actuator electronics. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-CTL-THRT-07] Return the shaft to programmed control after the fault is corrected and report it to the EOOW. requires: 06. danger: match the PACC order to the actual setting before the shift so speed does not change without warning.
