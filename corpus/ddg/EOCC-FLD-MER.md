1. [step: EOCC-FLD-MER-01] Report the flooding to the bridge, the Chief Engineer, and the DCC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FLD-MER-02] Find where the water comes from, and isolate the piping system or sea connection that feeds it. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FLD-MER-03] Secure electrical circuits, lights, and power in the flooding area before the water reaches them. requires: 02. danger: water reaching energized equipment is an electric shock hazard to the people in the space.
4. [step: EOCC-FLD-MER-04] Shift propulsion and generator load to the other engine room if machinery in the space is threatened. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FLD-MER-05] Plug or patch any holes, and set flooding boundaries at the adjacent spaces. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FLD-MER-06] Inspect the bulkhead packing glands and penetrations between the space and the other machinery spaces. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-FLD-MER-07] Dewater with the engine room main drainage eductor or portable pumps, in the order the stability diagram calls for. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-FLD-MER-08] Make repairs, and report the space status to the DCC and the EOOW. requires: 07
