symptoms:
- GTM module temperature high
- ENCLOSURE TEMP HIGH
- GTM cooling fan stopped
- module cooling air fan failure
causes:
- module cooling fan failed or tripped
- vent damper shut
- loss of ship's service air to the vent damper
- cooling air duct blocked
effects:
- GTM components above their rated temperature
- GTM shutdown

- EOCC-GTM-HTIT

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-ENCT-01] Verify the module temperature alarm and the cooling fan status on the PACC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-ENCT-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-ENCT-03] Restart the module cooling fan from the PACC or the SCU, or at its local controller. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-ENCT-04] Check that the module vent damper is open and has ship's service air. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-ENCT-05] If module cooling cannot be restored, reduce power and secure the GTM. requires: 04. danger: the cooling system must run for engine operation.
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-ENCT-06] Report the cause and the GTM status to the EOOW. requires: 05
