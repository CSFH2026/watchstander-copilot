symptoms:
- high OD box temperature
- OD box temperature above 150 F
- OD box hot to the touch
- oil distribution box overheating
causes:
- loss of prairie air to the OD box
- prairie air rotoseal leak
- operation in emergency ahead pitch
- high hydraulic oil temperature
effects:
- damage to the OD box seals and bearings
- loss of pitch control
associated:
- EOCC-CRP-EMPT
---
1. [step: EOCC-CRP-ODHT-01] Verify the OD box temperature locally; the DDG-51 limit is 150 F. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-CRP-ODHT-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-CRP-ODHT-03] Verify that prairie air is supplied to the OD box, and check the prairie air rotoseal for leaks. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-CRP-ODHT-04] Check the CRP hydraulic oil pressures and temperature against the EOSS. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-CRP-ODHT-05] Apply cooling water to the OD box housing if prairie air does not hold the temperature. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-CRP-ODHT-06] If the temperature keeps rising, reduce speed on the shaft and report to the EOOW. requires: 05
