symptoms:
- low LP air pressure alarm
- ship's service air pressure dropping
- air receiver pressure falling
- LPAC cannot keep up
- priority valves shut off non-vital air
causes:
- LPAC motor failure
- dirty intake filters
- ruptured or plugged intercooler or aftercooler
- pressure controller or unloader failure
- leaking relief valve or ruptured air piping
- air demand above compressor capacity
- dirty or broken compressor valves or piston rings
- slipping or broken drive belt
effects:
- loss or reduced ship's service air
- loss of air-operated control systems
associated:
- EOCC-AIR-HTMP
- EOCC-AIR-HPAC
---
1. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-02] Secure non-essential air services. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-03] Start the standby LPAC and put it into service, following the manufacturer's instructions for starting an idle compressor. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-04] Check the prime mover of the affected LPAC. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-05] Check the drive belts on a belt-driven unit; adjust the tension or replace them. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-06] Check the intake filters, and clean them if they are dirty. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-07] Check the operation and settings of the pressure controller and the unloader. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-08] Check the coolers for leaks or restriction, and repair or clean them. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-09] Check the piping and relief valves for leaks or ruptures, and correct them. requires: 08
10. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-10] Check the compressor air valves, and clean or replace them if they are dirty or broken. requires: 09
11. [step: EOCC-AIR-LOSP-11] Check the piston rings, and replace them as required. requires: 10
