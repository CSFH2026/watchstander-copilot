symptoms:
- RO unit output low
- product water flow low
- product conductivity rising
- salt passage increasing
- RO product purity not correct
causes:
- old RO module with low feed water temperature
- cleaning needed or done wrong
- high module pressure differential
- biological fouling
- low feed pressure to the modules
- feed water temperature too high
- membrane fouling
- conductivity indicator malfunction
effects:
- reduced fresh water output
- possible contamination of potable water
associated:
- EOCC-DIST-SAL

1. [step: EOCC-DIST-CAP-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-DIST-CAP-02] Reclean the affected section, with special cleaning reagents if needed, or replace the module. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-DIST-CAP-03] Remove foulants by acid cleaning according to the manufacturer's guide. requires: 01
4. [step: EOCC-DIST-CAP-04] Check the operation of the high pressure pump, and adjust the first and second stage pressures to their proper settings. requires: 01
5. [step: EOCC-DIST-CAP-05] Keep the feed water at the manufacturer's suggested temperature. requires: 01
6. [step: EOCC-DIST-CAP-06] Clean the membranes according to the manufacturer's instructions. requires: 01
7. [step: EOCC-DIST-CAP-07] Test and troubleshoot the conductivity indicator. requires: 01
