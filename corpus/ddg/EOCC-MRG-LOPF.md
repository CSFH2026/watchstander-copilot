1. [step: EOCC-MRG-LOPF-01] Verify the pump failure alarm and the MRG LO header pressure at the PACC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-MRG-LOPF-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-MRG-LOPF-03] If LO pressure is not held, stop the affected shaft until LO pressure is restored. requires: 02. danger: do not run the shaft below normal LO pressure; on a loss of all electric power, the coast-down pump runs only as long as the HP air lasts.
4. [step: EOCC-MRG-LOPF-04] Put the standby motor-driven LO pump into service, locally at its controller if remote control fails. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-MRG-LOPF-05] Check power to the controller of the failed pump. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-MRG-LOPF-06] Push the reset button and restart the pump. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-MRG-LOPF-07] Check the pump and motor for free turning. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-MRG-LOPF-08] Check the motor circuit for an open. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-MRG-LOPF-09] Reduce speed on the affected shaft until both LO pumps and both shafts are back on the line. requires: 08
