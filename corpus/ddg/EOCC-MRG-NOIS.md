symptoms:
- unusual noise in the MRG
- reduction gear noise
- loud or roaring noise from the reduction gear
- gear noise changes with speed
- grinding or knocking in the MRG
causes:
- gear tooth damage
- bearing failure
- loss of oil sprays
- foreign matter in the gear
- misalignment between the line shaft and the MRG
effects:
- gear tooth failure
- loss of the shaft
- damage to the MRG

- EOCC-SHFT-LOCK

1. [step: EOCC-MRG-NOIS-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW and keep the bridge informed of the ability to answer bells and the maximum speed available. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-MRG-NOIS-02] Slow the affected shaft at once; stop it if the noise persists. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-MRG-NOIS-03] Check the MRG LO pressure, the bearing temperatures, and the oil sprays and strainers, and look for babbitt or other foreign matter. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-MRG-NOIS-04] If there is a loud or roaring noise that indicates gear tooth damage, stop the shaft and check it at once. requires: 01
5. [step: EOCC-MRG-NOIS-05] Lock the shaft according to the EOSS/EOCC procedures or the manufacturer's instructions. requires: 04. danger: never before 04; the shaft must be stopped before it is locked.
6. [step: EOCC-MRG-NOIS-06] Make a preliminary inspection of the gear teeth and other parts of the MRG. requires: 05
