symptoms:
- high vibration on the GTM
- GG VIB HI
- PT VIB HI
- GTM vibration alarm

- PT VIB SENSOR FAIL

- fouled compressor blading
- bleed air valve binding

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-HVIB-01] Verify the vibration reading on the PACC, note whether it is the gas generator or the power turbine pickup, and check for a sensor fail alarm. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-HVIB-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-HVIB-03] If the vibration is excessive, shut down the GTM at once. requires: 02. danger: excessive vibration during a start or at speed needs an immediate shutdown.
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-HVIB-04] Bring the other GTM on that shaft on line if the bridge needs the power, and operate at reduced speed until the shafting and propeller are checked. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-HVIB-05] Examine the bleed air valve for binding, and check its sensing lines for blockage. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-HVIB-06] Inspect the inlet, the FOD screen, and the compressor inlet for foreign matter, and remove it. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-HVIB-07] Check the shock mounts and the engine alignment. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-GTM-HVIB-08] Report the findings to the EOOW before the GTM is restarted. requires: 07
