title: Propeller Pitch Drifts Toward Astern
system: Controllable reversible pitch propeller
tier: taken
source: COMSCINST 3541.6A Engineering Casualty Control Exercises (Military Sealift Command, 15 May 1986), exercise M-13 "Variable Pitch Propeller Goes Slowly from its Command Position to Astern", https://web.archive.org/web/20050208220520id_/http://www.msc.navy.mil/instructions/pdf/m35416a.pdf
status: training copy, unclassified. Steps follow the action list of MSC M-13 in source order; equipment names are changed to DDG-51 names. The MSC control and pilot oil are the CRP control oil supplied by the HOPM; the standby pump is the electric CRP pump. Generated by scripts/build_ddg.py from corpus/ddg/src/crp.yaml; do not edit.
symptoms:
- pitch drifting toward astern
- propeller pitch moving from the commanded position
- pitch going astern without a command
- pitch indication at the PACC and bridge does not match the order
causes:
- low or no control oil pressure
- no pilot oil pressure
- control oil temperature too high
effects:
- loss of ship's speed
- loss of maneuvering capability
associated:
- EOCC-CRP-LPCT
- EOCC-CRP-LHYD
---
1. [step: EOCC-CRP-ASTN-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-CRP-ASTN-02] Check the CRP control oil pressure at the HOPM, and remedy any defect in the control pump system. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-CRP-ASTN-03] Check the pilot oil pressure; if there is none, start the electric CRP pump and repair as necessary. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-CRP-ASTN-04] Check the control oil temperature; remedy any defect in the control pump or the actuating unit, and clean the oil cooler if necessary. requires: 03
