1. [step: EOCC-FUEL-FSEP-01] Verify the filter/separator differential pressure and water level indications at the console and locally. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FUEL-FSEP-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FUEL-FSEP-03] If the water level is high, check that the automatic drain valve dumps the sump, and drain it manually if it does not. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FUEL-FSEP-04] If a strainer is dirty, shift the duplex strainer to the clean basket; if water keeps coming, shift suction to the standby service tank. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FUEL-FSEP-05] Sample the filter/separator discharge and test it for sediment and free water. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FUEL-FSEP-06] If the sample fails or the differential pressure stays high, replace the coalescer elements and recirculate the service tank through the purifier. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-FUEL-FSEP-07] Report the filter/separator back to normal to the EOOW after a sample passes. requires: 06
