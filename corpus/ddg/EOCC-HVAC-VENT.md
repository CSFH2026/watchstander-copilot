symptoms:
- machinery space ventilation fan stopped
- engine room supply fan failure
- exhaust fan tripped
- engine room temperature rising
- no ventilation in the AMR
causes:
- fan motor or controller failure
- loss of power to the fan
- fan stopped by a fire or CO2 shutdown
- controller left in the wrong mode
effects:
- high machinery space temperature
- equipment overheating
- heat stress on watchstanders
associated:
- EOCC-FIRE-MER

1. [step: EOCC-HVAC-VENT-01] Verify which supply or exhaust fan has stopped and the space temperature. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-HVAC-VENT-02] Check whether the fan was stopped by a fire, Halon, or CO2 shutdown before restarting it. requires: 01. danger: do not restart ventilation to a space where a fire alarm or extinguishing agent release has stopped the fans.
3. [step: EOCC-HVAC-VENT-03] Report the casualty to the EOOW and the Chief Engineer. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-HVAC-VENT-04] Start the other supply or exhaust fan for the space from its local controller, or from the console with the controller in LOCAL/REMOTE. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-HVAC-VENT-05] Check the failed fan's controller and power supply, and reset it if no fault is found. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-HVAC-VENT-06] Watch the machinery space temperature and report to the EOOW if equipment must be shifted or secured. requires: 05
