1. [step: EOCC-STR-MOTR-01] Verify which steering pump motor has stopped, from the alarms at the bridge and in after steering. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-STR-MOTR-02] Report the casualty to the bridge, the EOOW, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-STR-MOTR-03] Switch over to the standby steering pump and motor from the bridge; if that is not possible, switch over in after steering when the bridge directs. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-STR-MOTR-04] Switch the steering power feeders to the alternate source. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-STR-MOTR-05] If power cannot be restored, use the hand pump to steer or to position the rudder, if installed. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-STR-MOTR-06] Recommend to the bridge that heading be held by maneuvering the shafts until steering is restored. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-STR-MOTR-07] Control the rudder with emergency tackle if facilities are provided. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-STR-MOTR-08] Check the motor and steering unit electric controllers. requires: 07
