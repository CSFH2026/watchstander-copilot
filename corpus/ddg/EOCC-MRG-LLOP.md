symptoms:
- low lube oil pressure on the MRG
- MRG LO header pressure low
- HEADER PRESS LO
- reduction gear low lube oil pressure
- no oil flow at the bearing sight glass bull's eye
- most remote bearing pressure low
causes:
- MRG LO pump failure
- standby LO pump fails to start
- restriction in LO piping or filters
- low MRG sump level
- high oil temperature
- ruptured LO line
- filter shifted wrong

- loss of maneuverability
- damage to MRG bearings and gears
- damage to shafting and thrust bearing
- loss of the shaft

1. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-01] Verify low MRG LO header pressure at the PACC, and check oil flow at the bearing sight glasses. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-03] Stop the affected shaft; set its pitch to zero and bring its GTMs to idle or secure them, and at the same time work to regain LO pressure. requires: 02. danger: do not keep the shaft turning without lube oil pressure; ask the bridge to take way off the ship so the shaft does not windmill.
4. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-04] Start the standby motor-driven LO pump. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-05] Check the LO level in the MRG sump and shift the LO filter to the clean side. requires: 03
6. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-06] Check the LO temperature. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-07] Inspect the bearings and strainers for metal flakes. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-08] Check power to the controller of the failed LO pump, push the reset button, and restart the pump. requires: 04
9. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-09] Check the pump and motor for free turning, and check the motor circuit for an open. requires: 08
10. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-10] Operate on the unaffected shaft at reduced speed until both shafts are back on the line. requires: 03
11. [step: EOCC-MRG-LLOP-11] Return the shaft to service only after LO pressure is restored and verified at the PACC. requires: 05, 06, 07, 09. danger: do not restore the shaft before LO pressure is restored.
