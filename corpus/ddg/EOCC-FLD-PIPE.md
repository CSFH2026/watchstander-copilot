1. [step: EOCC-FLD-PIPE-01] Secure or isolate the ruptured section of the piping system. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FLD-PIPE-02] Remove the lagging, clean the area around the rupture of grease, oil, dirt, and paint, and make sure the pipe is dry. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FLD-PIPE-03] Simplify the rupture by bending or removing irregular projections where practical. requires: 02. danger: make sure no explosive conditions exist before using spark-producing tools or burning equipment.
4. [step: EOCC-FLD-PIPE-04] Determine the amount of patch material needed. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FLD-PIPE-05] Put on the eyeshields and gloves, and mix all of the hardener into the resin. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FLD-PIPE-06] Coat both sides of the void cover with the mixture and tie it over the rupture with the chalk line. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-FLD-PIPE-07] Impregnate the woven roving cloth, center it over the void cover, and wrap it at least three turns around the pipe. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-FLD-PIPE-08] Wrap the PVC film at least two turns around the patch and tie it with the chalk line. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-FLD-PIPE-09] Restore pressure to the pipe only after the patch has cured and is cool to the bare hand. requires: 08. danger: pressure on an uncured patch can blow it off.
