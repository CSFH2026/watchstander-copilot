1. [step: EOCC-GAS-TEST-01] Calibrate the oxygen analyzer in fresh air on the weather deck. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GAS-TEST-02] Send the sensor into the space and check all around the compartment, noting the lowest reading. requires: 01. danger: the person carrying the sensor into the space must wear an OBA or an air-line mask.
3. [step: EOCC-GAS-TEST-03] If oxygen is below 20 percent or above 22 percent, ventilate the space and retest. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GAS-TEST-04] Turn on the combustible gas indicator, flush it with fresh air, and set the pointer to zero. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GAS-TEST-05] Sample various locations in the space with the hose until the space is safe or a gas concentration is found, and note where the highest reading is. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GAS-TEST-06] Test for carbon monoxide and other toxic gases with the gas sampler and the correct indicator tube. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GAS-TEST-07] Flush the instruments in fresh air and secure them. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-GAS-TEST-08] Let no one enter without breathing apparatus until the atmosphere is certified safe. requires: 07. danger: entry before the atmosphere is certified safe can kill by asphyxiation, explosion, or toxic gas.
