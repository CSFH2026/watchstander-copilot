1. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BLKO-01] Verify the loss of power at the EPCC and the switchboards, and note which SSGTGs are available to start. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BLKO-02] Report the loss of power to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BLKO-03] Start an available SSGTG; use HP air for the start if bleed air is not available. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BLKO-04] Close the SSGTG generator breaker onto its dead switchboard. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BLKO-05] Strip all unnecessary circuits, and restore power to vital loads first. requires: 04. danger: restoring all loads to one generator at once will overload it and trip the breakers again.
6. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BLKO-06] Energize the other switchboards through the bus ties. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BLKO-07] Start a second SSGTG and parallel it to the bus. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BLKO-08] Restore the remaining loads as generator capacity permits, and report the plant status to the EOOW. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-ELEC-BLKO-09] If a switchboard cannot be energized, rig casualty power from a live switchboard to the vital loads. requires: 04
