1. [step: EOCC-FIRE-FUEL-01] Report the fire to the bridge, the Chief Engineer, and the DCC, and alert the ship. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FIRE-FUEL-02] Secure the fuel service pump and close the suction valve that feeds the leak. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FIRE-FUEL-03] Stop the GTM or SSGTG whose fuel supply was secured. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FIRE-FUEL-04] Knock down the fire with PKP, then cover the fuel with AFFF. requires: 03. danger: PKP gives no reflash protection; fuel on hot surfaces reignites unless AFFF covers it.
5. [step: EOCC-FIRE-FUEL-05] Close openings in the fire area and de-energize electrical circuits in the fire area. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FIRE-FUEL-06] Correct the cause of the leak and remove the fire hazard. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-FIRE-FUEL-07] If the fire cannot be controlled, carry out the main engine room fire procedure. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-FIRE-FUEL-08] Cover spilled oil with absorbent to contain it, clean it up, and set a reflash watch. requires: 07
