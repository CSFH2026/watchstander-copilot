symptoms:
- SSGTG overspeed trip
- generator frequency high
- fluctuating generator speed
- engine racing
- overspeed alarm
causes:
- defective governor or governor linkage
- sudden loss of load
- coupling failure between engine and generator
- speed control set too sensitive
- low oil level in the governor
- overspeed module malfunction
effects:
- loss of electrical power
- overspeed damage to the engine and generator
- possible injury to personnel
associated:
- EOCC-SSGTG-GOVF
- EOCC-ELEC-LGEN

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-OSPD-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-OSPD-02] Disconnect the malfunctioning SSGTG from the bus if the situation permits. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-OSPD-03] De-energize non-vital circuits to prevent overload of the remaining SSGTG. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-OSPD-04] Put the standby SSGTG into service and parallel it to the operating generator. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-OSPD-05] Check the governor for parts failure or low oil level. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-OSPD-06] Check the governor linkage and the fuel metering valve linkage for freeness. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-OSPD-07] Examine the overspeed module, including its flexible drive shaft; replace it if necessary. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-OSPD-08] Inspect the generator and the gas turbine engine for overspeed damage. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-OSPD-09] After repairs, test all safety devices according to the manufacturer's instructions before the SSGTG returns to service. requires: 08. danger: do not return the SSGTG to service with a defective governor.
