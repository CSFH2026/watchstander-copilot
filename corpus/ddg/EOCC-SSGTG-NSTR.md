symptoms:
- SSGTG will not crank
- SSGTG does not turn over on start
- starter air pressure low
- start air low pressure alarm
- no rotation when the start is ordered
causes:
- no bleed air or HP start air
- start air control valve failed
- air leaks in the start air system
- open circuit in the start circuit
- remote
- local
- or CO2 shutdown switch open
- safety shutdown switch not reset
- control fuses blown
- starter gear frozen
effects:
- SSGTG not available
- no standby generator

- EOCC-AIR-HPAC

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-NSTR-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer, and keep the bridge informed. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-NSTR-02] Check start air pressure at the SSGTG starter LP and HP air control valves. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-NSTR-03] Shift the start to the other air supply, LP bleed air or HP air from the flasks; use the manual needle bypass valve for a manual HP start if the HP control valve fails. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-NSTR-04] If start air is low, start all available air compressors and check the air system and starting air valves for leaks. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-NSTR-05] Check that the remote, local, and CO2 pressure shutdown switches are reset and closed. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-NSTR-06] Check that the automatic safety shutdown switches (overspeed, high exhaust temperature, low oil pressure, high LO temperature) are reset. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-NSTR-07] Check the control panel fuses and connections, and re-arm the start circuit. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-NSTR-08] Examine the air starter and free up the starter gear if it is frozen. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-NSTR-09] Retry the start and report the result to the EOOW. requires: 08
