- LOSCA tank level dropping
causes:
- low MRG lube oil flow to the GTM lube oil cooler
- temperature control valve fault
- leaking tube or gasket in the GTM lube oil cooler
- failing engine bearing in one sump

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-HSCV-01] Verify which scavenge temperature is high on the PACC, one sump or all of them. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-HSCV-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-HSCV-03] Check MRG lube oil flow to the GTM lube oil cooler and its temperature control valve. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-HSCV-04] Reduce load on the affected GTM while the cause is found. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-HSCV-05] Watch the LOSCA tank level and the MRG sump level for oil passing through a cooler leak. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-HSCV-06] Secure the GTM if the cooler leaks or the temperature keeps rising; the source's other choice, a standby cooler, is not described for the LOSCA. requires: 05. danger: secure the GTM at once if one sump temperature keeps rising with a normal cooler outlet temperature, because that points to a failing bearing.
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-HSCV-07] Pressure test the cooler; plug the leaking tube, renew the gaskets, or replace the cooler. requires: 06
