symptoms:
- seawater service pressure low
- sanitary pressure low
- no flushing water in the heads
- air compressor stopped on high cooling water temperature
- A/C compressor stopped on high discharge pressure
- loss of cooling water to shaft bearings and stern tubes
causes:
- seawater service pump motor failure
- pump end failure
- restriction in suction or discharge piping
- ruptured piping
- flushometer valves stuck open
- pressure switch failure
effects:
- loss of air compressors
- loss of A/C and refrigeration compressors
- loss of cooling to spring bearings and stern tubes
- loss of plant
associated:
- EOCC-SW-LSUP
- EOCC-AIR-HTMP
- EOCC-AC-HDIS
- EOCC-SHFT-SEAL

1. [step: EOCC-SW-SANP-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SW-SANP-02] Put the standby seawater service pump into service. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SW-SANP-03] Supply the seawater main from the firemain through a reducing station if necessary. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SW-SANP-04] Check the suction and discharge strainers and make sure there are no ruptured pipes. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SW-SANP-05] Check the flushometer valves in the heads for valves stuck open. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SW-SANP-06] Check cooling water to the air compressors, the A/C and refrigeration condensers, the spring bearings and stern tubes, the SSGTGs, and other auxiliary machinery. requires: 05
