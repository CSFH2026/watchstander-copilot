1. [step: EOCC-LO-LEAK-01] Report the leak to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-LO-LEAK-02] Isolate the leak; secure the leaking pump and its suction valve, or shift to the standby pump or filter. If the leak is on the MRG LO service system, stop the shaft first. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-LO-LEAK-03] Alert the ship and make ready the firefighting equipment for a Class B fire. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-LO-LEAK-04] Remove ignition sources and fire hazards from the area of the leak. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-LO-LEAK-05] Throw sand or absorbent on oil on the deck to contain it and prevent slipping. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-LO-LEAK-06] Correct the cause of the leak. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-LO-LEAK-07] Clean up the spill before any equipment near it is started. requires: 06. danger: do not start hot machinery near the spill before the oil is cleaned up.
8. [step: EOCC-LO-LEAK-08] Put the equipment back in service and replenish lost oil. requires: 07
