symptoms:
- hot bearing on the SSGTG generator
- generator bearing temperature high
- FRONT BRG TEMP HIGH
- REAR BRG TEMP HIGH
- no oil flow in the bearing sight glass
- lube oil pressure low
causes:
- lube oil pump failure
- restriction in the LO system
- insufficient cooling water through the LO cooler
- worn or misaligned bearing
- low level in the oil sump
effects:
- damage to shafts and bearings
- bearing failure
- loss of electrical power
associated:
- EOCC-SSGTG-LLOP
- EOCC-SSGTG-HLOT

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HBRG-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HBRG-02] Shift the electrical load to the standby SSGTG. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HBRG-03] Secure the affected SSGTG; flush the LO system, fill it with clean oil, and clean the strainers. requires: 02. danger: never before 02 while the affected SSGTG carries load the others cannot pick up.
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HBRG-04] Check the LO cooler. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HBRG-05] Check the affected bearing for wear and misalignment, and check that the oil passages to and within the bearing shell are clear. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HBRG-06] Check the flexible coupling for dirt and misalignment. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HBRG-07] Check the sump level. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HBRG-08] Check that the LO pump puts out enough pressure. requires: 07
