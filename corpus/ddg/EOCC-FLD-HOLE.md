1. [step: EOCC-FLD-HOLE-01] Report the hole, its size, and its location to the bridge, the Chief Engineer, and the DCC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FLD-HOLE-02] Set and hold flooding boundaries around the damaged compartment. requires: 01. danger: do not open doors or hatches into a flooded compartment to reach the damage; other compartments will flood.
3. [step: EOCC-FLD-HOLE-03] Plug holes at or just above the waterline first, and holes on the low side if the ship lists; then plug the higher holes. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FLD-HOLE-04] Plug the smaller holes as well as the large ones, partially if a full plug is not possible. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FLD-HOLE-05] Plug small holes with cloth-wrapped softwood plugs and wedges, and caulk the remaining leaks with rags, oakum, and small wedges. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FLD-HOLE-06] Cover holes with jagged inboard edges with a box patch, and small holes with a hinged patch or a patch held by hook bolts and a strongback. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-FLD-HOLE-07] Start dewatering only after the holes are plugged and the boundaries are set. requires: 06
