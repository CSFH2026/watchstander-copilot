symptoms:
- GTM will not crank
- GTM does not turn over on start
- no gas generator speed on start
- start air pressure low
- start air low pressure alarm
causes:
- no bleed air or HP start air
- starter air regulating valve failed
- air leaks in the start air system
- open circuit in the start circuit
- shutdown not reset
- control fuses blown
- starter or starter gear frozen
effects:
- GTM not available
- reduced power on that shaft
associated:
- EOCC-AIR-HPAC
- EOCC-GTM-FTLO

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-NSTR-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer, and keep the bridge informed. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-NSTR-02] Check start air pressure to the GTM starter air regulating valve. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-NSTR-03] Select the other start air source at the PACC, BLEED AIR or HIGH PRESS, and try the start once more. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-NSTR-04] If start air is low, start all available air compressors, and check the air system and the starter air valve for leaks. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-NSTR-05] Check that all GTM shutdowns are reset. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-NSTR-06] Check the start circuit connections and fuses, and re-arm the start circuit. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-NSTR-07] Examine the starter and free up the starter gear if it is frozen. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-GTM-NSTR-08] Replace any shutdown switch found faulty. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-GTM-NSTR-09] Retry the start and report the result to the EOOW. requires: 08
