1. [step: EOCC-FIRE-HALN-01] Report the Halon release to the bridge, the Chief Engineer, and the DCC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FIRE-HALN-02] Verify that the space is evacuated before the 60-second time delay ends. requires: 01. danger: Halon decomposition products are toxic; anyone affected must be moved to fresh air at once.
3. [step: EOCC-FIRE-HALN-03] Verify that the ventilation to the space shut down and that the closures are made. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FIRE-HALN-04] Hold the Halon in the space for at least 10 minutes, and keep the space closed for a soak period of at least 15 minutes so it cools. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FIRE-HALN-05] Keep the fire boundaries manned and cooled during the soak period. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FIRE-HALN-06] Reenter only in an oxygen breathing apparatus, and keep it on until the atmosphere is certified safe. requires: 05. danger: hot surfaces can reflash when air enters; Halon has no cooling effect.
7. [step: EOCC-FIRE-HALN-07] Verify the fire is out and the oxygen and explosive gas tests are acceptable before ventilating. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-FIRE-HALN-08] Inspect the ventilation system, station fire parties at the blower and controller, and get the engineer officer's permission to start the blowers and desmoke. requires: 07
