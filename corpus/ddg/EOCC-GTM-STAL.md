symptoms:
- compressor stall on the GTM
- GTM compressor surge
- T5.4 rises fast during acceleration
- GTM loses power
causes:
- variable stator vanes out of schedule
- main fuel control fault
- inlet blocked or nylon FOD screen fitted with the throttle over its limit
- fouled compressor
effects:
- combustor and turbine damage
- loss of the GTM
associated:
- EOCC-GTM-HTIT
- EOCC-GTM-HVIB

1. [step: EOCC-GTM-STAL-01] Verify the stall from the PACC T5.4 and gas generator speed readings. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GTM-STAL-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GTM-STAL-03] Reduce the throttle on the affected GTM to idle. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-GTM-STAL-04] If T5.4 keeps rising or the stall does not clear, stop the GTM. requires: 03. danger: a severe stall can destroy the engine in seconds; stop it if the stall does not clear at once.
5. [step: EOCC-GTM-STAL-05] Check the inlet and the FOD screen for blockage, and check whether a nylon FOD screen is fitted. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-GTM-STAL-06] Check the variable stator vanes and the main fuel control for the cause. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GTM-STAL-07] Report the GTM ready for service to the EOOW after the cause is corrected. requires: 06
