- seawater service pressure low
- seawater strainer clogged
- strainer differential pressure high

- cooling water temperatures rising

1. [step: EOCC-SW-LSUP-01] Verify low seawater pressure at the console and at the pump discharge gauge. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SW-LSUP-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SW-LSUP-03] Start a second seawater service pump, or supply the seawater main through a firemain reducing station. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SW-LSUP-04] Reduce the load on the affected machinery to prevent overheating. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SW-LSUP-05] Check the operation of the seawater service pump and vent it if it is air bound. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SW-LSUP-06] Check and clean the seawater strainer; shift a duplex strainer to the clean side first. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SW-LSUP-07] If the sea chest is obstructed, shift to another sea suction and report it to the EOOW for clearing. requires: 06
