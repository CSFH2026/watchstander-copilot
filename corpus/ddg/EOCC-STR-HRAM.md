1. [step: EOCC-STR-HRAM-01] Verify the leak at the ram and check the replenishing tank level. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-STR-HRAM-02] Report the casualty to the bridge, the EOOW, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-STR-HRAM-03] If the leak cannot be held by keeping the tank full, secure the affected ram. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-STR-HRAM-04] Operate on one ram after securing the valves as the instructions posted in after steering direct. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-STR-HRAM-05] Replenish the hydraulic oil lost and purge the system. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-STR-HRAM-06] Renew the leaking ram seals before the ram returns to service. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-STR-HRAM-07] Keep the plunger surfaces clean and lubricated. requires: 06
