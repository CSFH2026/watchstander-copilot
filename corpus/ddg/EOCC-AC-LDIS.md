symptoms:
- A/C plant discharge pressure low
- low head pressure on the A/C compressor
- frost on the compressor suction side
- A/C compressor motor stops
causes:
- too much seawater through the condenser
- leaking compressor discharge valves
- expansion valve not working and flooding liquid back
- low refrigerant charge
- refrigerant leaks
effects:
- compressor damage
- loss of chilled water cooling

- EOCC-AC-CHWL

1. [step: EOCC-AC-LDIS-01] Verify low discharge pressure at the A/C plant gauges and look for frost on the compressor suction side. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-AC-LDIS-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-AC-LDIS-03] Start the standby A/C plant and shift the chilled water load to it. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-AC-LDIS-04] Check the cooling water flow and temperature through the condenser; throttle the inlet valve if necessary, and check the water regulating valve. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-AC-LDIS-05] Check the compressor valves, and repair or replace them as necessary. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-AC-LDIS-06] Check the expansion valve and the thermal bulb attachment and insulation; use the bypass and hand control if the valve does not work, and replace it. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-AC-LDIS-07] Check for enough refrigerant in the system, add charge if necessary, and check for leaks. requires: 06. danger: a leak vents refrigerant into the A/C machinery room; refrigerant vapor can displace the air in the space.
