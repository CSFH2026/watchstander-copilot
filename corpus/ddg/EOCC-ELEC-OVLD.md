- loss of a paralleled SSGTG

- unequal load sharing between paralleled generators

1. [step: EOCC-ELEC-OVLD-01] Verify the load, current, and frequency of each on-line SSGTG at the EPCC, and note any load shedding indicators. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-ELEC-OVLD-02] Report the overload to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-ELEC-OVLD-03] Start the standby SSGTG and parallel it to the bus. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-ELEC-OVLD-04] Shed or hold off non-vital loads until the generators are within their rating. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-ELEC-OVLD-05] Check load sharing between the paralleled SSGTGs and correct it at the EPCC. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-ELEC-OVLD-06] Restore the shed loads one at a time as capacity permits, and report the plant status to the EOOW. requires: 05
