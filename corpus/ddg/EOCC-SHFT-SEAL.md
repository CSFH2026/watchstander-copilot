- stern tube seal leaking
- water spraying from the shaft seal

1. [step: EOCC-SHFT-SEAL-01] Verify the leak or the loss of seal water flow at the stern tube. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SHFT-SEAL-02] Notify the bridge, the EOOW, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SHFT-SEAL-03] Reduce speed and/or stop the affected shaft. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SHFT-SEAL-04] If seal water is lost, cross-connect the firemain to the stern tube seal supply. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SHFT-SEAL-05] Immediately bring dewatering pumps or eductors to the space to control flooding. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SHFT-SEAL-06] When repairs are permitted, lock the shaft to prevent it from turning. requires: 03
7. [step: EOCC-SHFT-SEAL-07] Pressurize the inflatable seal and maintain pressure. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-SHFT-SEAL-08] Disassemble the seal housing and renew one or both seal rings; use packing if no spare seals are on board, as the manufacturer's manual directs. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-SHFT-SEAL-09] Reassemble the seal, deflate the inflatable seal, and unlock the shaft. requires: 08. danger: never before the seal housing is reassembled; deflating the inflatable seal early floods the space.
