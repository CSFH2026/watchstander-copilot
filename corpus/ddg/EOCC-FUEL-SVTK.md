symptoms:
- fuel service tank level low
- low level alarm on the service tank
- service tank running dry
causes:
- transfer not started in time
- fuel transfer pump failure
- fuel leak
- wrong valve lineup
effects:
- loss of fuel suction
- flameout of gas turbines and SSGTGs

- EOCC-FUEL-XFER

1. [step: EOCC-FUEL-SVTK-01] Verify the service tank level at the fuel control console and locally. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FUEL-SVTK-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FUEL-SVTK-03] Shift fuel suction to the standby service tank after its bottom is tested for water. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-FUEL-SVTK-04] Start a fuel transfer to the low service tank through the purifier. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-FUEL-SVTK-05] Check the fuel system for leaks and for a wrong valve lineup if the level dropped without cause. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-FUEL-SVTK-06] Report the service tank level restored to the EOOW. requires: 05
