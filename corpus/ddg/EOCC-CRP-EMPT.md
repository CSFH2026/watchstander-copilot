1. [step: EOCC-CRP-EMPT-01] Report to the EOOW and the bridge that emergency pitch will be set and that the shaft will not answer bells. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-CRP-EMPT-02] Shift the changeover valves at the OD box to OFF. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-CRP-EMPT-03] Secure the HOPM. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-CRP-EMPT-04] Connect the emergency pitch hand pump hoses at the OD box low-pressure chamber cover. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-CRP-EMPT-05] Pump the hub to the ahead pitch the EOSS directs, and verify the pitch at the local pitch indicator. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-CRP-EMPT-06] Monitor the OD box temperature continuously while in emergency ahead; keep prairie air on, and apply cooling water to the OD box housing if needed. requires: 05. danger: the OD box overheats in emergency ahead; do not leave it unattended.
7. [step: EOCC-CRP-EMPT-07] Report the emergency pitch setting to the EOOW and the bridge. requires: 06
