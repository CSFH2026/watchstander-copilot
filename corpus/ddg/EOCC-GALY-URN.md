symptoms:
- coffee maker down
- coffee urn not heating
- coffee urn will not brew
- no hot water from the coffee urn
- brewing light does not come on
- dial thermometer stays below the brew zone
- coffee urn gauge glass leaking
causes:
- thermostat dial at OFF or HOLD
- urn not yet at brewing temperature after a shutoff
- water tank low with no auto refill
- tripped breaker or blown fuse at the galley power panel
- failed heating element or thermostat
- timer or spray arm fault
- heavy scale in the liner
effects:
- no hot coffee for the watch
- no hot water for tea
- scald hazard from a leaking urn or gauge glass
---
1. [step: EOCC-GALY-URN-01] Verify the casualty with the thermostat dial at BREW; check the dial thermometer, the pilot light, and the brewing light. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-GALY-URN-02] Report the casualty to the galley watch captain and the Food Service Officer, and report any electrical fault to the EOOW. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-GALY-URN-03] If the urn was shut off overnight, allow at least 55 minutes at BREW to reach brewing temperature before you call it failed. requires: 01
4. [step: EOCC-GALY-URN-04] Check the water level in the gauge glass, and fill the tank by hand unless the urn has auto refill. requires: 01
5. [step: EOCC-GALY-URN-05] Check the urn's breaker or fuse at the galley power panel; reset it once, and do not reset a breaker that trips again. requires: 02. danger: a breaker that trips again shows a fault in the element or wiring; resetting it again can start a fire or shock the operator.
6. [step: EOCC-GALY-URN-06] Set the thermostat to OFF, secure power at the panel, and tag out the urn; brew from the other urn or a backup coffee maker. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-GALY-URN-07] If the urn heats but brews slowly or the gauge glass is fouled, destain it at 170 to 180°F for 1 hour, scrub the liner and gauge glass, and rinse it three or four times. requires: 04
8. [step: EOCC-GALY-URN-08] Have an electrician test the heating element, thermostat, and timer under the tag out, and report the parts needed and the repair estimate for the CASREP. requires: 06. danger: never open the urn's control panel or touch the heating element while power is on; the tank water can be near boiling.
