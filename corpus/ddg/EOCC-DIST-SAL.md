symptoms:
- high salinity alarm on the RO unit
- product water conductivity high
- salinity indicator reads high
- chemical test shows salt in the product water
causes:
- RO membrane fouling or failure
- low feed pressure to the modules
- dirty salinity probe
- conductivity indicator malfunction
- dirty chemical test equipment
effects:
- contamination of potable water
- loss of fresh water output while the product is dumped
associated:
- EOCC-DIST-CAP

1. [step: EOCC-DIST-SAL-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-DIST-SAL-02] Dump the high-salinity product water to the bilge; do not send it to the potable water tanks. requires: 01. danger: product water with high salinity must not reach the potable water tanks.
3. [step: EOCC-DIST-SAL-03] Check the salinity probe tips for contamination, and test and troubleshoot the conductivity indicator. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-DIST-SAL-04] Clean the chemical test equipment and repeat the chemical test. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-DIST-SAL-05] Check the operation of the high pressure pump, and adjust the first and second stage pressures to their proper settings. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-DIST-SAL-06] Clean the membranes according to the manufacturer's instructions, or replace the module. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-DIST-SAL-07] Sample the potable water tank that received product before the alarm, and report the result to the EOOW. requires: 06
