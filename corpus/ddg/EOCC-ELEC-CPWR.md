1. [step: EOCC-ELEC-CPWR-01] Report the loss of power to the vital load to the EOOW and the DCC, and get the order to rig casualty power. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-ELEC-CPWR-02] Shut off the normal supply to the power panel that will receive casualty power. requires: 01. danger: all terminals on power panels are hot; never connect a cable to a panel whose normal supply is still on.
3. [step: EOCC-ELEC-CPWR-03] Lay out the portable cables from the racks, and connect them starting at the load and working back toward the supply. requires: 02. danger: always connect from the load back to the supply so that no one handles an energized cable.
4. [step: EOCC-ELEC-CPWR-04] Match the leads to the terminals by color and by feel, black to A, white to B, and red to C. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-ELEC-CPWR-05] Secure the cables to the overhead clear of the deck. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-ELEC-CPWR-06] Post DANGER-HIGH VOLTAGE signs at each connection and every 10 feet along the cable. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-ELEC-CPWR-07] Close the casualty power supply breaker at the live switchboard. requires: 06
8. [step: EOCC-ELEC-CPWR-08] Report to the EOOW and the DCC that the vital load has power, and return the wrenches to their racks. requires: 07
