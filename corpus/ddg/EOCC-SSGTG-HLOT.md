symptoms:
- high lube oil temperature on the SSGTG
- LO temperature alarm
- LUBO TEMP HIGH
- sump level dropping
- oil in the cooler seawater test cock
- lube oil pressure drops
causes:
- ruptured tube or leaking gasket in the LO cooler
- seawater cooling pump failure
- insufficient seawater through the LO cooler
- cooler bypass valve set wrong
effects:
- overheating of bearings
- loss of lube oil
- loss of the SSGTG while the cooler is repaired
associated:
- EOCC-SSGTG-LLOP
- EOCC-SW-LSUP

1. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HLOT-01] Verify high LO temperature on the SSGTG at the LOCOP and the EPCC. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HLOT-02] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HLOT-03] Check that the SSGTG seawater cooling pump runs and holds discharge pressure; if it has failed, verify emergency cooling from the ship's seawater service system. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HLOT-04] Check the LO cooler seawater bypass valve setting, and open the cooler test cock to look for oil in the seawater. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HLOT-05] Start the standby SSGTG and parallel it to the bus. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HLOT-06] Shift the electrical load to the standby SSGTG. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HLOT-07] Change to the standby cooler, or secure the affected SSGTG if the cooler leaks or the temperature keeps rising. requires: 06. danger: never before 06 while the affected SSGTG carries load the others cannot pick up.
8. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HLOT-08] Pressure test the LO cooler; plug the leaking tube, renew the gaskets, or replace the cooler. requires: 07
9. [step: EOCC-SSGTG-HLOT-09] Report the SSGTG ready for service to the EOOW after LO temperature is normal on a restart. requires: 08
