symptoms:
- HP air pressure low
- HP air flask pressure dropping
- HP air compressor will not hold pressure
- low start air pressure alarm
- gas turbine fails to turn over on HP start air
causes:
- HP air compressor failure
- leaks in HP air lines or flasks
- heavy use of start air
- defective or stuck-open start air valves
- clogged dehydrator
effects:
- no HP air start for the SSGTGs and main engines
- loss of HP backup to the LP air system
- reduced maneuverability
associated:
- EOCC-SSGTG-NSTR
- EOCC-AIR-LOSP

1. [step: EOCC-AIR-HPAC-01] Report the casualty to the EOOW, the bridge, and the Chief Engineer, and keep the bridge informed of HP air status. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-AIR-HPAC-02] Verify HP air pressure at the flasks and the HP air main. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-AIR-HPAC-03] If starts are needed, secure all but one HP air flask and start all available HP air compressors. requires: 02
4. [step: EOCC-AIR-HPAC-04] Check each HP air compressor for its design output. requires: 03
5. [step: EOCC-AIR-HPAC-05] Check the HP air system for leaks, and check the condition of the start air valves. requires: 04
6. [step: EOCC-AIR-HPAC-06] Check the HP air dehydrator for restriction. requires: 05
7. [step: EOCC-AIR-HPAC-07] Select bleed air for SSGTG starts at the EPCC while HP air pressure is low. requires: 06
