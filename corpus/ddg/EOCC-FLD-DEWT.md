1. [step: EOCC-FLD-DEWT-01] Report the flooding to the DCC and get the dewatering order from the stability diagram; loose water and water high in the ship come out first. requires: none
2. [step: EOCC-FLD-DEWT-02] Dewater an engine room or auxiliary space with its installed main drainage eductor; open the drain well suction and the discharge stop-check valves. requires: 01
3. [step: EOCC-FLD-DEWT-03] To rig a portable eductor, run a hose from the nearest fireplug, submerge the eductor fully, then charge it. requires: 01. danger: firemain pressure must be at least three times the static discharge head, or the eductor adds water to the space.
4. [step: EOCC-FLD-DEWT-04] To rig a submersible pump, lower it by its nylon handling line with a basket strainer, lead the discharge hose short and free of kinks, and have a qualified electrician energize it. requires: 01. danger: never use an electric pump where explosive vapors may be present, and never use it for gasoline or heavy oil.
5. [step: EOCC-FLD-DEWT-05] To rig a P-250, tighten the suction connections, submerge the strainer in clear water, and connect the exhaust hose before running it in a confined space. requires: 01. danger: stop the P-250 if no pressure shows within 45 seconds; its exhaust is toxic in a closed space.
6. [step: EOCC-FLD-DEWT-06] Keep the strainers clean and the suctions submerged, and report progress and any list to the DCC. requires: 01
